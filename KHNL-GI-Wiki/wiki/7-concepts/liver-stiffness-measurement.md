Liver stiffness measurement (LSM) is a non-invasive estimate of hepatic fibrosis and portal pressure using elastography — a core [[noninvasive-liver-disease-assessment|NILDA]] tool. It is the point-of-care backbone of the [[portal-hypertension|cACLD/CSPH]] framework, letting clinicians stage [[cirrhosis|compensated advanced chronic liver disease]] and stratify decompensation risk **without biopsy or [[hepatic-venous-pressure-gradient|HVPG]]**.

## Modalities

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Vibration-controlled transient elastography (VCTE / FibroScan)** | Most validated; all Baveno VII / AASLD kPa cut-offs are **TE-specific** |
| Point shear-wave elastography (pSWE / ARFI) | Ultrasound-integrated; cut-offs not interchangeable with TE |
| 2D shear-wave elastography (2D-SWE) | Real-time SWE map; cut-offs not yet validated to TE thresholds |
| Magnetic resonance elastography (MRE) | Highest accuracy; costly; separate thresholds |

## Clinical thresholds (TE)

The full **"rule of 5"** algorithm, the cACLD/CSPH rule-in/rule-out cut-offs, the Baveno VI criteria to avoid endoscopy, and spleen-stiffness cut-offs all live on **[[portal-hypertension]]** (one home). Key anchors [[baveno-vii-2022-portal-hypertension]]:

| LSM by TE | Meaning (Baveno VII statement, grade) |
|---|---|
| **<10 kPa** | Rules out cACLD absent other clinical/imaging signs; negligible (≤1%) 3-yr risk of decompensation and liver-related death (2.4 B.1, 2.5 A.1) |
| **7–10 kPa** + ongoing liver injury | Monitor case-by-case for progression to cACLD (2.10 C.2) |
| **10–15 kPa** | *Suggestive* of cACLD (2.4 B.1) |
| **>15 kPa** | *Highly suggestive* of cACLD (2.4 B.1) |
| **<15 kPa + platelets >150 ×10⁹/L** | Rules out [[portal-hypertension\|CSPH]] (Sn and NPV >90%) (2.15 B.2) |
| **≥25 kPa** | Rules in CSPH (Sp and PPV >90%) — **only** in virus-/alcohol-related or **non-obese (BMI <30) NASH** cACLD (2.16 B.1) |

- **Rule of 5 (10-15-20-25 kPa)** denotes progressively higher relative risk of decompensation and liver-related death, independent of aetiology (2.9 B.1).
- **Spleen stiffness (SSM) by TE** — **<21 kPa rules out**, **>50 kPa rules in** CSPH, validated only in **viral-hepatitis cACLD** (untreated HCV; untreated or treated HBV) (2.21 B.2).
- **Avoiding screening endoscopy is conditional on NSBB candidacy.** Baveno VII does not simply exempt LSM <20 kPa + platelets >150 ×10⁹/L: it directs patients who **cannot start an NSBB** (contraindication/intolerance) to endoscopy if **LSM ≥20 kPa *or* platelets ≤150 ×10⁹/L** — either alone triggers it (2.19 A.1) — with **SSM ≤40 kPa** as the escape that still avoids endoscopy (2.22 C.2). Full pathway on [[portal-hypertension]].

## Monitoring & confounders

- In cACLD, LSM **could be repeated every 12 months** to monitor change (2.12 B.2); a clinically significant *decrease* — associated with substantially reduced decompensation and liver-related death — = **≥20% drop with LSM <20 kPa, or any drop to <10 kPa** (2.13 C.2).
- TE gives **false positives**: confirm an index LSM ≥10 kPa **fasting**, or corroborate with a serum fibrosis marker — **FIB-4 ≥2.67, ELF ≥9.8, FibroTest ≥0.58** (alcohol-related/viral) or **≥0.48** (NAFLD) (2.11 B.2).
- **False elevation** with: recent food intake, active hepatitis / ALT >3× ULN, cholestasis/biliary obstruction, hepatic congestion, [[obesity]] (unreliable — use XL probe), and infiltrative disease. Cut-offs are less reliable in [[nafld-masld|NAFLD/MASLD]] and PSC with dominant strictures.
- LSM cut-offs **cannot** be applied to exclude cirrhosis in [[porto-sinusoidal-vascular-disorder|PSVD]] and cannot substitute for endoscopy in PSVD.
