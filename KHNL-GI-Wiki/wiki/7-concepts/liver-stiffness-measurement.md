Liver stiffness measurement (LSM) is a non-invasive estimate of hepatic fibrosis and portal pressure using elastography — a core [[noninvasive-liver-disease-assessment|NILDA]] tool. It is the point-of-care backbone of the [[portal-hypertension|cACLD/CSPH]] framework, letting clinicians stage [[cirrhosis|compensated advanced chronic liver disease]] and stratify decompensation risk **without [[liver-biopsy|biopsy]] or [[hepatic-venous-pressure-gradient|HVPG]]**.

## Modalities

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Vibration-controlled transient elastography (VCTE / FibroScan)** | Most validated. **In Baveno documents, "LSM" means VCTE unless another modality is named**, and every kPa cut-off below is VCTE-specific |
| Point shear-wave elastography (pSWE / ARFI) | Ultrasound-integrated; cut-offs not interchangeable with VCTE. Relative *changes* likely carry similar meaning, but evidence is **insufficient to recommend them as monitoring tools** ([[baveno-viii-2026-portal-hypertension]] 2.10) |
| 2D shear-wave elastography (2D-SWE) | Real-time SWE map; same caveat as pSWE (2.10) |
| Magnetic resonance elastography (MRE) | Highest accuracy; costly; separate thresholds. **≥20% relative change** is clinically meaningful in MASLD-related cACLD (2.11) |

## Performing VCTE — technique and validity criteria

[[aga-2017-elastography-liver-fibrosis]]:

- **Probe placement:** intercostal skin, **9th–11th intercostal space**; sampled region **25–65 mm below the skin surface (M-probe)** or **35–75 mm (XL-probe)**.
- **Minimum 10 valid measurements** → composite score.
- **A result is adequate quality only if both hold:** **≥10 validated measurements** *and* **IQR/median ratio of liver stiffness ≤30%**. Treat a reading failing either as unreliable.
- Reportable range **2.5–75 kPa**.
- **Technical limits on obtaining a reading:** narrow intercostal space; obesity (XL probe).

## Clinical thresholds (VCTE)

The full **"rule of 5"** algorithm, the three Baveno VIII CSPH rule-in criteria, the endoscopy-sparing rule, and all spleen-stiffness cut-offs live on **[[portal-hypertension]]** (one home). Key anchors [[baveno-viii-2026-portal-hypertension]]:

| LSM by VCTE | Meaning (Baveno VIII statement) |
|---|---|
| **<10 kPa** | Rules out cACLD absent other clinical/imaging signs; negligible (≤1%) 3-yr risk of decompensation and liver-related death (1.3, 1.5). **Exception:** with clinical or imaging signs of portal hypertension, LSM <10 kPa points to **vascular liver disease** rather than reassuring (1.6, new) |
| **7–10 kPa** + ongoing liver injury | Monitor case-by-case for progression to cACLD (Baveno VII 2.10) |
| **10–15 kPa** | *Suggestive* of cACLD (1.3) |
| **>15 kPa** | *Highly suggestive* of cACLD (1.3) |
| **≤15 kPa + platelets ≥150 ×10⁹/L** | Rules out [[portal-hypertension\|CSPH]] (NPV >90%) (1.15 — **upgraded to LoE 1, strong** from Baveno VII's B.2) |
| **≥25 kPa** | **Diagnostic of** CSPH — **only** in virus-/alcohol-related or **non-obese (BMI <30 kg/m²) MASLD**-related cACLD (1.16b) |
| **<10 kPa**, or **<15 kPa with SSM <25 kPa**, in a **recompensated** patient | May rule out CSPH → the non-invasive route to stopping [[nonselective-beta-blockers\|carvedilol/cNSBB]] (7.19a–b). **LSM >25 kPa** rules CSPH back in (7.19c) |

- **Rule of 5 (10-15-20-25 kPa)** denotes progressively higher relative risk of decompensation and liver-related death, independent of aetiology (1.8).
- **Spleen stiffness (SSM)** now has three separate, non-interchangeable cut-offs — **>55 kPa at 100 Hz** rules CSPH in; **<40 kPa** spares screening endoscopy; **<25 kPa with LSM <15 kPa** rules CSPH out after recompensation. ⚠ **Baveno VII's SSM <21 / >50 kPa pair (viral cACLD only) is superseded** — do not mix the two sets. Table on [[portal-hypertension]].
- **Avoiding screening endoscopy is conditional on NSBB candidacy.** Baveno VIII 1.19 sends patients who **cannot start an NSBB** to endoscopy **unless LSM <20 kPa *and* platelets ≥150 ×10⁹/L, or SSM <40 kPa**. Full pathway on [[portal-hypertension]].

### Etiology-specific VCTE cutoffs — AGA 2017

A parallel, **etiology-specific** ladder aimed at *histologic cirrhosis*, not at CSPH. Every cutoff was deliberately chosen to **minimize false negatives** — the panel judged missing cirrhosis worse than overcalling it — so specificity is low and false-positive rates are high, particularly in low-prevalence settings. [[aga-2017-elastography-liver-fibrosis]]

| Clinical question | VCTE cutoff | Pooled sens / spec | Strength · quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cirrhosis in [[hepatitis-c\|chronic hepatitis C]] | **12.5 kPa** | 0.86 / 0.91 | Conditional · low |
| Cirrhosis in [[chronic-hepatitis-b\|chronic hepatitis B]] | **11.0 kPa** | 0.81 / 0.83 | Conditional · low |
| Cirrhosis in chronic alcoholic liver disease | **12.5 kPa** | 0.95 / 0.71 | Conditional · low |
| Rule out advanced fibrosis (F3–F4) **post-SVR**, noncirrhotic HCV | **9.5 kPa** | 0.78 / 0.86 | Conditional · very low |
| Rule out **high-risk esophageal varices** in suspected compensated cirrhosis | **19.5 kPa** | 0.89 / 0.56 | Conditional · low |
| Rule out **CSPH** before elective **nonhepatic surgery** | **17.0 kPa** | 0.83 / 0.52 | Conditional · low |
| Cirrhosis in NAFLD | **no cutoff given** | — | *No recommendation — knowledge gap* |

- **Prefer VCTE over the nonproprietary serum indices** to detect cirrhosis: **strong, moderate-quality** in chronic hepatitis C (VCTE sens 0.89/spec 0.91 vs APRI 0.77/0.78 and FIB-4 0.87/0.91); **conditional, low-quality** in chronic hepatitis B (VCTE 0.86/0.85 vs APRI 0.66/0.74 and FIB-4 0.87/0.65). Serum-index cut-points themselves live on [[noninvasive-liver-disease-assessment]].
- **Qualifiers that travel with these numbers:**
  - The 12.5 kPa alcohol cutoff was derived in **chronic** alcoholic liver disease and must **not** be applied to acute alcoholic hepatitis.
  - The 19.5 kPa varices rule does **not** apply to decompensated cirrhosis, or to compensated cirrhosis with known varices or portal hypertension — those patients go to endoscopy regardless of the LSM.
  - A cirrhosis cutoff alone should **not** drive an invasive downstream decision such as screening endoscopy; that has its own threshold (19.5 kPa).
  - In hepatitis C, **repeat elastography after viral cure frequently falls**, so a single pre-treatment value overcalls cirrhosis over time.
- **MRE vs VCTE:** in chronic hepatitis C, **VCTE is preferred** (conditional, very low) — MRE added little accuracy for true cirrhosis but had poorer specificity (0.81 vs 0.91) and higher false positives, at higher cost and without point-of-care availability. In NAFLD the direction reverses for high-risk patients only — see [[nafld-masld]].
- ⚠ **These are superseded where they overlap the current ladders above.** The AASLD cross-etiology cut-points (rule-out <8, rule-in ≥12, cirrhosis ≥20 kPa) on [[noninvasive-liver-disease-assessment]] and the Baveno VIII cACLD/CSPH ladder govern current practice; AGA 2017 remains the reference for **etiology-specific** thresholds and for the pre-operative CSPH question.

## Monitoring & confounders

- **Measure fasting, always** — for cACLD *and* for CSPH ([[baveno-viii-2026-portal-hypertension]] 1.4). Baveno VII required fasting only on the confirmatory repeat.
- **Repeat NITs for CSPH every 12 months** in cACLD (1.9, strengthened from "could be repeated"). Changes carry prognostic information, but **the most recent value guides clinical decision-making** (1.10, new).
- **Confirming an index LSM ≥10 kPa:** repeat it, or complement it with **another validated NIT of advanced fibrosis** (1.4). Baveno VIII names no serum cut-offs; the specific ones — **FIB-4 ≥2.67, ELF ≥9.8, FibroTest ≥0.58** (alcohol/viral) or **≥0.48** (NAFLD) — are **Baveno VII's** (2.11) and are cited as such on [[noninvasive-liver-disease-assessment]].
- **How much change matters** — Baveno VIII Panel 2 quantifies this for the first time, and frames NIT change as a change in the **risk of liver-related events**, not as a fibrosis-stage change (2.8):

| Test | Change that is clinically meaningful | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| **LSM (VCTE)** | **Confirmed ≥30% relative change** | [[alcohol-associated-liver-disease\|Alcohol-related]] cACLD (2.3); MASLD-related cACLD (2.9), **especially when it crosses 15 kPa** |
| **MRE** | **≥20% relative change** | MASLD-related cACLD (2.11) |
| **ELF** | **0.5-unit absolute change** | MASLD (2.12) |
| **pSWE / 2D-SWE** | Probably similar to VCTE, but **evidence insufficient to recommend** | (2.10) |

- **What produces a ≥30% LSM reduction:** **≥10% weight reduction** in MASLD-related cACLD (2.13). In MASLD-related cACLD **without CSPH**, a confirmed ≥30% relative LSM reduction may indicate **≥1 stage fibrosis regression** (2.6) — and combining it with a **≥0.5-unit absolute ELF reduction** assesses that better than LSM alone (2.7).
- The Baveno VII definition of a clinically significant *decrease* — **≥20% drop with LSM <20 kPa, or any drop to <10 kPa** (2.13) — is not restated in Baveno VIII; treat the ≥30% relative-change rule as the current one.
- **False elevation / unreliable reading** with: recent food intake — AGA 2017 puts the window at **food within 2–3 hours** — active or **acute hepatitis** / ALT >3× ULN, **alcohol abuse**, cholestasis/biliary obstruction (AGA 2017: **extrahepatic** cholestasis), hepatic congestion / **congestive heart failure**, [[obesity]] (unreliable — use XL probe), and infiltrative disease. Cut-offs are less reliable in MASLD and [[primary-sclerosing-cholangitis|PSC]] with dominant strictures. [[aga-2017-elastography-liver-fibrosis]]
- **LSM-based criteria cannot be applied in [[porto-sinusoidal-vascular-disorder|PSVD/NCPF]]** ([[baveno-viii-2026-portal-hypertension]] 8.48) — but **spleen** stiffness can: SSM <40 kPa with bilirubin <1 mg/dL spares endoscopy there (8.47).
- **Paediatrics:** LSM is a valuable complementary tool for identifying children at risk of portal hypertension and monitoring progression, but **management cannot rely on LSM and/or SSM alone** — the paediatric [[variceal-upper-gi-bleeding|varices]] cut-offs are not yet validated (1.25–1.26).
