**Confocal laser endomicroscopy (CLE)** = real-time, high-magnification in vivo imaging of the mucosa or duct wall — an "optical biopsy," read at the time of endoscopy. Blue laser light illuminates the tissue after **intravenous fluorescein** ([[acg-2022-barretts|American College of Gastroenterology (ACG) 2022]]).

## Platforms

| Type | Delivery |
|---|---|
| **Probe-based CLE (pCLE)** | Thin confocal laser probe passed down the working channel; in the biliary tree it is introduced into the bile duct through the duodenoscope during [[ercp\|endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP)]] ([[acg-2023-biliary-strictures\|ACG 2023]], [[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures\|American Society for Gastrointestinal Endoscopy (ASGE) 2023]]) |
| **Endoscope-based CLE (eCLE)** | Confocal imaging built into the endoscope |
| **Needle-based CLE (nCLE)** | Probe placed directly into a pancreatic cyst through a **19-gauge fine-needle aspiration (FNA) needle**; images the cyst epithelium in vivo ([[acg-2018-pancreatic-cysts\|ACG 2018]]) |

- Of the two esophageal systems developed, **only the probe-based system is still commercially available** ([[acg-2022-barretts]]).

## Positions by Indication

> [!pearl] No society document recommends CLE for routine use anywhere in the GI tract
> Every ingested guideline either makes no recommendation, suggests against routine use, or grants only an **adjunctive role at expert centers** — none makes it a standard part of any examination ([[asge-2019-barretts-screening-surveillance]], [[acg-2022-barretts]], [[acg-2023-biliary-strictures]]).

| Indication | Position as the document states it | Strength / grade |
|---|---|---|
| Indeterminate [[biliary-stricture\|biliary stricture]] | No recommendation made. ASGE: pCLE is **difficult to master and expensive**, so widespread adoption is likely limited in the near future. ACG: pCLE performance **does not meet the threshold needed to drive oncological decision making** at the large majority of institutions; it **remains an adjunct at select referral centers** | Ungraded panel comment ([[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures\|ASGE 2023]], [[acg-2023-biliary-strictures\|ACG 2023]]) |
| Dominant stricture in [[primary-sclerosing-cholangitis\|primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC)]] | **Not studied specifically** in PSC dominant strictures (performance figures come from indeterminate strictures in general) | No recommendation ([[acg-2015-psc\|ACG 2015]]) |
| [[barretts-esophagus\|Barrett's esophagus (BE)]] surveillance | ASGE: **suggest against routine use** of CLE compared with white-light endoscopy plus Seattle-protocol biopsy sampling (recommendation 4). ACG: in centers with a **high prevalence of neoplasia or dysplasia** CLE **may be helpful** in targeting biopsies and guiding therapy, though its value above high-definition white-light endoscopy (HD-WLE) and electronic chromoendoscopy **is unclear**. American Gastroenterological Association (AGA): endomicroscopy **may be used as an adjunctive imaging technique** to identify dysplasia — **not required** for a high-quality exam, and the data to date **do not support routine use** | Conditional, low quality ([[asge-2019-barretts-screening-surveillance\|ASGE 2019]]); ungraded narrative ([[acg-2022-barretts\|ACG 2022]]); Best Practice Advice (BPA) #5 ([[aga-2022-barretts-new-technology\|AGA 2022]]) |
| Low-grade dysplasia in BE | CLE (with autofluorescence, optical frequency domain imaging, spectroscopy, high-resolution microendoscopy) **"cannot be recommended in the routine clinical management of these patients"** | Ungraded ([[aga-2016-low-grade-dysplasia-barretts\|AGA 2016]]) |
| [[pancreatic-cysts\|Pancreatic cysts]] | nCLE is "promising," but only a small number of cases were compared with gold-standard histopathology; ACG **awaits ongoing studies** before defining its potential role — no recommendation either way | No recommendation ([[acg-2018-pancreatic-cysts\|ACG 2018]]) |
| Dysplasia surveillance in [[inflammatory-bowel-disease\|inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)]] | **Deliberately excluded** from the focused questions: CLE cannot practically be used for primary examination of the entire colonic surface area, as surveillance requires. Its potential role would be **characterization of lesions already identified** | Out of scope, no recommendation ([[asge-2015-scenic-ibd-dysplasia\|SCENIC 2015]]) |
| Indeterminate pancreatic duct stricture ([[chronic-pancreatitis\|chronic pancreatitis]], post-surgical anastomosis) | **"Emerging technology that may prove useful"**; brushings for cytology are what is actually done at endotherapy | Narrative, ungraded ([[asge-2015-benign-pancreatic-disease\|ASGE 2015]]) |
| Food-trigger elimination diets in [[irritable-bowel-syndrome\|irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)]] | Elimination diets based on CLE after food challenges: **few data support them** | Ungraded ([[aga-2022-diet-ibs\|AGA 2022]]) |

> [!pitfall] CLE characterizes a lesion — it does not find one
> The imaged field is microscopic, so CLE cannot survey an organ. That is exactly why the SCENIC panel left it out of IBD surveillance, and why in BE it is positioned as an adjunct to Seattle-protocol sampling rather than an alternative to it ([[asge-2015-scenic-ibd-dysplasia]], [[aga-2022-barretts-new-technology]]).

## Diagnostic Performance

| Setting | Measure | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indeterminate biliary strictures — pCLE, meta-analysis | Pooled sensitivity | 90% (95% confidence interval [CI] 86–94) | [[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures\|ASGE 2023]] |
| Indeterminate biliary strictures — pCLE, meta-analysis | Pooled specificity | 72% (95% CI 65–79) | [[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures\|ASGE 2023]] |
| Indeterminate biliary strictures — systematic review | Negative predictive value (NPV) for malignancy | 94% — named as pCLE's **best application** | [[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures\|ASGE 2023]] |
| Indeterminate strictures in general (not PSC-specific) | Sensitivity / specificity | 98% / 67% | [[acg-2015-psc\|ACG 2015]] |
| Cholangiographic impression + pCLE + histopathology combined, prospective multicenter study | Sensitivity and accuracy | in the range of 90% | [[acg-2023-biliary-strictures\|ACG 2023]] |
| BE — dysplasia/esophageal adenocarcinoma (EAC) vs histopathology; 7 studies, 473 patients (pCLE + eCLE pooled) | Pooled **per-patient** sensitivity / specificity | 89% (95% CI 0.82–0.94) / 83% (95% CI 0.78–0.86) | [[acg-2022-barretts\|ACG 2022]] |
| BE — 14 studies, 789 patients, 4047 lesions | Pooled **per-lesion** sensitivity / specificity | 77% (95% CI 0.73–0.81) / 89% (95% CI 0.87–0.90) | [[aga-2022-barretts-new-technology\|AGA 2022]] |
| BE — CLE vs narrow-band imaging, within-patient; 5 studies, 251 patients | Additional per-lesion neoplasia detection by CLE | 19.3% (95% CI 0.05–0.33); per-patient sensitivity and specificity **comparable** | [[aga-2022-barretts-new-technology\|AGA 2022]] |
| Pancreatic cysts — nCLE, serous cystadenoma (SCA) vs other cyst types | Sensitivity / specificity | 59%–69% / 100% | [[acg-2018-pancreatic-cysts\|ACG 2018]] |
| Pancreatic cysts — nCLE, intraductal papillary mucinous neoplasm (IPMN) / mucinous cystic neoplasm (MCN) vs other cyst types; prospective single center | Sensitivity | 80%, with excellent specificity | [[acg-2018-pancreatic-cysts\|ACG 2018]] |

- Biliary: **specificity is the weak axis** — the high NPV means pCLE is better at ruling malignancy *out* than ruling it in ([[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures]]).
- BE: heterogeneity is high in the pooled estimates (I² 31.6% for sensitivity, 90.1% for specificity) ([[acg-2022-barretts]]).

## Interpretation — Miami and Paris Criteria (Biliary)

Read the two systems together: Miami names malignancy, Paris names the benign inflammatory mimics ([[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures]]).

| System | Reads for | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Miami** (endoscopist consensus) | **Malignant** stricture | **Thick dark bands of the collagen fibrils**; **thickened white bands within the vessels** |
| **Paris** (subsequent refinement) | **Benign inflammatory** stricture | **Vascular congestion**; **dark granular patterns with scales**; **increased interglandular space**; **thickened reticular structures** |

- Miami's stated limitation is **low interobserver agreement** — Paris was developed to further define the benign inflammatory criteria.
- No ingested source supplies example pCLE images of the Miami or Paris patterns.

## Limitations

- Considerable **capital cost**, the need for intravenous fluorescein, **training in image interpretation**, and added procedure time ([[acg-2022-barretts]]).
- BE performance data come largely from centers with a high prevalence of dysplasia/neoplasia — **applicability to a general surveillance population is unknown** ([[acg-2022-barretts]]).
- It does not obviate tissue: multimodality sampling ([[brush-cytology|brush cytology]], fluoroscopy-directed biopsy, [[fish|fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH)]]) still governs the biliary diagnosis — see [[biliary-stricture]]. The same ceiling applies to visual interpretation at [[cholangioscopy]] ([[acg-2023-biliary-strictures]]).
- ACG notes that with added molecular markers and [[artificial-intelligence-endoscopy|artificial intelligence]], pCLE-based optical biopsy *may* eventually obviate histopathology — as imaging alone already does for [[hepatocellular-carcinoma|hepatocellular carcinoma]] — but that is not current practice ([[acg-2023-biliary-strictures]]).
