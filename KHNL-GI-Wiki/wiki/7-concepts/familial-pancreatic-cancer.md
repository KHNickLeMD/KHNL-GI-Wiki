## Definition

- **FPC kindred** = a family containing **at least a pair of first-degree relatives** with [[pancreatic-cancer|pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma]], **without** an association with a known hereditary cancer syndrome ([[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]]).
  - Diagnosis of exclusion — if a recognized syndrome accounts for the cluster ([[brca-pathogenic-variants|BRCA1/2]], PALB2, [[fammm-syndrome|FAMMM]], [[peutz-jeghers-syndrome|Peutz-Jeghers]], [[lynch-syndrome|Lynch]], ATM, [[hereditary-pancreatitis|hereditary pancreatitis]]), it is not FPC.
- **Inheritance:** modeling studies suggest autosomal-dominant inheritance of a rare allele as the likely etiology; no causative gene is identified (that is what distinguishes FPC from the named syndromes).

## Surveillance

*Full modality, interval, yield, and harms data: see [[pancreatic-cancer-screening]].*

- **Who:** **all first-degree relatives** of affected family members ([[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]], Rec 6d).
- **When to start:** age **50**, or **10 years before** the youngest affected relative — whichever is earlier.
- **Interval:** annual (conditional, very low quality).
- **Modality:** [[endoscopic-ultrasound|EUS]], [[mri-mrcp|MRI]], or EUS alternating with MRI, by patient preference and local expertise (conditional, very low quality).
- FPC start age (50) is later than [[fammm-syndrome|FAMMM]] (40) and [[peutz-jeghers-syndrome|Peutz-Jeghers]] (35) — those carry ≥10 relative risk and start 2 SD below the mean age of diagnosis, whereas FPC starts ~1 SD below.

> **Gap — FPC-specific risk magnitude not sourced.** [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]] quantifies lifetime risk for [[brca-pathogenic-variants|BRCA1/2]] but not for FPC kindreds (e.g. risk by number of affected first-degree relatives). An FPC-specific source would be needed.
