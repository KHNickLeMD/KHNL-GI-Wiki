Familial pancreatic cancer (FPC) kindreds are defined as families containing at least a pair of first-degree relatives with [[pancreatic-cancer|pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma]] without an association with a known hereditary cancer syndrome. Modeling studies suggest autosomal-dominant inheritance of a rare allele as the likely etiology. Per [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening|ASGE 2022]], [[pancreatic-cancer-screening|pancreatic cancer surveillance]] is recommended for all first-degree relatives of affected family members, starting at age 50 or 10 years before the youngest affected relative.
