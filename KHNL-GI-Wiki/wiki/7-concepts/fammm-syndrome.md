Familial atypical multiple mole melanoma (FAMMM) syndrome is a hereditary cancer syndrome (classically associated with germline CDKN2A pathogenic variants) characterized by multiple atypical nevi, melanoma, and markedly increased risk of [[pancreatic-cancer|pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma]]. Because of its high relative risk, it is considered a very-high-risk condition for which [[endoscopic-ultrasound|EUS]] may be the preferred initial screening modality. Per [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening|ASGE 2022]], [[pancreatic-cancer-screening|pancreatic cancer surveillance]] should start at age 40 or 10 years before the youngest affected relative.
