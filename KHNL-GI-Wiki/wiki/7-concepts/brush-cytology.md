**Brush cytology** is the acquisition of exfoliated epithelial cells from a biliary (or pancreatic) stricture by passing a cytology brush over a guidewire during [[ercp|ERCP]]. It is the baseline first-line intraductal tissue-acquisition method for [[biliary-stricture|biliary strictures]] of undetermined etiology.

## Test Characteristics

Per [[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures]]:

| Strategy | Sensitivity for malignancy | Miss rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Brush cytology alone** | **.40** (95% CI .37–.43) | **58%** (95% CI 46–71) |
| Fluoroscopic-guided biopsy alone | **.52** (95% CI .49–.56); *P* = .006 vs brushing | **41%** (95% CI 31–52) |
| **Brushing + fluoroscopic-guided biopsy** | **.66** (95% CI .63–.69); *P* < .001 vs brushing | — |

- **Incremental yield** of adding intraductal biopsy to brushing: **20%** (95% CI 9–31).
- Specimen adequacy is higher for brush cytology — but ASGE attributes this to the **technical difficulty and failure of fluoroscopic-guided biopsy**, not to specimen quality.
- No difference in **adverse events** overall (OR .53; 95% CI .14–2.05) — though the 2 severe events (prolonged bleeding; perforation requiring surgical choledochotomy) were both in the biopsy arm.
- **No sensitivity difference** for proximal vs distal strictures, or biliary vs pancreatic masses.
- Adjuncts that improve yield on brushings include [[fish|FISH (fluorescence in situ hybridization)]] and next-generation sequencing. Note FISH's performance is **attenuated in [[primary-sclerosing-cholangitis|PSC]]** ([[acg-2023-biliary-strictures]]) — see [[fish]].

## Brush Cytology Alone Is Not Sufficient

Because sensitivity is ~0.40, neither guideline accepts brushing as a stand-alone strategy:

- **[[acg-2023-biliary-strictures]] Rec 3 (Strong recommendation, low quality of evidence):** for a suspected malignant perihilar stricture, use **multimodality intraductal sampling** — **at least 2 diagnostic sampling modalities** at the index ERCP — not brush cytology alone. The same approach applies to extrahepatic strictures without an associated mass.
- **[[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures]] Rec 1 (Conditional, very low quality):** add fluoroscopic-guided biopsy sampling to brush cytology rather than brush cytology alone — **where to do it is part of the recommendation:** because intraductal biopsy is more technically challenging, more time-consuming, and produced the more severe adverse events, it should be performed **at tertiary care centers or where endoscopic expertise exists**.
- Fluoroscopy-directed biopsy, [[cholangioscopy|cholangioscopy]]-directed biopsy, and FISH each add diagnostic value over brushing alone; choose by stricture location — see [[biliary-stricture]].
