**Fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH)** is a cytogenetic adjunct applied to [[brush-cytology|biliary brush cytology]] specimens during [[ercp|ERCP]], used to raise the diagnostic yield of cytology in [[biliary-stricture|biliary strictures]] of undetermined etiology.

## Principle

- Fluorescently labeled DNA probes assess brushed cells for **chromosomal abnormalities**, which are associated with malignancy ([[acg-2023-biliary-strictures]]).
- **Polysomy** (gain of multiple chromosomes) is the abnormality that carries the association with carcinoma.
- Applied to the same specimen as routine cytology — no additional pass or device is required beyond the standard brushing.

## Test Performance

Per [[acg-2023-biliary-strictures]] (study of ~500 consecutive patients undergoing ERCP with brush cytology for pancreaticobiliary strictures):

| Sampling | Sensitivity for malignancy |
|---|---|
| Brush cytology alone | 20.1% |
| Brush cytology + FISH | 42.9% |

- Multiple additional studies and a meta-analysis confirm FISH **adds substantial diagnostic value over brush cytology alone**.
- For comparison, the pooled sensitivity of brush cytology alone across the [[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures|ASGE 2023]] meta-analysis is ~0.40 — see [[brush-cytology]] for the full modality comparison.

## Limitations — what FISH cannot decide

Two caveats are decision-critical ([[acg-2023-biliary-strictures]]):

1. **Performance is attenuated in [[primary-sclerosing-cholangitis|PSC]]** — presumably because of the substantial inflammatory component. Do not carry the non-PSC sensitivity figures into a PSC stricture.
2. **An abnormal FISH alone does not establish a treatment-level diagnosis.** Some surgeons use FISH as a *contributing* factor in the decision to resect, but it is **highly uncommon for oncologists to offer chemotherapy on the basis of an abnormal FISH analysis alone.**

[[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures|ASGE 2023]] makes no formal recommendation on FISH; it names PSC as a research priority precisely because the diagnostic algorithm may differ there, FISH having a higher role in that population.

## Role in Multimodality Sampling

- Fluoroscopy-directed biopsies, [[cholangioscopy|cholangioscopy]]-directed biopsies, and FISH **each** add diagnostic value to brush cytology alone.
- On that basis ACG 2023 recommends **at least 2 diagnostic sampling modalities** at the time of ERCP-based biliary stricture evaluation (**Rec 3 — Strong recommendation, low quality of evidence**, for suspected malignant perihilar stricture; the same multimodality approach applies to extrahepatic strictures without an associated mass).
- FISH is therefore an adjunct that helps satisfy the multimodality requirement — not a replacement for tissue.
