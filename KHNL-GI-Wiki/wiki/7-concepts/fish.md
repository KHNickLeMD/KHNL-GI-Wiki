**Fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH)** is a cytogenetic adjunct applied to [[brush-cytology|biliary brush cytology]] specimens that detects chromosomal aneuploidy (polysomy) as a marker of malignancy in indeterminate [[biliary-stricture|biliary strictures]]. It improves the sensitivity of cytology, and per `[[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures]]` plays a particularly important role in [[primary-sclerosing-cholangitis|primary sclerosing cholangitis]], where conventional cytology performs poorly.
