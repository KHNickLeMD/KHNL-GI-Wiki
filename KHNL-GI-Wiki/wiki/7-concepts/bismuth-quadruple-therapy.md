# Bismuth Quadruple Therapy (BQT)

---

## Components

| Drug | Dose | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| PPI (e.g. omeprazole 20mg) | Standard dose | Twice daily |
| Bismuth subsalicylate | 525mg | Four times daily |
| Metronidazole | 250mg | Four times daily |
| Tetracycline | 500mg | Four times daily |

**Duration:** 10–14 days (14 days preferred per [[acg-2017-hp-guidelines]])

**Commercial formulation:** Pylera (bismuth subcitrate potassium 140mg + metronidazole 125mg + tetracycline 125mg per capsule; 3 capsules qid + PPI bid)

---

## Indications

- **Preferred first-line** H. pylori eradication in the US (high clarithromycin resistance)
- Any region with clarithromycin resistance >15%
- Penicillin allergy (replaces amoxicillin-based regimens)
- Second-line after failed clarithromycin-based triple therapy

---

## Efficacy

- Eradication rates: ~80–90% (intention-to-treat); up to 93% per-protocol
- Superior to clarithromycin triple therapy in regions with high resistance
- Less affected by metronidazole resistance than metronidazole-containing dual-drug regimens (high-dose, long-duration partially overcomes resistance)

---

## Mechanism of Bismuth

- Bismuth salts disrupt bacterial cell membrane integrity
- Inhibit urease, phospholipases, and adherence to gastric epithelium
- No known resistance mechanisms — bismuth activity is maintained regardless of antibiotic resistance patterns

---

## Adverse Effects

- **Bismuth:** black stools (harmless), black tongue, constipation
- **Metronidazole:** metallic taste, nausea, peripheral neuropathy (prolonged use), disulfiram-like reaction with alcohol
- **Tetracycline:** photosensitivity, esophageal irritation (take with full glass of water, remain upright), contraindicated in pregnancy
- **PPI:** generally well tolerated; headache, diarrhea, hypomagnesemia (long-term)

---

## Contraindications / Cautions

- Tetracycline: **avoid in pregnancy** (fetal bone/teeth development) and children <8 years
- Metronidazole: avoid alcohol during treatment and 48h after (disulfiram reaction)
- Bismuth: caution in renal impairment (bismuth accumulation)
