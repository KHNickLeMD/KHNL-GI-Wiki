# Needed Sources — Download List

Papers, guidelines, tables, and figures that would close a specific gap on a wiki page but are not in `raw/`. Add a file to `raw/` (in the right society subfolder), ingest it, and delete its row. Rows are grouped by area; the last column names the page(s) waiting on it. Pages never carry this list themselves — they state the gap in one reader-facing sentence and nothing more.
