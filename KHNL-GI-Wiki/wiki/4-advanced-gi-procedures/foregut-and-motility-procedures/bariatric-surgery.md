# Bariatric Surgery

Surgical weight-loss procedures (e.g. laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy, Roux-en-Y gastric bypass, adjustable gastric banding, duodenal switch) for [[obesity]]. Considered for patients who fail or are not candidates for less invasive options; per [[aga-2021-intragastric-balloons|AGA 2021]], it is one option for weight-loss maintenance after [[intragastric-balloon]] removal, and IGB may serve as a bridge to surgery.
