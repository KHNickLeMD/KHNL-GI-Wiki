Laparoscopic Heller myotomy (LHM) is the surgical anterior myotomy of the lower esophageal sphincter for [[achalasia]], typically paired with a partial fundoplication (Dor or Toupet) to limit postoperative reflux. It is the principal surgical alternative to [[poem|peroral endoscopic myotomy (POEM)]] and to pneumatic dilation. Unlike POEM, the added fundoplication gives LHM a built-in anti-reflux component, so post-procedure [[gerd|GERD]] is generally lower than after POEM.
