Laparoscopic Heller myotomy (LHM) is the surgical anterior myotomy of the lower esophageal sphincter for [[achalasia]], typically paired with a [[antireflux-surgery|partial fundoplication]] (Dor or Toupet) to limit postoperative reflux. It is the principal surgical alternative to [[poem|peroral endoscopic myotomy (POEM)]] and to pneumatic dilation.

- **LHM vs POEM:** either approach is acceptable; selection is individualized. POEM is especially advantageous for **type III achalasia / spastic disorders** given a tailorable, longer myotomy ([[sages-2024-poem]]). Achalasia subtypes are defined on [[chicago-classification-v4]].
- **Reflux:** post-POEM [[gerd|GERD]]/reflux is common, whereas **LHM is typically combined with a partial fundoplication** ([[sages-2024-poem]]) — the built-in antireflux component is the principal trade-off distinguishing the two.
