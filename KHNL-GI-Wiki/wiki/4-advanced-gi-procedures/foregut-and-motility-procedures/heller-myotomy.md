## Overview

Laparoscopic Heller myotomy (LHM) is the surgical myotomy of the lower esophageal sphincter (LES) for [[achalasia]], paired with a [[antireflux-surgery|partial fundoplication]] (Dor or Toupet). One of the 3 definitive therapies alongside [[pneumatic-dilation|pneumatic dilation (PD)]] and [[poem|peroral endoscopic myotomy (POEM)]]; all are palliative, not curative ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).

- **Preoperative workup = the achalasia workup** ([[achalasia]]): [[upper-endoscopy|upper endoscopy]] to exclude pseudoachalasia, [[high-resolution-manometry|high-resolution manometry (HRM)]] for subtype, timed barium esophagram (TBE). Without manometry, patients may get the wrong operation (e.g., Nissen fundoplication for presumed reflux) ([[sages-2024-poem]]).
- **Subtype drives the choice** — [[chicago-classification-v4|Chicago Classification v4.0]].
- **Response = Eckardt score (ES)**; success = ES ≤3 in most studies below.
- **Failure:** ES or HRM alone should not define it; TBE is the first-line test for continued or recurrent symptoms (American College of Gastroenterology [ACG] Recommendation [Rec] 17, *Strong; Very low*, [[acg-2020-achalasia]]).

## Technique

- **Divide the circular muscle layer of the LES without breaching the mucosa** ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).
- Add a partial fundoplication in the same operation (below); current standard = minimally invasive laparoscopic myotomy with partial fundoplication ([[asge-2020-achalasia]]).

### Surgical Approach

- **Laparoscopic is the preferred method** — decreased morbidity and faster recovery ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).

| Approach | Studies (patients) | Symptom improvement, mean (range) | Gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) **without** fundoplication | GERD **with** fundoplication |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open transthoracic (thoracotomy) | 13 (842) | 83% (64–97%) | 29% | 14% |
| Open transabdominal (laparotomy) | 10 (732) | 85% (48–100%) | 28% | 8% |
| Thoracoscopic | 8 (211) | 78% (31–94%) | 28% | no study |
| **Laparoscopic** | 39 (3,086) | **89% (77–100%)** | 31% | **9%** |

*Surgical myotomy outcomes by approach*
*Source: data from cohort and case-control studies summarized in ACG 2020 (no randomized comparisons; follow-up and success definitions vary)*

### Fundoplication After Myotomy

> **[[acg-2020-achalasia|ACG 2020]] Rec 7:** *"We recommend that myotomy with fundoplication is superior to myotomy without fundoplication in controlling distal esophageal acid exposure."* (Strong; Moderate)

- **GERD falls with a wrap** after thoracotomy, laparotomy and laparoscopy; no study has added fundoplication after thoracoscopic myotomy (table above). Without fundoplication, post-myotomy reflux runs ~30% or higher on pH testing or endoscopy, so Heller myotomy is nearly always paired with one ([[sages-2024-poem]]).
- **Double-blind randomized controlled trial (RCT), myotomy ± Dor:** abnormal acid exposure on pH monitoring **47% without vs 9% with** fundoplication — **relative risk (RR) 0.11 (95% confidence interval [CI] 0.02–0.59)**. Its 11-year follow-up: similar patient-reported reflux symptom control in both arms. ACG concludes the benefit is sustained long term; evidence limited by study heterogeneity ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).

> **ACG Rec 8:** suggest **either Dor or Toupet** fundoplication to control acid exposure after surgical myotomy (Conditional; Moderate).

| Dor (anterior) vs Toupet (posterior) | Finding ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]) |
|---|---|
| Multicenter RCT (Dor 24, Toupet 19) | Abnormal pH test **41% vs 21%** — not significant; similar dysphagia and regurgitation improvement |
| Metaregression of RCTs | Odds of abnormal 24-hour pH **0.16 (95% CI 0.11–0.24)** anterior vs **0.18 (0.13–0.25)** posterior — acid exposure not different; **dysphagia and reintervention significantly lower with posterior** |
| Updated meta-analysis | **Toupet better for length of stay and quality of life**; GERD, dysphagia, complications and treatment failure equivalent |

*Dor vs Toupet fundoplication after myotomy*
*Source: data from the trials and meta-analyses cited in ACG 2020*

### Myotomy Length

- **Type III needs a longer myotomy** — type III does poorly when treatment is confined to the LES or a short myotomy; ACG's algorithm gives type III a *tailored* POEM or *tailored* Heller myotomy ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).
- A laparoscopic myotomy cannot be extended proximally without limit, which POEM can ([[aga-2024-poem-advances|American Gastroenterological Association (AGA) 2024]]).
- Theorized, unproven: a long myotomy may predispose to a **blown-out myotomy**; limiting esophageal length while ensuring a complete myotomy at the esophagogastric junction may reduce it ([[aga-2024-poem-advances]]).

## Efficacy

- **Laparoscopic myotomy with an antireflux procedure:** symptom relief **90.3% (77–100%)**, complication rate **6.3%** (systematic review, 105 studies, 7,855 patients; open and minimally invasive myotomy) ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).
- **LHM meta-analysis cited by [[asge-2020-achalasia|American Society for Gastrointestinal Endoscopy (ASGE) 2020]]** (53 studies, 5,834 patients): [[dysphagia]] improvement **87.7% (95% CI 87–88%)** at mean 40 months; modelled **91.0% at 12 months**, **90.0% at 24 months**. Success = dysphagia improvement, not an ES threshold.
- Head-to-head data vs PD and POEM: [[#Selection vs POEM and Pneumatic Dilation]].

| Subtype | LHM success (meta-analysis, 1,575 patients) |
|---|---|
| **Type I** | **81%** |
| **Type II** | **92%** |
| **Type III** (spastic) | **71%** — vs **POEM 93%**; POEM more likely to succeed (odds ratio [OR] 3.50, 95% CI 1.39–8.77; P=0.007) |

*LHM success by achalasia subtype*
*Source: data from a 2019 meta-analysis of achalasia treatment outcomes by subtype (1,575 patients), cited in ACG 2020*
*[[acg-2020-achalasia|ACG 2020 Achalasia Guideline]]*
*LHM = laparoscopic Heller myotomy · POEM = peroral endoscopic myotomy*

- **Separate 9-study meta-analysis (429 LHM patients):** type II vs III **OR 6.86 (95% CI 2.72–17.28)**; type I vs II **OR 0.26 (0.12–0.56)**; type I vs III not significant (OR 1.89, 0.80–4.50) — **type II best, type III worst**, same rank order as PD ([[asge-2020-achalasia]]).
- **Type III, retrospective POEM (49) vs LHM (26):** response **98.0% vs 80.8%** (P=0.01), adverse events **6% vs 27%** (P<0.01) ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).

### Durability

- **Efficacy falls with time** — 73-patient series: excellent/good response **89% at 6 months → 57% at 6 years** ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).
- **Recurrent or persistent symptoms:** about **5–15%**, up to **21%** in some series; **retreatment in 18–27%** at a median 5.3 years ([[asge-2020-achalasia]]). About 10–15% after LHM or POEM ([[aga-2024-poem-advances]]).
- **Failure rate after LHM or POEM: 5–30% over 1–3 years**, higher with follow-up to 10 years and beyond ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).

## Post-LHM Reflux

**The rate depends on how GERD is defined** — read every figure with its definition:

| Definition (source) | Rate after LHM |
|---|---|
| GERD **symptoms** (ASGE meta-analysis, 5,834 patients) | **17.5%** (95% CI 16–19%) |
| GERD on **endoscopy** (same) | **11.5%** (95% CI 9–15%) |
| GERD on **pH monitoring** (same) | **11.1%** (95% CI 10–13%) |
| Abnormal distal acid exposure on **pH monitoring**, LHM with fundoplication (ACG-cited 2018 meta-analysis, 2,581 patients) | **16.8%** (95% CI 10.2–26.4%) |

*Post-LHM reflux rates by GERD definition*
*Source: data from the LHM meta-analysis cited in ASGE 2020 and from the literature summarized in ACG 2020*
*[[asge-2020-achalasia|ASGE 2020 Achalasia Guideline]] · [[acg-2020-achalasia|ACG 2020 Achalasia Guideline]]*
*GERD = gastroesophageal reflux disease · LHM = laparoscopic Heller myotomy*

- ACG's by-approach GERD rates (definition not stated) are in the [[#Surgical Approach]] table.
- **Follow-up:** the Society of American Gastrointestinal and Endoscopic Surgeons (SAGES) suggests **considering routine upper endoscopy after both POEM and LHM with fundoplication** to find the asymptomatic patient with significant reflux who may be at risk of adenocarcinoma; no data on timing or protocol. A symptomatic patient needs an objective GERD workup before treatment (expert opinion; [[sages-2024-poem]]). Testing: [[reflux-testing]], [[ambulatory-reflux-monitoring]].

## Selection vs POEM and Pneumatic Dilation

### Guideline Positions

|---|---|---|

| [[asge-2020-achalasia\|ASGE 2020]] | LHM and PD comparable (Rec 7, recommend; Moderate); POEM and LHM comparable (Rec 8, suggest; Low) — both by shared decision-making | **POEM preferred** (Rec 3, suggest; Very low) |

- **What changed in SAGES 2024:** comparator reframed to POEM *with appropriate PPI use* vs LHM *with fundoplication*; certainty very low → low. Appropriate PPI use = treating objectively confirmed abnormal acid exposure ([[sages-2024-poem]]).
- **Unfit for definitive therapy → [[botulinum-toxin|botulinum toxin]]**, not as definitive therapy in surgical candidates (ASGE Rec 2, recommend against; Moderate). ACG recommends LHM over botulinum toxin in patients fit for surgery (Strong; Moderate).

### LHM vs Pneumatic Dilation

**The LHM advantage is early, not durable** (Cheng meta-analysis, 5 RCTs, 498 patients; [[asge-2020-achalasia]]):

| Timepoint | Clinical success, LHM vs PD |
|---|---|
| 3 months | **RR 1.16 (95% CI 1.01–1.35), P=.04** — favors LHM |
| 1 year | **RR 1.14 (1.02–1.27), P=.02** — favors LHM |
| 2 years | RR 1.05 (0.91–1.22) — no difference |
| 5 years | RR 1.17 (0.84–1.64) — no difference |

*Clinical success of LHM vs PD over time*
*Source: data from the Cheng meta-analysis of randomized trials, cited in ASGE 2020*
*[[asge-2020-achalasia|ASGE 2020 Achalasia Guideline]]*
*LHM = laparoscopic Heller myotomy · PD = pneumatic dilation · RR = risk ratio · CI = confidence interval*

- Reflux, LES pressure and quality of life did **not** differ.
- **Serious mucosal injury favors LHM:** mucosal tears needing intervention at LHM vs perforation needing treatment after PD — **RR 0.25 (95% CI 0.08–0.81), P=.02**.
- European RCT behind ACG Rec 14: [[achalasia#Pneumatic Dilation (PD)]].

### LHM vs POEM

- **RCT, POEM (112) vs LHM with Dor (109):** clinical success at 2 years **83% vs 82%** — POEM noninferior ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).
- **SAGES 2024 pooled comparison** (3 RCTs + 23 observational studies): similar quality of life, dysphagia, ES, regurgitation and reintervention (OR 1.65, CI 0.38–7.08); POEM had more **PPI use >12 months (OR 2.62, 1.40–4.89; high certainty)** and more grade C/D esophagitis >12 months (OR 2.18, 0.17–27.50; very low). Late post-fundoplication side effects (gas-bloat, diarrhea) are not clearly reported in comparative studies, which prevents a fair comparison ([[sages-2024-poem]]).
- **Reflux is the main trade-off** (2018 meta-analysis, POEM 1,542 vs LHM with fundoplication 2,581): abnormal acid exposure **39.0% (95% CI 24.5–55.8%) vs 16.8% (10.2–26.4%)**; esophagitis **29.4% (18.5–43.3%) vs 7.6% (4.1–13.7%)**. Second 2018 meta-analysis (POEM 1,958 vs surgical myotomy 5,834): dysphagia improvement **93.5% vs 91.0%** at 12 months, **92.7% vs 90.0%** at 24 months (both P=0.01); POEM reflux OR **9.31** erosive esophagitis, **1.69** symptomatic GERD, **4.30** on pH monitoring ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).
  - ACG: POEM vs LHM with fundoplication or PD is associated with more GERD (Rec 10, Strong; Moderate).
- **Awaiz meta-analysis** (7 trials, 483 patients; [[asge-2020-achalasia]]): no difference in adverse events (OR 1.25), GERD (OR 1.27), length of stay, pain or long-term reflux; LHM procedure time longer (not significant). **Short-term clinical failure higher with LHM — 13% vs 0.85% (OR 9.82, 95% CI 2.06–46.80; P<.01)**, from 3 studies.

## Adverse Events

- **Intraoperative mucosal tear/perforation** — the characteristic LHM injury; serious tears are less frequent than PD perforation ([[#LHM vs Pneumatic Dilation]]).
  - **Prior PD** may raise the intraoperative mucosal perforation rate, without changing long-term symptomatic outcome ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]); SAGES panel experience differs ([[#Contradictions]]).
  - **Prior [[botulinum-toxin|botulinum toxin]]** does not significantly affect performance or outcomes of myotomy (ACG Rec 5, Conditional; Low) — though in one retrospective series (209 LHM), complications (dysphagia, perforation) were **10.4% after prior endoscopic treatment vs 5.4%** with myotomy alone (P<0.05) ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).
  - **Redo LHM:** adhesions → perforation **1.5–20%** ([[asge-2020-achalasia]]).
- **Post-fundoplication problems** — a **tight wrap or wrap herniation** can cause myotomy failure ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]); late gas-bloat syndrome and diarrhea ([[sages-2024-poem]]). Post-fundoplication outflow workup: [[hrm-antireflux-surgery]].
- **GERD** — [[#Post-LHM Reflux]].

## Failed Myotomy — Salvage and Sequencing

- **Evaluate first:** TBE is the first-line test (ACG Rec 17, [[#Overview]]); AGA adds repeat [[upper-endoscopy|esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD)]] and HRM ([[aga-2024-poem-advances]]).
- **Mechanisms:** incomplete myotomy, scarring, anatomic distortion; after LHM, also tight wrap or wrap herniation ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).

> [!pearl] Salvage after failed LHM is a shared decision
> The only RCT after failed LHM found POEM 62% vs PD 27% success; choose by shared decision-making weighing the cause of failure, local expertise, patient characteristics and preferences (table below; [[aga-2024-poem-advances]]).

### Salvage Options After Failed LHM

| Option | Outcome | Study design | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **POEM vs PD** | **62% vs 27%** success; no difference in esophagitis, reflux symptoms, PPI use or serious adverse events | The only RCT, after failed LHM | AGA 2024 (BPA 12) |
| **PD** | **89%** success; mean 2.5 dilations (range 1–3), mean 26-month interval; complications extremely low · ASGE: response **50–75%** | Systematic review, 87 patients | ACG 2020, ASGE 2020 |
| **POEM** | **94%** (Eckardt ≤3) at 12 months; 7 patients with adverse events (2 mediastinitis managed conservatively, 6 mucosal defects closed endoscopically) | 13-center prospective registry, 51 patients, mean 9.5 years after LHM | ACG 2020 |
| POEM | **95.7%** (Eckardt ≤3, no further treatment) at median 28 months · ASGE: **92–100%** across studies | Single-center study, 46 patients | ACG 2020, ASGE 2020 |
| POEM | **81% vs 94%** in LHM-naïve patients (P=0.01); technical success 98% vs 100%, adverse events 8% vs 13% (both not significant) | Retrospective cohort, 90 vs 90, median 8.5 months | ACG 2020, ASGE 2020 |
| **Redo LHM** | **73–89%**; perforation 1.5–20% from adhesions | No head-to-head comparison with POEM | ASGE 2020 |

- **[[pneumatic-dilation|PD]]** is an appropriate and safe option after initial surgical myotomy or POEM (ACG Rec 18, Strong; Moderate); spares a second invasive procedure and can address incomplete myotomy, scarring and a tight fundoplication ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).
- **POEM** is a safe option after prior PD or LHM (ACG Rec 20, Strong; Low; [[acg-2020-achalasia]]) — a safety statement, not a comparative one. **POEM may be superior to PD** after failed POEM or LHM; decide by shared decision-making weighing reflux risk, repeat interventions, preference and local expertise (AGA BPA 12). After prior LHM, AGA strongly suggests a POEM tunnel **opposite** the prior surgery site (fibrosis) ([[aga-2024-poem-advances]]). Detail: [[poem]].
- **ASGE Rec 4** (suggest; Very low): after failed POEM or LHM, PD or redo myotomy by the same or the alternative technique ([[asge-2020-achalasia]]).

### Heller Myotomy Before Esophagectomy

> **ACG Rec 19:** *"We recommend that Heller myotomy be considered before esophagectomy in patients who have failed PD and POEM if the anatomy is conducive and there is evidence of incomplete myotomy."* (Strong; Very low — small case series in heterogeneous populations)

- **End-stage achalasia:** barium esophagram with **severe dilation (width >6 cm)** and complicated anatomic distortion (**sink-trap**), with severe symptoms and life-threatening complications (aspiration, malnutrition) ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]).
- **Workup:** barium esophagram (anatomy), upper endoscopy (esophagitis, stricture), manometry or [[flip-panometry|functional lumen imaging probe (FLIP)]] (LES function).
  - Severe anatomy + significant bolus retention + **complete** myotomy → refer for esophagectomy.
  - Evidence of **incomplete** myotomy → offer Heller myotomy.
- **Esophagectomy** (ACG Rec 11, Strong; Low) is a last resort, at high-volume referral centers ([[acg-2020-achalasia]]) — [[achalasia#Esophagectomy]].

## Contradictions

- **Post-LHM GERD rate** differs between ACG 2020 and ASGE 2020 by definition (symptoms vs endoscopy vs pH) — [[#Post-LHM Reflux]].
- **More reflux after POEM than LHM?** Yes per ACG 2020 (Rec 10) and SAGES 2024 (more long-term PPI use); no significant difference in the Awaiz meta-analysis cited by ASGE 2020 (OR 1.27). The newer, larger analyses carry the page.
- **Post-POEM abnormal acid exposure:** ACG 39.0% (95% CI 24.5–55.8%) vs newer AGA 2024 41–56%; [[poem]] uses the AGA range, and 39.0% appears here only beside its paired 16.8% post-LHM rate.
- **Prior PD:** ACG — may raise intraoperative perforation; SAGES 2024 panel experience — did not make POEM or LHM harder. Different endpoints, neither graded ([[acg-2020-achalasia]], [[sages-2024-poem]]).
