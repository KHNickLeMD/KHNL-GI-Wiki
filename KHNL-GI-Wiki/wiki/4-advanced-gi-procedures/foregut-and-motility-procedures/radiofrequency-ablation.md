Radiofrequency ablation (RFA) is the preferred primary ablative modality within [[endoscopic-eradication-therapy|endoscopic eradication therapy]] for flat dysplastic [[barretts-esophagus|Barrett's esophagus]]. It delivers controlled thermal energy via balloon-based (circumferential) or focal catheters to destroy metaplastic epithelium, applied after [[polypectomy-emr|EMR]] of all visible lesions. Pooled adverse events (RFA ± EMR): ~8.8% serious, stricture 5.6% (most common), bleeding 1%, perforation 0.6% ([[asge-2018-barretts-eet|ASGE 2018]]).
