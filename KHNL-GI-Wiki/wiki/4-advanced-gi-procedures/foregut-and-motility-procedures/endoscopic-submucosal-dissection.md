# Endoscopic Submucosal Dissection (ESD)

En-bloc endoscopic resection of early GI neoplasia using electrosurgical knives (rather than a snare), circumventing the size limit of EMR and enabling accurate R0 assessment of larger lesions. First-line (over surgery) for many early-stage esophageal and gastric cancers; preferred over [[polypectomy-emr|EMR]] for larger lesions where piecemeal EMR would prevent an R0 margin. Per [[asge-2023-esd]].

## Definitions
- **Early-stage cancer:** malignant tumor confined to mucosa and/or submucosa, no deeper involvement, no locoregional or distant spread.
- **En-bloc resection:** entire neoplastic/dysplastic/cancerous tissue removed in **1 piece** (vs piecemeal). EMR en-bloc is capped by maximum snare diameter → larger lesions need piecemeal EMR, which cannot reliably confirm R0.
- **R0 resection:** resection margins microscopically disease-free.
- **ESD principle:** electrosurgical-knife dissection removes lesions much larger than snares allow in one piece → critical for a low recurrence rate.

## ESD vs EMR vs Surgery — Lesion-Based Selection

All recommendations are *conditional* (GRADE "suggest"). Thresholds below are for early-stage, well/moderately-differentiated, **nonulcerated** cancers. [[asge-2023-esd]]

| Cancer | ESD over EMR | Either ESD or EMR | Surgery |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Esophageal SCC (ESCC)** | >15 mm | ≤15 mm | **Against surgery** when endoscopic resection feasible |
| **Esophageal adenocarcinoma (EAC)** | >20 mm | ≤20 mm | (no comparative ESD-vs-surgery evidence) |
| **Gastric adenocarcinoma (GAC)**, intestinal type | 20–30 mm | <20 mm | **Against surgery** for ≤30 mm; **poorly differentiated (any size) → surgical evaluation** over endoscopy |

- Lesions exceeding size/depth criteria (e.g. deep submucosal invasion) → **surgical evaluation** over endoscopic approaches.
- Applies to early [[esophageal-cancer|ESCC/EAC]], early [[gastric-adenocarcinoma|GAC]], and their precursor lesions ([[barretts-esophagus|Barrett's]] dysplasia, gastric [[gastric-intestinal-metaplasia|intestinal metaplasia]]/dysplasia).

## Curative (R0) Resection Criteria
Clinical success = **curative resection** requires ALL of the following on histology of the resected specimen:
1. Lateral **and** deep margins microscopically free of malignant cells (R0)
2. Well (G1) or moderate (G2) differentiation
3. No lymphovascular invasion
4. No deep invasion beyond the submucosa

Failure of any criterion → not curative → consider surgery / adjuvant therapy.

## Depth of Invasion
| Level | Depth |
|---|---|
| **M1** | Intraepithelial noninvasive carcinoma / carcinoma in situ |
| **M2** | Microinvasive into the lamina propria |
| **M3** | Microinvasive into the muscularis mucosa |
| **SM1** | Upper third of submucosa |
| **SM2** | Middle third of submucosa |
| **SM3** | Lower third of submucosa |

Deeper invasion → higher lymph-node metastasis risk → favors surgery.

## Gastric ESD Appropriateness Criteria
- **Absolute criteria:** mucosal adenocarcinoma (or high-grade dysplasia), **intestinal type**, G1/G2 differentiation, measuring **≤2 cm**, nonulcerated.
- **Expanded criteria** (include, e.g.): intestinal-type G1/G2 of any size without ulceration; intestinal-type G1/G2 with submucosal invasion <500 µm; intestinal-type G1/G2 ≤3 cm with ulceration; diffuse-type G3/G4 ≤2 cm without ulceration; en-bloc resection for lesions at risk for submucosally invasive cancer. [[asge-2023-esd]]

## Outcomes
Meta-analysis (8 observational studies; 821 ESD vs 1306 EMR):
- **Clinical success (curative resection):** ESD **93.3%** vs EMR **72.1%** (RR 1.33, 95% CI 1.02–1.74).
- **Local recurrence:** ESD **0.5%** vs EMR **5.2%** (RR 0.19, 95% CI 0.07–0.48).
- **Distant recurrence:** no difference.

## Adverse Events
- **Bleeding:** no significant difference vs EMR.
- **Stricture:** no significant difference (9.2% ESD vs 7.4% EMR).
- **Perforation:** **higher with ESD — 5.7% vs 0.8%** (RR 4.30, 95% CI 1.22–15.12).
- Technically demanding; often available only at tertiary/quaternary referral centers.

## Surveillance
- Data on post-ESD surveillance tools and intervals were **notably lacking**; guideline guidance here relies on expert opinion. Follow lesion-/organ-specific surveillance (see [[barretts-esophagus]], [[gastric-adenocarcinoma]], [[colorectal-cancer]]).
