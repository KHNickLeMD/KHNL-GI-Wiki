Peripheral μ-opioid receptor agonist antimotility agent for symptomatic control of [[acute-diarrhea|acute diarrhea]]. May be given to immunocompetent adults with watery diarrhea; **avoid** in children <18 y and in fever/inflammatory diarrhea (toxic-megacolon risk).
