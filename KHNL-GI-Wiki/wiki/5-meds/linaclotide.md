## Class

Guanylate cyclase-C (GC-C) agonist; intestinal secretagogue (↑cGMP → ↑Cl⁻/HCO₃⁻ secretion).

## Indications & Dosing

- **[[chronic-idiopathic-constipation]]**: 145 µg PO daily (72 µg if poor tolerance) — Strong/Moderate ([[aga-acg-2023-constipation]])
- **[[irritable-bowel-syndrome]]-C**: 290 µg PO daily — Strong/High ([[acg-2020-ibs]])
- Take ≥30 min before first meal

## Adverse Effects

- Diarrhea (most common; dose-limiting in ~20%)
- Contraindicated in pediatric <6y (fatal dehydration in juvenile animals)

## Place in Therapy

- Stepwise after OTC failure for CIC; first-line Rx for IBS-C alongside [[plecanatide]]
