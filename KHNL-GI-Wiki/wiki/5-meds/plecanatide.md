## Class

Guanylate cyclase-C (GC-C) agonist — a nonabsorbed 16-amino-acid uroguanylin analog. Stimulates GC-C on enterocytes → cGMP → CFTR activation → fluid and electrolyte secretion; also reduces visceral hypersensitivity in an animal model. Unlike [[linaclotide]] (not pH-sensitive), plecanatide binds GC-C with **higher affinity in the acidic proximal duodenum**.

## Indications & Dosing

| Indication | Dose | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| [[chronic-idiopathic-constipation\|CIC]], after OTC agents fail | **3 mg PO daily** | Strong rec, moderate certainty ([[aga-acg-2023-constipation]] **Rec 9**) |
| [[irritable-bowel-syndrome\|IBS]]-C | **3 mg PO daily** | Strong/High ([[acg-2020-ibs]]); AGA: conditional, moderate certainty ([[aga-2022-ibs-c]] **Rec 2**) |

- **One dose, both indications: FDA-approved at 3 mg once daily for IBS-C *and* CIC** ([[aga-2022-ibs-c]]; [[aga-acg-2023-constipation]] — "approved at the same dose for IBS-C"). Trials studied **3 mg and 6 mg**; **6 mg is not an approved dose for either indication** — there is no separate, higher IBS-C dose.
- **Administration:** with or without food (contrast [[linaclotide]], which must be taken ≥30 min before the first meal).
- **Duration:** trials ran **12 weeks**; the drug label sets no limit. Can replace or be added to OTC agents.
- **Refractory constipation:** plecanatide is among the FDA-approved agents to be trialled alone *and* in combination before off-label agents or surgery ([[aga-2026-refractory-constipation]] BPA 7). Its refractory-setting evidence is **narrower than [[prucalopride]]'s or [[linaclotide]]'s** — plecanatide has shown efficacy in *"severe"* constipation rather than in constipation unresponsive to OTC laxatives, and AGA notes these trials **lacked rigorous evaluation of prior OTC therapy or colonic transit**.

### Who was studied — trial entry criteria (IBS-C)

Phase 3 entry ([[aga-2022-ibs-c]]) — the population the conditional recommendation rests on:

- Met **Rome III** criteria for [[irritable-bowel-syndrome|IBS]]-C
- During the 2-week baseline, reported "**no more than 3 CSBMs or >6 SBMs per week**" and an **average worst abdominal pain score of 3 on a 0–10 scale**
- Could **not** report worst abdominal pain of **0 for more than 2 days**, **Bristol 7 for 1 or more days**, or **Bristol 6 for more than 1 day**

## Efficacy

**IBS-C** (3 mg, **n = 814 vs 818** placebo, 3 RCTs, 12 wk) ([[aga-2022-ibs-c]]):

- FDA responder endpoint met by **27.4% vs 16.9%**; failure of symptom relief RR **0.87** (95% CI 0.83–0.92) — **108 fewer failures per 1,000** (67–141 fewer). Certainty **moderate**.
- Individual symptoms over the initial 12 weeks: abdominal-pain improvement RR **0.86** (0.81–0.92), **risk difference 10.1%**; CSBM RR **0.84** (0.79–0.91), **risk difference 10.9%**.
- [[acg-2020-ibs]] pooled estimate: RR of symptom persistence **0.88** (0.82–0.94), **NNT 10**; OR of FDA-responder response **1.87** (1.47–2.38), **NNT 9**. Separation from placebo appeared by **the end of the first treatment week** and held through 12 weeks.

**CIC** (3 mg, 3 RCTs, 12 wk) ([[aga-acg-2023-constipation]]):

| Outcome | Effect vs placebo | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| CSBMs per week | MD **+1.1** (95% CI 0.85–1.35) | High |
| SBMs per week | MD **+1.66** (1.37–1.94) | High |
| Responder (**≥3 CSBMs/wk *and* ≥1 CSBM over baseline for ≥9 of 12 weeks, including ≥3 of the last 4**) | RR **1.78** (1.46–2.18) — **88 more per 1,000** (52–134) | Moderate |
| Stool consistency (Bristol) | MD **+0.83** (0.60–1.05) | Moderate |
| **Diarrhea leading to discontinuation** | RR **5.39** (2.40–12.11) — **27 more per 1,000** (9–69) | Moderate |

**Overall certainty for plecanatide: moderate** in both guidelines.

## Adverse Effects

- **Diarrhea** — most common AE: **4.3%** vs 1% placebo; led to discontinuation in **1.2%** (vs 0% placebo); **severe diarrhea 0%**. Serious adverse events 0.8%, similar to placebo, with **no diarrhea-related SAEs** (the single death, a drowning, was deemed unrelated) ([[acg-2020-ibs]], [[aga-2022-ibs-c]]).
- IBS-C: any AE leading to discontinuation RR **5.68** (95% CI 2.10–15.39) — **23 more per 1,000** (5–70) ([[aga-2022-ibs-c]]). In CIC the absolute risk is likewise small.
- Diarrhea odds vs placebo are increased for both GC-C agonists with **no significant difference between plecanatide (OR 5.55, 95% CI 1.62–19.00) and linaclotide (OR 8.02, 95% CI 5.20–12.37)** — there are no head-to-head trials, so plecanatide should not be described as better tolerated.
- No clear outcome differences in patients >65 y (sample too small for formal analysis).

## Contraindications

> **Gap — no contraindication for plecanatide is stated anywhere in the ingested corpus.** [[aga-acg-2023-constipation]] prints a mechanical-GI-obstruction contraindication for **[[linaclotide]] only**; neither it nor [[aga-2022-ibs-c]] nor [[acg-2020-ibs]] states one for plecanatide. **Do not extrapolate linaclotide's contraindication onto this page** — FDA labeling would need to be ingested first.

> **Gap — pediatric use not in the ingested corpus.** None of the ingested sources addresses use in children ([[aga-acg-2023-constipation]] explicitly excludes pediatric populations). A previously stated contraindication in children <6 y was unsourced and has been removed.

## Place in Therapy

- Alternative to [[linaclotide]] for IBS-C and CIC; **no head-to-head trials**.
- **CIC:** strong recommendation, alongside [[linaclotide]] and [[prucalopride]]; [[lubiprostone]] is only conditional ([[aga-acg-2023-constipation]]).
- **IBS-C:** one of the **conditional** options with [[tenapanor]], [[lubiprostone]] and [[tegaserod]]; [[linaclotide]] is the only **strongly** recommended IBS-C agent in [[aga-2022-ibs-c]].
