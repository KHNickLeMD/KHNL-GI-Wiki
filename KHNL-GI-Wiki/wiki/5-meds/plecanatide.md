## Class

Guanylate cyclase-C (GC-C) agonist — a nonabsorbed 16-amino-acid uroguanylin analog. Stimulates GC-C on enterocytes → cGMP → CFTR activation → fluid and electrolyte secretion; also reduces visceral hypersensitivity in an animal model. Unlike [[linaclotide]] (not pH-sensitive), plecanatide binds GC-C with **higher affinity in the acidic proximal duodenum**.

## Indications & Dosing

| Indication | Dose | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| [[chronic-idiopathic-constipation\|CIC]], after OTC agents fail | **3 mg PO daily** | Strong rec, moderate certainty ([[aga-acg-2023-constipation]]) |
| [[irritable-bowel-syndrome\|IBS]]-C | **3 mg PO daily** | Strong/High ([[acg-2020-ibs]]); AGA: conditional, moderate certainty ([[aga-2022-ibs-c]]) |

- **FDA-approved at 3 mg once daily for both IBS-C and CIC.** Phase 3 trials studied **3 mg and 6 mg**; 3 mg is the approved and recommended dose.
- **Administration:** with or without food.
- **Duration:** trials ran **12 weeks**; the drug label sets no limit. Can replace or be added to OTC agents.
- Efficacy (IBS-C, 3 mg, n=814 vs 818 placebo): FDA responder endpoint met by **27.4% vs 16.9%**; abdominal-pain and CSBM responder risk differences ~**10%** each.

## Adverse Effects

- **Diarrhea** — most common AE: **4.3%** vs 1% placebo; led to discontinuation in **1.2%** (vs 0% placebo). Serious adverse events 0.8%, same as placebo.
- In CIC, diarrhea leading to discontinuation is more likely than placebo (RR 5.39, 95% CI 2.40–12.11; 27 more per 1,000), but absolute risk is small.
- Diarrhea odds vs placebo are increased for both GC-C agonists with **no significant difference between plecanatide (OR 5.55, 95% CI 1.62–19.00) and linaclotide (OR 8.02, 95% CI 5.20–12.37)** — there are no head-to-head trials, so plecanatide should not be described as better tolerated.
- No clear outcome differences in patients >65 y (sample too small for formal analysis).

> **Gap — pediatric contraindication not in the ingested corpus.** None of the ingested sources addresses use in children ([[aga-acg-2023-constipation]] explicitly excludes pediatric populations). A previously stated contraindication in children <6 y was unsourced and has been removed.

## Place in Therapy

- Alternative to [[linaclotide]] for IBS-C and CIC; **no head-to-head trials**.
