5-HT4 receptor agonist prokinetic/secretory agent, **6 mg BID**, for **women <65 with ≤1 cardiovascular risk factor** who have [[irritable-bowel-syndrome|IBS-C]] failing secretagogues (ACG 2020, Conditional/Low). Cardiovascular-risk restriction reflects prior withdrawal over ischemic-event signal.
