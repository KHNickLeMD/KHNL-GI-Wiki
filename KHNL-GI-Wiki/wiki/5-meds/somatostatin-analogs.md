# Somatostatin Analogs

Somatostatin analogs (SSAs) — **octreotide** (including long-acting LAR) and **lanreotide** — bind somatostatin receptors (chiefly SSTR2/5) on neuroendocrine cells, suppressing hormone secretion and exerting an antiproliferative effect. In [[gastroenteropancreatic-neuroendocrine-tumors|GEP-NETs]] they are first-line for symptom control in functioning tumors (e.g., carcinoid syndrome) and for tumor-growth control in somatostatin-receptor-positive (SSTR-PET-avid) metastatic disease. SSAs are also used around [[gastroenteropancreatic-neuroendocrine-tumors|peptide receptor radionuclide therapy]] and to manage carcinoid crisis ([[nccn-2026-net]]).
