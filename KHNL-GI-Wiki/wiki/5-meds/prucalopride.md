## Class

Highly selective 5-HT4 receptor agonist; prokinetic.

## Indications & Dosing

- **[[chronic-idiopathic-constipation]]**: **2 mg PO once daily** in adults — Strong/Low ([[aga-acg-2023-constipation]])
  - **Severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance <30 mL/min): 1 mg daily.**
  - **No age-based dose reduction** — efficacy in persons **≥65 y** is comparable to the overall study population.
- Approved for chronic constipation, **not** [[irritable-bowel-syndrome|IBS-C]].
- No CV signal in large studies (unlike older 5-HT4 agonists [[tegaserod]]/cisapride) — cardiovascular adverse events were not more common than placebo.

## Contraindications

Intestinal perforation or obstruction; [[crohns-disease|Crohn's disease]]; [[ulcerative-colitis|ulcerative colitis]]; [[toxic-megacolon|toxic megacolon]]/megarectum ([[aga-acg-2023-constipation]]).

## Adverse Effects

- Headache (most common, often transient), nausea, diarrhea, abdominal pain — typically within the first week, resolving in a few days.
- **5%** discontinued prucalopride because of side effects.
- Label cautions clinicians to watch for unusual changes in mood/behavior and suicidal ideation; causality unclear.
- No drug-associated risk of miscarriage, major birth defects, or adverse maternal/fetal outcomes identified.

## Place in Therapy

- Rx option after OTC laxative failure for CIC; reasonable for patients with slow-transit pattern
- Acts by inducing and increasing the amplitude of colonic **high-amplitude propagated contractions**, which propel colonic contents
