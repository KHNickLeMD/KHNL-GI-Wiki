Potassium-competitive acid blocker ([[potassium-competitive-acid-blockers|P-CAB]]) — the only agent of the class available in the US. Blocks the gastric H⁺/K⁺-ATPase competitively at the potassium-binding site. Antisecretory effect is **more rapid, more robust and more prolonged than standard-dose [[proton-pump-inhibitors|proton pump inhibitors (PPIs)]]**, holding intragastric pH above 6 for longer; taken fed or fasted, and not dependent on CYP2C19 metabolism ([[acg-2024-hp-treatment]]). For class-level indications and the American Gastroenterological Association (AGA) 2024 Best Practice Advice (BPA) on when to use a P-CAB, see [[potassium-competitive-acid-blockers]].

**Brand name:** Voquezna (US Food and Drug Administration (FDA)-approved 2022 for [[helicobacter-pylori-infection|H. pylori]] eradication)

*The full P-CAB-vs-PPI mechanism/property comparison lives on [[potassium-competitive-acid-blockers#Mechanism vs PPIs|the class page]] — not repeated here.*

## Indications & Dosing

### H. pylori — First-Line

All first-line regimens run **14 days** ([[acg-2024-hp-treatment]] Table 5).

| Regimen | Components | American College of Gastroenterology (ACG) 2024 grade |
|---|---|---|
| **P-CAB dual** (Voquezna DualPak) | Vonoprazan **20 mg b.i.d.** + amoxicillin **1,000 mg t.i.d.** | Conditional, moderate quality — suggested as a first-line option in treatment-naive patients (Rec 3) |
| **P-CAB triple** (Voquezna TriplePak) | Vonoprazan **20 mg b.i.d.** + amoxicillin **1,000 mg b.i.d.** + clarithromycin **500 mg b.i.d.** | Conditional, moderate quality — suggested **over** PPI-clarithromycin triple when clarithromycin susceptibility is unknown (Rec 4) |

> [!pitfall] Amoxicillin frequency differs between the two packs
> Dual therapy is amoxicillin **t.i.d.**; triple therapy is amoxicillin **b.i.d.** Vonoprazan is 20 mg b.i.d. in both, and both run 14 days ([[acg-2024-hp-treatment]]).

- **Where vonoprazan sits:** only optimized [[bismuth-quadruple-therapy|bismuth quadruple therapy (BQT)]] carries a *strong* first-line recommendation (Rec 1, moderate quality) and is the preferred empiric regimen when susceptibility is unknown. P-CAB dual (and rifabutin triple) are suitable empiric alternatives **in patients without penicillin allergy** — of all first-line options only BQT is suitable in true penicillin allergy.
- **Dual therapy avoids clarithromycin entirely**, so it needs no pretreatment susceptibility testing — the only vonoprazan regimen usable without susceptibility data. In the US/European registration trial it was noninferior to lansoprazole-clarithromycin triple in clarithromycin-sensitive strains (modified intention-to-treat 79% vs 79%) and superior in **clarithromycin-resistant** strains (**70% vs 32%**); overall 77% vs 69%.
- **Triple therapy is not truly empiric.** Although FDA-approved for first-line use independent of resistance testing, ACG states clarithromycin-containing regimens should be reserved for **demonstrated clarithromycin-sensitive strains**; where susceptibility is unknown, ACG positions it only for patients with **no history of macrolide exposure and no penicillin allergy, when no other obvious first-line option is available**.
- **Societies diverge on how strongly to prefer vonoprazan here** — [[aga-2024-pcab|AGA 2024]] BPA 7 says use a P-CAB *in place of* a PPI in eradication regimens for most patients, while ACG keeps both vonoprazan regimens conditional; the contradiction is worked through on [[potassium-competitive-acid-blockers#Helicobacter pylori|the class page]].

### H. pylori — Salvage

Salvage regimens also run 14 days; there are **no FDA-approved regimens for persistent infection** ([[acg-2024-hp-treatment]] Table 6).

| Regimen | Components | ACG 2024 stance | Absolute conditions if used |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P-CAB triple** (Voquezna TriplePak) | Vonoprazan 20 mg + clarithromycin 500 mg + amoxicillin 1,000 mg, all b.i.d. | **No recommendation — evidence gap.** FDA-approved for H. pylori but never evaluated as salvage, so ACG recommends neither for nor against | Susceptibility testing **required**: demonstrated clarithromycin-sensitive strain, **and** no previous macrolide exposure |
| **High-dose dual** | Vonoprazan 20 mg (or double-dose PPI) b.i.d. **or** t.i.d. + amoxicillin 1,000 mg t.i.d. | **No recommendation — evidence gap.** Insufficient North American evidence (Rec 11) | Voquezna DualPak itself has never been evaluated as salvage |

- **Where ACG does suggest P-CAB-clarithromycin triple:** in treatment-experienced patients whose infection is **confirmed clarithromycin-sensitive**, PPI- or P-CAB-clarithromycin triple therapy is suggested (Key concept 5, expert consensus). If a patient was adherent to but failed PPI-clarithromycin triple, ACG suggests escalating acid suppression — esomeprazole, rabeprazole, or a P-CAB — if not already used.
- **After a vonoprazan-based first-line failure**, the suggested next step is optimized BQT (Rec 6, conditional/very low) since BQT has not yet been used; BQT-experienced patients go to rifabutin triple instead (Rec 8). Full salvage sequencing: [[helicobacter-pylori-infection#Salvage Therapy (After First-Line Failure)|H. pylori therapeutics]].
- Where susceptibility testing is unavailable, ACG's salvage algorithm still lists **high-dose PPI or P-CAB dual** as an option in patients without penicillin allergy — but only when optimized BQT and rifabutin triple are not options.
- **Test of cure after any course:** urea breath test, fecal antigen, or biopsy-based test ≥4 weeks after therapy — see [[helicobacter-pylori-infection#Post-Treatment Confirmation|post-treatment confirmation]]. Whether vonoprazan itself must be held beforehand is unsettled ([[potassium-competitive-acid-blockers#Value / Safety Caveats|class page]]).

### Erosive Esophagitis and Other Indications

*The AGA 2024 P-CAB Best Practice Advice is class-level and lives, indication by indication with its BPA numbers, on [[potassium-competitive-acid-blockers#When to Use — AGA 2024 Best Practice Advice|the class page]] — including the [[gerd|gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD)]] / [[reflux-testing|Los Angeles (LA) grade]] A-B vs C-D split and [[peptic-ulcer-disease|peptic ulcer disease (PUD)]]. Vonoprazan is the only US agent, so every one of those statements applies to it directly.*

- **Doses studied** in the single US/Europe erosive esophagitis (EE) trial, the trial driving the severe-EE advice: **20 mg daily for healing** (8 weeks) and **10 or 20 mg daily for maintenance of healing** (24 weeks), against lansoprazole 30 mg and 15 mg respectively ([[aga-2024-pcab]]). Outcomes on the class page.

## Safety & Value

*Class-level cost, prior-authorization, and long-term-safety caveats are on [[potassium-competitive-acid-blockers#Value / Safety Caveats|the class page]].* Agent-specific:

- Generally well tolerated in randomized controlled trials (RCTs); **diarrhea (4%) and dysgeusia (4%)** were the most common adverse events in the vonoprazan-clarithromycin triple trial, with low discontinuation rates ([[acg-2024-hp-treatment]]).
- Same enteric-infection risk as PPIs, including [[clostridioides-difficile|*C. difficile*]]; absorption-related drug interactions similar to PPIs, but **no effect on CYP450-metabolized drugs** ([[acg-2024-hp-treatment]] Supplementary Table 1).
