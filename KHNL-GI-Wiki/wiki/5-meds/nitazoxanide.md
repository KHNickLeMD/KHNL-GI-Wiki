## Class

Antiprotozoal.

## Indications & Dosing

- **[[cryptosporidiosis]]:** 500 mg BID × 3 days in **immunocompetent** hosts ([[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]]).
  - **Limited efficacy in advanced immunosuppression** — immune restoration (antiretroviral therapy / immune reconstitution) is the key intervention, not nitazoxanide.
- **[[giardiasis]]:** 500 mg BID × 3 days — **alternative** agent ([[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]]). Tinidazole 2 g single dose is preferred; metronidazole 250 mg TID × 5–7 days is an effective alternative.
