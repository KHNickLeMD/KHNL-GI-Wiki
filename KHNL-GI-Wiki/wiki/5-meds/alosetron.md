5-HT3 receptor antagonist for **women with severe [[irritable-bowel-syndrome|IBS-D]]** refractory to conventional therapy (ACG 2020, Conditional/Low). Restricted-distribution **REMS program** because of the risk of **[[colon-ischemia|ischemic colitis]]** and complications of constipation.
