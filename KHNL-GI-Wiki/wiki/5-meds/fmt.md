Transfer of stool-derived microbiota to restore a disrupted gut microbiome. Established role: **recurrent [[clostridioides-difficile|C. difficile]] infection** after appropriate antibiotics — via conventional FMT or FDA-approved products (fecal microbiota, live-jslm [RBL]; fecal microbiota spores, live-brpk). AGA 2024 conditionally advises **against** routine use for IBD, IBS, or other indications outside recurrent CDI.
