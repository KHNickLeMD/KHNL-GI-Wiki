Glucagon-like peptide-2 (GLP-2) agonists (eg, teduglutide) are trophic to intestinal mucosa — increasing villous height and crypt depth and slowing GI transit — and are used to enhance **intestinal adaptation** and facilitate weaning from long-term parenteral nutrition in **short bowel syndrome**, including IBD-associated intestinal failure. See [[nutrition-in-ibd]] for the role in SBS management. [[aga-2024-diet-nutrition-ibd]]

> Full agent-level detail (dosing, monitoring, adverse effects) needs a dedicated SBS/intestinal-failure source; the AGA 2024 diet CPU references GLP-2 agonists only in the context of PN weaning.
