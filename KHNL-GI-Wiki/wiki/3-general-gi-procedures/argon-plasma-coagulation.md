**Argon plasma coagulation (APC)** — a non-contact thermal (electrocoagulation) modality delivering monopolar current to tissue via ionized argon gas, used endoscopically for hemostasis and tissue ablation of superficial vascular lesions.

Common GI uses (from ingested sources): ablation of **gastric antral vascular ectasia (GAVE)** and small-bowel/colonic **[[angioectasia|angioectasias]]** ([[aga-2024-ida-management]]); [[radiation-proctopathy|chronic radiation proctopathy]]; and endoscopic hemostasis in GI bleeding ([[acute-lower-gi-bleeding]], [[upper-gi-bleeding]]).
