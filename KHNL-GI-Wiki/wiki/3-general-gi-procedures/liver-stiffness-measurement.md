Liver stiffness measurement (LSM) is a non-invasive estimate of hepatic fibrosis and portal pressure using elastography — a core [[noninvasive-liver-disease-assessment|noninvasive liver disease assessment (NILDA)]] tool. It stages [[cirrhosis|compensated advanced chronic liver disease (cACLD)]] and detects [[portal-hypertension|clinically significant portal hypertension (CSPH)]] **without [[liver-biopsy|biopsy]] or [[hepatic-venous-pressure-gradient|hepatic venous pressure gradient (HVPG)]]**. US guidance (American Association for the Study of Liver Diseases [AASLD], American Gastroenterological Association [AGA]) governs; Baveno VIII/VII fill gaps.

## Modalities

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Vibration-controlled transient elastography (VCTE / FibroScan)** | Reference method. In Baveno VIII, "LSM" means VCTE unless another modality is named; every kPa cut-off on this page is VCTE unless labelled otherwise. Preferred over ultrasound shear wave elastography in clinical pathways — more standardized, better established cutoffs ([[aga-2026-metald]]) |
| Point shear-wave elastography (pSWE / acoustic radiation force impulse [ARFI]) | Performance more platform- and operator-dependent ([[aga-2026-metald]]). Non-transient elastography cut-offs not solidly validated for CSPH or high-risk varices ([[aasld-2023-portal-hypertension]]) |
| 2D shear-wave elastography (2D-SWE) | Same caveats as pSWE |
| Magnetic resonance elastography (MRE) | Separate thresholds: **<3.5 kPa** generally rules out cACLD, **≥5.0 kPa** rules it in ([[aasld-2023-portal-hypertension]]); **≥5.1 kPa** suggests CSPH ([[aasld-2024-nilda-portal-htn]]). Higher cost, no point-of-care availability ([[aga-2017-elastography-liver-fibrosis]]) |

## Technique

VCTE ([[aga-2017-elastography-liver-fibrosis]] unless noted):

- **Fasting, always** — for cACLD *and* CSPH ([[baveno-viii-2026-portal-hypertension]] 1.4). Food intake within **2–3 hours** gives inaccurate readings ([[aga-2017-elastography-liver-fibrosis]]).
- **Probe placement:** intercostal skin, **9th–11th intercostal space**; sampled region **25–65 mm below the skin (M-probe)** or **35–75 mm (XL-probe)**.
- **Minimum 10 valid measurements** → composite score.
- **Adequate quality only if both:** **≥10 validated measurements** *and* **interquartile range (IQR)/median ≤30%**.
- Reportable range **2.5–75 kPa**.
- **Technical limits:** narrow intercostal space; obesity.
- LSM must not drive decisions without confirmed high study quality and adequately trained operators ([[aasld-2023-portal-hypertension]]).
- **Alcohol:** in [[metald|metabolic dysfunction– and alcohol-associated liver disease (MetALD)]] and alcohol-related disease, measure **after ≥2–4 weeks of alcohol reduction or abstinence**; check serum phosphatidylethanol (PEth) or urinary ethyl glucuronide **at the time of the scan**. Stiffness falls substantially **within days** of withdrawal ([[aga-2026-metald]]).

## Thresholds (VCTE)

The full CSPH pathway, the "rule of 5" and treatment decisions live on **[[portal-hypertension]]** (one home); anchors below.

### cACLD

| LSM | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **<10 kPa** | Rules out cACLD (Baveno: absent other clinical/imaging signs); negligible (≤1%) 3-year risk of decompensation and liver-related death | [[aasld-2023-portal-hypertension]]; [[baveno-viii-2026-portal-hypertension]] 1.3, 1.5 |
| **<10 kPa** + clinical/imaging signs of portal hypertension | Suspect **vascular liver disease** | Baveno VIII 1.6 |
| **7–10 kPa** + ongoing liver injury | Monitor case-by-case for progression to cACLD | [[baveno-vii-2022-portal-hypertension]] 2.10 |
| **10–15 kPa** | Suggestive of cACLD | Baveno VIII 1.3 |
| **≥15 kPa** | Rules in cACLD (Baveno VIII: >15 kPa highly suggestive) | AASLD 2023; Baveno VIII 1.3 |

- **Index LSM ≥10 kPa:** repeat it (strong), or complement with another validated non-invasive test (NIT) of advanced fibrosis (weak) (Baveno VIII 1.4). Named serum cut-offs (fibrosis-4 [FIB-4] ≥2.67, enhanced liver fibrosis [ELF] ≥9.8, FibroTest ≥0.58 or ≥0.48 in nonalcoholic fatty liver disease [NAFLD]) are Baveno VII's (2.11), not restated in Baveno VIII.
- **Rule of 5 (10-15-20-25 kPa):** progressively higher relative risk of decompensation and liver-related death, independent of aetiology (Baveno VIII 1.8).

### CSPH

| LSM + platelets | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **<15 kPa + platelets >150,000/mcl** | Essentially rules out CSPH | [[aasld-2024-nilda-portal-htn]] (Figure 1); AASLD 2023 (>150 K/mm³) |
| ↳ Baveno wording | **≤15 kPa + platelets ≥150 ×10⁹/L** rule out CSPH (negative predictive value >90%; level of evidence [LoE] 1, strong) | Baveno VIII 1.15 |
| **<15 kPa + platelets <150,000/mcl** | CSPH uncertain | AASLD 2024 |
| **10–15 kPa + platelets <150,000/mcl**, or **15–25 kPa + platelets >150,000/mcl** | CSPH possible → after excluding other causes of thrombocytopenia: MRE ≥5.1 kPa, spleen stiffness measurement (SSM) ≥40 kPa, [[upper-endoscopy\|esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD)]] (varices indicate CSPH), or HVPG | AASLD 2024 |
| **15–25 kPa** | "Gray zone" — clinical judgement; CSPH more likely with platelets **≤110,000/mcl** | AASLD 2024 |
| **15–19.9 kPa + platelets <110 K/mm³**, or **20–24.9 kPa + platelets <150 K/mm³** | CSPH presumed (positive predictive value >60%) | AASLD 2023 |
| **≥25 kPa** | Rules in CSPH **irrespective of platelet count**, absent confounders (right heart failure, severe hepatic inflammation) | AASLD 2024; AASLD 2023 |

- **≥25 kPa population limits:** validated in hepatitis B/C, alcohol-associated and **non-obese (body mass index [BMI] <30 kg/m²)** NAFLD; positive predictive value falls to **63%** in obesity and NAFLD/nonalcoholic steatohepatitis (AASLD 2023). Baveno VIII 1.16b likewise limits it to virus-/alcohol-related and non-obese metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD)-related cACLD.
- AASLD 2024 **suggests against** using LSM to quantify higher degrees of portal hypertension (HVPG ≥12 mmHg) (Statement 3; conditional, low).
- **Recompensated cirrhosis** (Baveno VIII, all weak): **LSM <10 kPa**, or **LSM <15 kPa + SSM <25 kPa**, may rule out CSPH; **LSM >25 kPa** may rule it in (7.19a–c). Stopping [[nonselective-beta-blockers|carvedilol/conventional nonselective beta-blockers (NSBB)]] requires confirmed CSPH resolution (7.18).

> [!pitfall] Platelet sign differs by guideline
> AASLD rules out CSPH at LSM **<15** kPa with platelets **>150,000/mcl**; Baveno VIII uses **≤15** and **≥150 ×10⁹/L** ([[aasld-2024-nilda-portal-htn]], [[baveno-viii-2026-portal-hypertension]] 1.15).

### Spleen Stiffness and Screening Endoscopy

| SSM | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **≥40 kPa** | Suggests CSPH (SSM field rapidly evolving) | AASLD 2024 |
| **≤46 kPa** | May rule out varices needing treatment (skip endoscopy) in patients who would otherwise need screening by Baveno VI criteria | AASLD 2023 |
| **>55 kPa (100 Hz)** | Diagnostic of CSPH | Baveno VIII 1.16c |
| **<40 kPa** | Spares screening EGD in NSBB-ineligible cACLD | Baveno VIII 1.19 |
| **<25 kPa + LSM <15 kPa** | May rule out CSPH after recompensation | Baveno VIII 7.19b |

- **AASLD 2024 does not address variceal screening.** AASLD 2023 cites the **Baveno VI** criteria for skipping screening: **LSM <20 kPa and platelets ≥150 K/mm³** (Figure 3).
- Baveno VIII 1.19: NSBB-ineligible cACLD → EGD **unless LSM <20 kPa and platelets ≥150 ×10⁹/L, or SSM <40 kPa**. Full pathway on [[portal-hypertension]].
- Baveno VII's SSM pair (<21 kPa rule-out / >50 kPa rule-in, viral cACLD only; 2.21) is not restated in Baveno VIII.

### Special Populations

> [!pitfall] Cut-offs not validated in autoimmune hepatitis
> The ≤15 kPa + platelets ≥150 ×10⁹/L rule-out and ≥25 kPa rule-in were derived in viral, alcohol and MASLD populations: up to **52%** with [[autoimmune-hepatitis|autoimmune hepatitis (AIH)]] and clear portal hypertension had LSM below them; [[thiopurines]] and mycophenolate mofetil lower platelets ([[easl-2025-autoimmune-hepatitis]]).

- **AIH** ([[easl-2025-autoimmune-hepatitis]]): fibrosis accurately assessable only **after 6 months of immunosuppression**; **9 kPa** severe fibrosis, **12.5–16 kPa** cirrhosis; transient elastography recommended for monitoring (LoE 3, strong), serial LSM e.g. **every 12 months**. In AIH-related cirrhosis, EGD for varices needing treatment if **LSM ≥20 kPa or platelets ≤150 ×10⁹/L**.
- **[[porto-sinusoidal-vascular-disorder|Porto-sinusoidal vascular disorder (PSVD)/non-cirrhotic portal fibrosis (NCPF)]]:** LSM-based criteria for variceal screening cannot be applied (Baveno VIII 8.48); **SSM <40 kPa with bilirubin <1 mg/dL** spares endoscopy (8.47).
- **Children:** LSM helps identify those at risk of portal hypertension and monitor progression, but management cannot rely on LSM and/or SSM alone — paediatric [[variceal-upper-gi-bleeding|varices]] cut-offs need validation (Baveno VIII 1.25–1.26).

### Etiology-specific VCTE cutoffs — AGA 2017

Cutoffs for *histologic cirrhosis*, each chosen to **minimize false negatives** — specificity is low and false positives are common, especially in low-prevalence settings ([[aga-2017-elastography-liver-fibrosis]]).

| Clinical question | VCTE cutoff | Pooled sens / spec | Strength · quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cirrhosis in [[hepatitis-c\|chronic hepatitis C]] | **12.5 kPa** | 0.86 / 0.91 | Conditional · low |
| Cirrhosis in [[chronic-hepatitis-b\|chronic hepatitis B]] | **11.0 kPa** | 0.81 / 0.83 | Conditional · low |
| Cirrhosis in chronic [[alcohol-associated-liver-disease\|alcoholic liver disease]] | **12.5 kPa** | 0.95 / 0.71 | Conditional · low |
| Rule out advanced fibrosis (F3–F4) after sustained virologic response (SVR), noncirrhotic hepatitis C virus (HCV) | **9.5 kPa** | 0.78 / 0.86 | Conditional · very low |
| Rule out **high-risk esophageal varices** in suspected compensated cirrhosis | **19.5 kPa** | 0.89 / 0.56 | Conditional · low |
| Detect **CSPH** before elective **nonhepatic surgery** | **17.0 kPa** | 0.83 / 0.52 | Conditional · low |
| Cirrhosis in NAFLD | — | — | No recommendation — knowledge gap |

- **VCTE over nonproprietary serum indices** for cirrhosis: **strong, moderate** in hepatitis C (VCTE 0.89/0.91 vs aspartate aminotransferase-to-platelet ratio index [APRI] 0.77/0.78, FIB-4 0.87/0.91); **conditional, low** in hepatitis B (VCTE 0.86/0.85 vs APRI 0.66/0.74, FIB-4 0.87/0.65).
- **Qualifiers:**
  - 12.5 kPa alcohol cutoff: **chronic** disease only — not acute alcoholic hepatitis.
  - 19.5 kPa varices rule: not for decompensated cirrhosis or compensated cirrhosis with known varices/portal hypertension — those go to endoscopy regardless.
  - A cirrhosis cutoff alone should not drive screening endoscopy; that has its own cutoff (19.5 kPa).
  - Hepatitis C: stiffness frequently falls after viral cure.
- **MRE vs VCTE:** hepatitis C → **VCTE preferred** (conditional, very low; MRE specificity 0.81 vs 0.91). NAFLD at higher risk of cirrhosis → MRE suggested; see [[nafld-masld]].
- Where these overlap the newer AASLD and Baveno cACLD/CSPH thresholds above, the newer thresholds govern; AGA 2017 remains the reference for etiology-specific cirrhosis cutoffs and the pre-operative CSPH question.

## Monitoring & confounders

- **Repeat:** Baveno VIII — NITs for CSPH **every 12 months** in cACLD (1.9). AASLD — annual LSM + platelets may give prognostic information in cACLD without baseline CSPH whose aetiology remains active/uncontrolled (AASLD 2023 Statement 8). AASLD 2024 Statement 4 (ungraded): serial LSM + platelets may be followed to monitor for progression to CSPH in adults with CLD without baseline CSPH whose etiology is active/uncontrolled.
- **The most recent value guides decisions** (Baveno VIII 1.10).
- **Caution with longitudinal change:** AASLD 2024 **suggests caution** using LSM or SSM to detect change in portal hypertension in cirrhosis on treatment or with known portal hypertension (Statement 5; conditional, low). Baveno VIII quantifies meaningful change in the **risk of liver-related events**, not fibrosis stage (2.8):

| Test | Clinically meaningful change | Setting (Baveno VIII) |
|---|---|---|
| **LSM (VCTE)** | **Confirmed ≥30% relative change** | Alcohol-related cACLD (2.3); MASLD-related cACLD (2.9), especially when it crosses 15 kPa |
| **MRE** | **≥20% relative change** | MASLD-related cACLD (2.11) |
| **ELF** | **0.5-unit absolute change** | MASLD (2.12) |
| **pSWE / 2D-SWE** | Likely similar to VCTE; evidence insufficient to recommend | (2.10) |

- **≥10% weight reduction** is associated with a ≥30% LSM reduction in MASLD-related cACLD (2.13). Without CSPH, a confirmed ≥30% reduction may indicate **≥1 stage fibrosis regression** (2.6); adding a ≥0.5-unit ELF reduction improves that assessment (2.7).
- Baveno VII's clinically significant decrease (**≥20% with LSM <20 kPa, or any drop to <10 kPa**; 2.13) is not restated in Baveno VIII.
- **Inaccurate or falsely high readings:** acute hepatitis, alcohol abuse, food within 2–3 hours, congestive heart failure, extrahepatic cholestasis ([[aga-2017-elastography-liver-fibrosis]]). Thresholds calibrate poorly in [[obesity]] and NAFLD, alanine aminotransferase (ALT) >3× upper limit of normal, and [[primary-sclerosing-cholangitis|primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC)]] with dominant stricture ([[aasld-2023-portal-hypertension]]).
