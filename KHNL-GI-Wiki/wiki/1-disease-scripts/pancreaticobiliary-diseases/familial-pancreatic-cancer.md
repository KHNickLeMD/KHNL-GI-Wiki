## Assessment

### Establishing the Diagnosis

- **Familial pancreatic cancer (FPC) kindred** = a family containing **at least a pair of first-degree relatives (FDRs)** with [[pancreatic-cancer|pancreatic cancer]], **without** an association with a known hereditary cancer syndrome ([[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]]).
- **Diagnosis of exclusion** — if a recognized syndrome accounts for the cluster, it is not FPC (see [[#Differential Diagnosis]]).
- **[[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer|ACG 2015]] casts the net wider.** An individual is considered at risk for familial pancreatic adenocarcinoma if **any one** of:
  1. a **known genetic syndrome** associated with pancreatic cancer — hereditary breast–ovarian cancer syndrome, [[fammm-syndrome|FAMMM]], [[peutz-jeghers-syndrome|PJS]], [[lynch-syndrome|Lynch syndrome (LS)]], or another pancreatic-adenocarcinoma risk gene; **OR**
  2. **two relatives** with pancreatic adenocarcinoma, **one of whom is an FDR**; **OR**
  3. **≥3 relatives** with pancreatic cancer; **OR**
  4. a history of [[hereditary-pancreatitis|hereditary pancreatitis]].
- The two definitions diverge on **criterion 2**: ASGE 2022 requires a *pair of FDRs*, ACG 2015 accepts *two relatives, only one of whom is an FDR*. ASGE 2022 is the newer guideline and sets the bar used for the surveillance program below.
- **Inheritance:** segregation analysis of multi-case families shows an **autosomal-dominant** pattern; modeling studies suggest a rare allele as the likely etiology. No causative gene is identified — that is what distinguishes FPC from the named syndromes ([[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]).
- Known mutations explain only ~20% of familial clustering, so a **negative gene panel does not remove a kindred from FPC-based eligibility** for surveillance.
- Up to **10%** of pancreatic cancer patients have a first- or second-degree relative with pancreatic cancer.

### Severity Assessment

Risk is graded by the **number of pancreatic cancer–affected FDRs** in the kindred ([[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]):

| Affected FDRs | Relative risk of pancreatic cancer |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | **4- to 7-fold** |
| ≥3 | **17- to 32-fold** |

- Segregation analysis gives a **32% lifetime risk** by age 85 in FPC families.
- **PancPRO** risk-prediction model refines individual estimates using ages at diagnosis, family size, and relationships between affected members.
- **Family history that does not meet FPC criteria** — ~**2-fold** risk; degree of relatedness and age at onset in the index case do not change it, and screening is **generally not recommended** ([[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]]).

## Differential Diagnosis

*Full comparison of high-risk conditions, modalities, yield, and harms: see [[pancreatic-cancer-screening]].*

Syndromes that must be excluded before calling a kindred FPC — each has its own gene, risk magnitude, and start age:

- [[brca-pathogenic-variants|BRCA1/2]] and PALB2
- [[fammm-syndrome|familial atypical multiple mole melanoma (FAMMM)]] (CDKN2A)
- [[peutz-jeghers-syndrome|Peutz-Jeghers syndrome (PJS)]] (STK11/LKB1)
- [[lynch-syndrome|Lynch syndrome]]
- ATM
- [[hereditary-pancreatitis|Hereditary pancreatitis]]

## Diagnostics

- **Pedigree, not a gene test, makes the diagnosis** — FPC is defined by family structure plus the absence of a syndromic explanation.
- Genetic testing in suspected FPC should analyze **BRCA1/2, CDKN2A, PALB2, and ATM**; add [[peutz-jeghers-syndrome|PJS]]-, [[lynch-syndrome|Lynch]]-, and [[hereditary-pancreatitis|hereditary-pancreatitis]]-associated genes when the personal/family history meets those criteria ([[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]). A positive result reclassifies the kindred out of FPC.
- **Surveillance modality:** [[endoscopic-ultrasound|endoscopic ultrasound (EUS)]], [[mri-mrcp|magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)]], or EUS alternating with MRI — chosen by patient preference and local expertise (conditional, very low quality).

## Therapeutics

**Surveillance program** ([[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]], Rec 6d):

- **Who:** **all first-degree relatives** of affected family members. [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer|ACG 2015]] (Rec 22, conditional/very low) narrows this to FPC-kindred members **with a pancreatic-cancer–affected FDR**, and adds that surveillance should ideally run at an **experienced center, multidisciplinary, under research conditions**.
- **When to start:** age **50**, or **10 years earlier** than the youngest relative with pancreatic cancer — whichever comes first. Both guidelines give the same start age; the rationale for 50 rather than the earlier ages used in higher-risk syndromes is on [[pancreatic-cancer-screening]].
- **Interval:** annual (conditional, very low quality).
- **What a positive finding triggers:** a **pancreatic cystic lesion** found on surveillance in a hereditary-pancreatic-cancer–prone family member carries a higher cancer risk than a sporadic cyst and needs evaluation at an experienced center; the decision to operate is individualized after multidisciplinary assessment ([[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]], Rec 24, conditional/low). Yield, harms, and the full abnormal-finding pathway are on [[pancreatic-cancer-screening]].
- Neither guideline states a **stopping age**, and the interval after a normal baseline examination is undefined.
