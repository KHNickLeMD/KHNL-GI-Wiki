Hereditary pancreatitis is an inherited cause of recurrent acute and [[chronic-pancreatitis|chronic pancreatitis]], most commonly autosomal-dominant from germline PRSS1 (cationic trypsinogen) pathogenic variants, presenting at a young age. It markedly increases lifetime risk of [[pancreatic-cancer|pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma]]. Per [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening|ASGE 2022]], [[pancreatic-cancer-screening|pancreatic cancer surveillance]] in autosomal-dominant hereditary pancreatitis should begin at age 40.
