## Assessment

### Establishing the Diagnosis

- Rare **autosomal dominant** disease, reported in only a few families; the phenotype is **attenuated colonic polyposis** [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]
- Originally described in a large **Ashkenazi Jewish** family with multiple colorectal polyps and cancer [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- **Neither [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer|American College of Gastroenterology (ACG) 2015]] nor the [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis|US Multi-Society Task Force on Colorectal Cancer (USMSTF) 2022]] statement gives diagnostic criteria** — hereditary mixed polyposis syndrome (HMPS) is recognised by its polyp mix in a dominantly inherited kindred, then confirmed genetically
- Original Whitelaw kindred: **13 members diagnosed with [[colorectal-cancer|colorectal cancer (CRC)]]**, **23 with multiple polyps of several histologic types** (adenomatous, hyperplastic, juvenile) [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]
- Polyp spectrum across affected patients: mixed juvenile–adenomatous polyps, plus adenomatous, hyperplastic, serrated adenomas, mixed hyperplastic–adenomatous polyps and adenocarcinomas [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- Age at onset: **mean age of polyp occurrence 28 years** in one family [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]; **polyposis starts in the late 20s** in the largest series (4 families, 16 affected members) [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]

> [!pearl] One polyp, several histologies — that is the diagnosis
> The characteristic lesion is a **single polyp with an admixture of variable histologies** — adenomatous, hyperplastic, juvenile and mixed; USMSTF Table 2 describes it as pathologically mixed with features of adenoma, hyperplastic polyps and inflammatory polyps ([[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]).
> HMPS may be misdiagnosed as [[juvenile-polyposis-syndrome|juvenile polyposis syndrome (JPS)]] or [[serrated-polyposis-syndrome|serrated polyposis syndrome (SPS)]], and vice versa ([[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]).

**Genetics:**

- Linked to a locus on chromosome **15q13.3–q14**, which includes the **CRAC1** gene, in a number of families — the etiology nonetheless remained elusive [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- Associated with **large duplications of the promoter region or the entire *GREM1* gene**; a duplication **40 kb upstream** of the *GREM1* locus was found in two individuals with HMPS [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]] [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]
- These duplications **increase GREM1 expression**, which **inhibits the bone morphogenetic protein (BMP) pathway** → predisposition to multiple colorectal polyps [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]
- The promoter duplication was found in **0.7% of Ashkenazi Jews in Israel who met clinical criteria for [[lynch-syndrome|Lynch syndrome]]** [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]
- **The underlying genetic basis of the majority of HMPS families is unknown** [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]

### Severity Assessment

- **Colon is the only organ listed at risk.** USMSTF 2022 Table 3 grades the colorectal risk only as **"increased"**, against a **4.3%** general-population lifetime risk of colon cancer (Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results [SEER] 1975–2017). No penetrance figure, lifetime risk percentage or mean age at diagnosis is given [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]
- **Whether extraintestinal neoplasia is a risk is unknown** — there are not enough data, and neither guideline specifies any extraintestinal surveillance [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]] [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]

## Differential Diagnosis

*Workup: see [[colorectal-polyposis]].*

- **JPS** — polyps are uniformly juvenile (inflammatory, pathologically indistinct from inflammatory polyps); *SMAD4* or *BMPR1A*
- **SPS** — defined by serrated polyp count and distribution (World Health Organization [WHO] criteria); no proven genetic etiology
- **[[familial-adenomatous-polyposis|familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP)]] / attenuated FAP (AFAP)** — purely adenomatous; *APC*
- **[[mutyh-associated-polyposis|MUTYH-associated polyposis]]** — autosomal **recessive**; adenomas ± serrated polyps; biallelic *MUTYH*
- **Lynch syndrome** — few polyps, mismatch repair (MMR) deficiency; relevant because the *GREM1* duplication is found in a small fraction of Ashkenazi kindreds **meeting Lynch clinical criteria** with no MMR variant

## Diagnostics

- **[[colonoscopy]] with [[polypectomy]] and histology** of multiple polyps — suspect HMPS when **more than one polyp histology** occurs in the same patient or kindred
- **Who gets a genetic evaluation** — USMSTF 2022 uses one trigger across the hamartomatous polyposis syndromes: **≥2 lifetime hamartomatous polyps**, a **family history of hamartomatous polyps**, or **a cancer associated with a hamartomatous polyposis syndrome in a first- or second-degree relative**. Genetic testing, when indicated, is by **multigene panel** *(strong recommendation, low quality of evidence)* [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]
- ***GREM1*** is one of the genes in the **minimum multigene panel** specified for evaluating adenomatous polyposis [[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]]
- **Genetic testing for *GREM1* mutation and expression** *might be considered* in families with **adenomatous and hamartomatous polyposis in which an etiology cannot be determined** [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]

## Therapeutics

- **Begin colonoscopic surveillance in the late 20s** — the age at which polyposis starts in the largest series [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]
- **Optimal surveillance interval is unknown** — there are not enough data to set one [[aga-2022-hamartomatous-polyposis]]
- Management "from what is now known should probably be **similar to that for FAP**, depending on the polyp number, size, and histology" [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]] — the polyp-burden thresholds that drive endoscopic clearance vs colectomy are on [[familial-adenomatous-polyposis|the FAP page]]
