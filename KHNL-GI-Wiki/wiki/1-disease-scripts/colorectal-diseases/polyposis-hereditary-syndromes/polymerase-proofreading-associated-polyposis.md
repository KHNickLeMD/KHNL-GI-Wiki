## Assessment

### Establishing the Diagnosis

- **Autosomal dominant**, caused by germline pathogenic variants (GPVs) in the **exonuclease (proofreading) domains** of ***POLD1*** or ***POLE***, which encode the catalytic subunits of DNA polymerases delta and epsilon ([[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]])
- Loss of proofreading → tumours with a **high mutational burden and a distinct mutational spectrum** (hypermutated, often ultramutated)
- **Rare:** GPVs detected in **0.3%–0.7%** of 2,813 unrelated probands tested for colorectal cancer (CRC) and polyposis
- *POLE* and *POLD1* sit on the **minimum multigene panel** for adenomatous polyposis — see [[colorectal-polyposis]] for the full gene list and the cumulative-adenoma thresholds that trigger testing

### Severity Assessment

From a review of 132 cases — 105 *POLE*, 27 *POLD1* ([[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]]):

| | ***POLE*** | ***POLD1*** |
|---|---|---|
| **CRC** | **70.5%**, median age **44.5 yr** | **44%**, median age **41 yr** |
| **Colorectal adenomas** | **74.3%** had **1–100** adenomas (median 12), median age 36 yr | **66.6%** had **2–70** adenomas (median 13), median age 40 yr |
| **Duodenal adenomas** | **15.2%**, median age 51 yr | **7.4%**, median age 55 yr |
| **Duodenal cancer** | **9.5%** | Not reported |
| **Endometrial cancer** | **30%–53%** | **12%** |
| **Ovarian cancer** | **9%–12%** | — |
| **Other** | Brain (astrocytoma, glioblastoma), breast, pancreas, skin | Brain, breast, pancreas, skin |

- Duodenal adenomas were reported in **more than 50%** of patients who actually underwent upper gastrointestinal (GI) endoscopy — the 15.2% / 7.4% figures reflect how few were scoped
- The colorectal phenotype in [[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]] Table 5 is **30–100 adenomas** with small-intestinal duodenal adenomas

## Differential Diagnosis

*Workup: see [[colorectal-polyposis]].*

- **[[familial-adenomatous-polyposis]] / AFAP** — *APC*; the commonest explanation for an oligo-adenomatous phenotype
- **[[mutyh-associated-polyposis|MUTYH-associated polyposis]]** — biallelic *MUTYH*, autosomal recessive
- **[[lynch-syndrome]]** — the endometrial-plus-colorectal pattern overlaps; distinguished by the mismatch repair genes on panel testing and by tumour mismatch-repair immunohistochemistry
- **[[colonic-polyposis-of-unknown-etiology]]** — the label when the whole panel, including *POLE* and *POLD1*, is negative
- Other rare recessive adenomatous syndromes — ***NTHL1*** tumor syndrome, ***AXIN2***-, ***MLH3***-, ***MSH3***-associated polyposis, ***MBD4***-associated neoplasia syndrome; summarised on [[colorectal-polyposis]]

## Diagnostics

> [!pearl] Refer to an expert centre
> PPAP, *NTHL1* tumor syndrome, *AXIN2*-, *MSH3*- and *MLH3*-associated polyposis and *MBD4*-associated neoplasia syndrome should all be **referred to an expert centre** — the syndromes are rare, the cancer risks are still evolving, and management is multispecialty ([[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]] KC 5).

Surveillance proposed by [[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]] (Figure 6) for the whole group of *POLE*, *POLD1*, *NTHL1*, *AXIN2*, *MLH3*, *MSH3* and *MBD4* syndromes — explicitly a **"standing on the side of safety"** suggestion, since publication and ascertainment bias preclude understanding the true spectrum of risk:

| Test | When to start | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| **[[colonoscopy]]** | **Age 25–30 yr**, or **5 years before the youngest age at diagnosis of adenomatous polyposis** and **10 years before the youngest CRC** in an affected relative — whichever is earlier | **Every 1–3 yr** |
| **[[upper-endoscopy\|EGD]]** — ***POLE* and *POLD1* only** | **Age 25–30 yr**, alongside a colonoscopy | By **Spigelman stage** of duodenal polyposis (table on [[familial-adenomatous-polyposis]]) |
| **Post-colectomy lower endoscopy** | After surgery | **Annually** |

- **Women with a *POLD1* GPV:** pelvic ultrasound and selective endometrial biopsy may be considered, given the endometrial and possible brain tumour association ([[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]])
- No thyroid ultrasound is recommended for this group

## Therapeutics

- Colorectal management follows the adenomatous-polyposis principles on [[familial-adenomatous-polyposis]]: resect polyps ≥10 mm without features of submucosal invasion, excise polyps ≥4 mm where feasible, and move to colectomy for CRC or an endoscopically unmanageable burden
- Duodenal disease is staged and managed by **Spigelman stage**, as in FAP
- **Larger cohorts are required** before management recommendations for these disorders will evolve; no chemoprevention recommendation is made
