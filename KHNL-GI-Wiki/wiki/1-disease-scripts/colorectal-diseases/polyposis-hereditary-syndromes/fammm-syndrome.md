## Assessment

- Hereditary cancer syndrome classically associated with germline **CDKN2A** pathogenic variants; multiple atypical nevi, melanoma, and markedly increased risk of [[pancreatic-cancer|pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma]].

### Establishing the Diagnosis

- **Germline testing for CDKN2A is recommended** in the hereditary-pancreatic-cancer testing panel (alongside BRCA1/2, PALB2, ATM). [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- Familial atypical multiple mole melanoma (FAMMM) is one of the **known genetic syndromes** that by itself satisfies the criteria for being "at risk for familial pancreatic adenocarcinoma". [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- None of this page's guidelines states the clinical (nevus-count / family-history) diagnostic criteria for FAMMM; each addresses only its pancreatic cancer risk and assumes the syndrome diagnosis has already been made.

### Severity Assessment

- **Pancreatic cancer relative risk (RR) 13–39-fold** vs the general population (CDKN2A). [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- RR ≥10 places FAMMM in the **very-high-risk** tier, which is why screening starts **2 standard deviations (SD)** below the mean age of pancreatic cancer diagnosis vs 1 SD for lower-risk conditions. [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]]
- RR for every other hereditary pancreatic-cancer syndrome, with each condition's start age and family-history requirement, is tabulated on [[pancreatic-cancer-screening]].
- No melanoma risk estimate is given by any of these guidelines.

## Differential Diagnosis

*Surveillance program for all of these: see [[pancreatic-cancer-screening]].*

- **[[peutz-jeghers-syndrome]]** — STK11; the other very-high-risk (RR ≥10) pancreatic syndrome; hamartomatous polyps + mucocutaneous pigmentation rather than atypical nevi.
- **[[familial-pancreatic-cancer|FPC]]** — ≥2 first-degree relatives with pancreatic cancer **without** an association with a known hereditary cancer syndrome. [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]]
- **[[brca-pathogenic-variants|BRCA1/2]] and PALB2/ATM carriers** — lower RR; different age-to-start.
- **[[hereditary-pancreatitis]]** — recurrent pancreatitis phenotype; highest single-syndrome RR.

## Diagnostics

- **[[endoscopic-ultrasound|Endoscopic ultrasound (EUS)]] may be preferred** as the initial screening test in FAMMM, precisely because it is one of the very-high-risk conditions; magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) alone, or EUS alternating with MRI, are the permitted alternatives. [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]]
- American College of Gastroenterology (ACG) 2015 surveillance for hereditary pancreatic cancer generally: **EUS and/or [[mri-mrcp|MRI]] of the pancreas, annually**, at experienced centers, ideally under research conditions *(Rec 22–23; conditional / very low)* [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- Modality-selection rules and EUS/MRI technique (linear-array vs radial, contrast, magnet strength) are tabulated on [[pancreatic-cancer-screening]].
- [[pancreatic-cysts|Cystic pancreatic lesions]] found on surveillance require evaluation at a center experienced with high-risk individuals *(Rec 24; conditional / low)*. [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]

## Therapeutics

- Management is **surveillance**, not treatment of the syndrome itself.
- **Who qualifies: any CDKN2A carrier — no pancreatic cancer in the family is required.** The first-/second-degree-relative requirement that ACG 2015 attaches to BRCA1/2, PALB2, ATM and Lynch carriers does **not** apply to FAMMM, which is surveilled on carrier status alone *(Rec 22)*; [[caps-2020-pancreatic-surveillance|International Cancer of the Pancreas Screening (CAPS) Consortium 2020]] agrees (surveillance regardless of family history, 77% agreement, grade 1 "definitely do it"). [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- **Age to start: 40**, or 10 years earlier than the youngest relative with pancreatic cancer *(American Society for Gastrointestinal Endoscopy (ASGE) 2022 Table 1e; no grade attached)* [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]] — CAPS 2020 also puts CDKN2A at 40. Contrast: the generic hereditary-pancreatic-cancer start age is 50 *(ACG 2015 Rec 23)*.
- **Interval: annual** *(ASGE 2022 Rec 4; conditional, very low)* — the same annual cadence ACG 2015 Rec 23 gives. [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]]
- **Shortened interval, specific to CDKN2A.** A CDKN2A p16 carrier with a newly detected concerning finding *not* going to surgery — **mild main-pancreatic-duct dilation, or a duct stricture without a mass** — is re-imaged at **3–6 months** instead of 12 (98.5% agreement, grade 2 "probably do it"). [[caps-2020-pancreatic-surveillance]]
- Melanoma surveillance is not addressed by any of these guidelines; all three scope FAMMM solely as a pancreatic cancer risk condition.
- Detected lesions are managed as [[pancreatic-cancer|pancreatic cancer]]; the thresholds that decide when a surveillance-detected lesion is resected are tabulated on [[pancreatic-cancer-screening]].
