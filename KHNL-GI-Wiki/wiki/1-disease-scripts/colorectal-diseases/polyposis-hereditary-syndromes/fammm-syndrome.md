## Assessment

- Hereditary cancer syndrome classically associated with germline **CDKN2A** pathogenic variants; multiple atypical nevi, melanoma, and markedly increased risk of [[pancreatic-cancer|pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma]].

### Establishing the Diagnosis

- **When to refer for genetic assessment:** a patient with pancreatic cancer who has a **personal or family history of melanoma, or multiple dysplastic nevi**. [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- **Germline testing for CDKN2A is recommended** in the hereditary-pancreatic-cancer testing panel (alongside BRCA1/2, PALB2, ATM). [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- Familial atypical multiple mole melanoma (FAMMM) is one of the **known genetic syndromes** that by itself satisfies the criteria for being "at risk for familial pancreatic adenocarcinoma". [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- American College of Gastroenterology (ACG) 2015, American Society for Gastrointestinal Endoscopy (ASGE) 2022 and the International Cancer of the Pancreas Screening (CAPS) Consortium 2020 give no nevus-count or family-history criteria for diagnosing FAMMM itself — each addresses only its pancreatic cancer risk and assumes the diagnosis has already been made.

### Severity Assessment

- **Pancreatic cancer relative risk (RR) 13–39-fold** vs the general population (CDKN2A). [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- RR ≥10 places FAMMM in the **very-high-risk** tier, which is why screening starts **2 standard deviations (SD)** below the mean age of pancreatic cancer diagnosis vs 1 SD for lower-risk conditions. [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]]
- **16% of CDKN2A p16-Leiden carriers who developed pancreatic cancer were diagnosed before age 45** — the stated basis for starting surveillance at 40. [[caps-2020-pancreatic-surveillance]]
- Neither a melanoma risk estimate nor melanoma surveillance guidance is given by ACG 2015, ASGE 2022 or CAPS 2020; all three scope FAMMM solely as a pancreatic cancer risk condition.

## Differential Diagnosis

*Surveillance program for all of these: see [[pancreatic-cancer-screening]].*

- **[[peutz-jeghers-syndrome]]** — STK11; the other very-high-risk (RR ≥10) pancreatic syndrome; hamartomatous polyps + mucocutaneous pigmentation rather than atypical nevi.
- **[[familial-pancreatic-cancer|familial pancreatic cancer (FPC)]]** — ≥2 first-degree relatives with pancreatic cancer **without** an association with a known hereditary cancer syndrome. [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]]
- **[[brca-pathogenic-variants|BRCA1/2]] and PALB2/ATM carriers** — lower RR; different age-to-start.
- **[[hereditary-pancreatitis]]** — recurrent pancreatitis phenotype; RR 53-fold. [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]

## Diagnostics

- **[[endoscopic-ultrasound|Endoscopic ultrasound (EUS)]] may be preferred** as the initial screening test in FAMMM, precisely because it is one of the very-high-risk conditions; magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) alone, or EUS alternating with MRI, are the permitted alternatives *(Rec 2; conditional / very low)*. [[asge-2022-pancreatic-cancer-screening]]
- ACG 2015 surveillance for hereditary pancreatic cancer generally: **EUS and/or [[mri-mrcp|MRI]] of the pancreas, annually**, at experienced centers, ideally under research conditions *(Rec 22–23; conditional / very low)* [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]
- [[pancreatic-cysts|Cystic pancreatic lesions]] found on surveillance require evaluation at a center experienced with high-risk individuals; when to operate is individualized after multidisciplinary assessment *(Rec 24; conditional / low)*. [[acg-2015-hereditary-gi-cancer]]

## Therapeutics

- Management is **surveillance** of the pancreas, not treatment of the syndrome itself. Detected lesions are managed as [[pancreatic-cancer|pancreatic cancer]].

| Decision | Rule in FAMMM |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Any CDKN2A carrier — **no pancreatic cancer in the family is required**. The first-/second-degree-relative requirement ACG 2015 attaches to BRCA1/2, PALB2, ATM and [[lynch-syndrome\|Lynch syndrome]] carriers does not apply here *(Rec 22)*. CAPS 2020 agrees — regardless of family history **77% agreement, grade 1** ("definitely do it"), rising to **99%, grade 1** with ≥1 affected first-degree relative; its eligibility table counts only CDKN2A mutations that change the **p16** protein, while its summary table says all germline CDKN2A carriers |
| **Age to start** | **40**, or 10 years earlier than the youngest relative with pancreatic cancer *(ASGE 2022 Rec 6(e), Table 1; no grade attached)*. CAPS 2020 also puts CDKN2A at 40, as a literature-based recommendation — its vote on the age to start reached no consensus. The generic hereditary-pancreatic-cancer start age is 50 *(ACG 2015 Rec 23)* |
| **Interval** | **Annual** *(ASGE 2022 Rec 4; conditional / very low)* — the same cadence as ACG 2015 Rec 23, which adds that frequency depends on the severity of observed lesions and the need for pathologic examination *(Table 10)* |
| **Shortened interval — CDKN2A-specific** | **3–6 months** when a p16 carrier has a newly detected concerning finding *not* going to surgery: **mild main pancreatic duct dilation, or a duct stricture without a mass** *(CAPS 2020; 98.5% agreement, grade 2 "probably do it")* |

*Shared with every other high-risk condition and tabulated on [[pancreatic-cancer-screening]]: relative risks and start ages for the other syndromes, modality selection and EUS/MRI technique, and the thresholds that decide when a surveillance-detected lesion is resected.*
