## Assessment

### Establishing the Diagnosis

> [!pearl] A positive label, not a failed work-up
> **≥10 cumulative lifetime colorectal adenomas with negative germline multigene panel testing (MGPT)** meets criteria for CPUE ([[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]] key concept (KC) 6). It carries its own colonoscopy, [[upper-endoscopy|esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD)]] and thyroid surveillance — the patient is not returned to average-risk screening.

- **The panel must have covered the minimum 17-gene set** (listed on [[colorectal-polyposis]]). A negative *APC*/*MUTYH*-only test, a panel missing any minimum gene, or testing done before 2013 is an **incomplete work-up, not CPUE** — further evaluation and referral to a hereditary-cancer provider or centre are advised (KC 3)
- **≥20 adenomas with uninformative MGPT** → consider referral for ***APC* mosaicism** testing, case by case. **Where access and expertise for mosaicism evaluation are limited, manage as CPUE** (KC 4)
- **Count defining the label:** ACG 2026 applies CPUE to **>10–99 cumulative lifetime adenomas** with negative germline testing that optimally includes colorectal cancer (CRC) and polyposis genes; its surveillance guidance also covers patients with **≥100** adenomas and negative testing
- Other terms in use: **multiple colorectal adenomas**, and **idiopathic adenomatous polyposis (IAP)**, which growing expert consensus supports as the preferred acronym
- **Common:** 3 European studies of 27,689 patients having colonoscopy for a positive fecal test in population-screening programs (ages 61–71 yr) — **2%–3.2% had ≥10 adenomas** on the examination. In one, >90% of those with ≥20 adenomas underwent MGPT and **no germline pathogenic variant (GPV) was identified in 96.7%** of those tested

### Severity Assessment

**Cumulative lifetime adenoma count is the stratifier** — 10–19, 20–99 and ≥100 adenomas set the surveillance intervals ([[#Surveillance — Patient]]), what relatives are offered ([[#Surveillance — First-Degree Relatives]]) and the risk of upper gastrointestinal (GI) neoplasia ([[#Upper GI Yield]]).

Polyposis **progression should be expected, and colorectal surgery is commonly needed**:

| Cohort | CRC | Progression | Colorectal surgery |
|---|---|---|---|
| **83 patients**, 10–99 cumulative adenomas at age ≥25 yr, no *APC* or biallelic *MUTYH* GPV (2 hereditary registries) | **19.3% on the baseline [[colonoscopy]]** (median age 58.3 yr, range 35.8–72.7); metachronous CRC in 3 of those with baseline CRC | Adenoma count rose in **23.9%** of the 71 under surveillance over a median 3.9 yr | **17 patients**, for polyp burden or size |
| **27 patients**, mean 35 ± 35.9 adenomas, negative *APC*/*MUTYH* | **7.4%** (one at age 24 on the first colonoscopy) | **3-fold more adenomas at 1 yr and 4-fold at 2 yr** despite resection of all polyps | **67%**, the majority for endoscopically unmanageable polyposis |
| **70 patients**, ≥10–99 cumulative adenomas | **5.7%**, mean age 66 yr | **2.3 adenomas/yr** over 12.3 yr of surveillance | **10%**, for advanced neoplasia or endoscopically unmanageable polyp burden |

*Natural history of CPUE in the three cohorts the guideline relies on*
*data from 83, 27 and 70 patients with 10–99 cumulative colorectal adenomas and negative (or APC/MUTYH-negative) germline testing, cited in ACG 2026*
*[[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis|ACG Clinical Guideline: Diagnosis and Management of Adenomatous Colorectal Polyposis Syndromes (2026)]]*
*CRC = colorectal cancer · GPV = germline pathogenic variant*

- All three are **retrospective**, with ascertainment bias, small cohorts, inconsistent use of MGPT and no mosaicism testing
- **Extracolonic risk is not fully established** — what is known is in [[#Upper GI Yield]]

## Differential Diagnosis

*Workup: see [[colorectal-polyposis]].*

- **[[familial-adenomatous-polyposis|Familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP)]] / attenuated FAP (AFAP) from *APC* mosaicism** — the commonest explanation for a CPUE phenotype; mosaicism accounts for up to **63%** of unexplained FAP with >20 colorectal adenomas. In a systematic review of mosaic patients, **65% had an attenuated (20–100 adenoma) phenotype**, 30% a classic polyposis phenotype and 5% no adenomas
- **[[mutyh-associated-polyposis|MUTYH-associated polyposis]]** and the rarer recessive syndromes — excluded only if the panel included *MUTYH*, *NTHL1*, *MSH3*, *MLH3*, *MBD4*
- **[[polymerase-proofreading-associated-polyposis|Polymerase proofreading-associated polyposis (PPAP)]] (*POLE*/*POLD1*)** and ***AXIN2*-associated polyposis** — likewise panel-dependent
- **Therapy-associated polyposis (TAP)** — survivors of childhood and young-adult cancer exposed to chemotherapy (particularly alkylating agents) and/or radiotherapy. In one multi-institutional study, **53% of 34 patients with TAP and negative MGPT had ≥10 cumulative adenomas**
- **[[serrated-polyposis-syndrome|Serrated polyposis syndrome (SPS)]]** — adenomas, including advanced adenomas, are detected in up to **80%** of SPS patients, and in one observational study of 115 SPS cases **10% had >10 cumulative lifetime adenomas**
- **[[lynch-syndrome]]** — few polyps; excluded by the mismatch repair genes on the panel

Germline MGPT for an adenomatous polyposis syndrome is warranted in TAP and SPS patients who meet established testing criteria (≥10–20 cumulative adenomas).

## Diagnostics

### Surveillance — Patient

| Cumulative adenomas | Colonoscopy | EGD | Thyroid ultrasound |
|---|---|---|---|
| **≥20** | **Every 1–3 yr**, interval by polyp size, number and histology; lengthen as findings allow (KC 29) | **At diagnosis**, then by **Spigelman stage**, or by the features of gastric polyposis / ampullary adenoma if present; lengthen if numerous EGDs are normal (KC 30) | **Offer baseline at diagnosis**; surveillance ultrasound could be considered per the FAP and AFAP recommendations; cease if multiple examinations are normal (KC 31) |
| **10–19** | By polyp size, number and histology ([[colonoscopy-surveillance\|US Multi-Society Task Force (USMSTF) intervals]]) | **At diagnosis**, then by Spigelman stage; cease if multiple examinations are normal | Not addressed |

*Endoscopic and thyroid surveillance in CPUE, by adenoma count*
*from Figure 6 and key concepts 29–31 of ACG 2026*
*[[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis|ACG Clinical Guideline: Diagnosis and Management of Adenomatous Colorectal Polyposis Syndromes (2026)]]*
*EGD = esophagogastroduodenoscopy · FAP = familial adenomatous polyposis · AFAP = attenuated FAP · KC = key concept · USMSTF = US Multi-Society Task Force on Colorectal Cancer*

- The **Spigelman staging table and its surveillance intervals** live on [[familial-adenomatous-polyposis]]
- **No prospective evidence informs any of these intervals.** They rest on the retrospective data above; the spectrum of cancer risk in CPUE is **not as high as FAP** and varies with lifestyle and environmental exposures as well as high-penetrance genes

### Surveillance — First-Degree Relatives

| Adenoma count in the patient with CPUE | First-degree relative (FDR) |
|---|---|
| **≥100** | Incept colonoscopy and EGD per the **FAP** recommendations ([[familial-adenomatous-polyposis]]) |
| **20–99** | Incept colonoscopy and EGD per the **AFAP** recommendations ([[familial-adenomatous-polyposis]]) |
| **10–19** | Colonoscopy at **10 yr younger than the youngest age of CPUE diagnosis, or age 40 yr, whichever is earlier**. Polyps present → interval by size, number and histology per [[usmstf-2020-followup-colonoscopy\|USMSTF]]. **Colonoscopy normal → consider surveillance in 5 yr**, then at an interval based on the negative findings and family history |

*Upper gastrointestinal neoplasia in reported CPUE series*
*data from four retrospective series of patients with ≥10 cumulative colorectal adenomas and negative germline testing, cited in ACG 2026*
*[[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis|ACG Clinical Guideline: Diagnosis and Management of Adenomatous Colorectal Polyposis Syndromes (2026)]]*

- **Yield tracks the colorectal adenoma count** — ampullary adenomas appeared only above 100 colorectal adenomas, and in the Italian registry every duodenal adenoma and both duodenal cancers occurred at ≥50
- EGD was omitted in a large share of patients in each series (65% in the Italian registry), so the **utility of EGD in CPUE with fewer than 50 adenomas has not been established**
- Thyroid ultrasound in 75 patients with CPUE detected **no thyroid cancer**

## Therapeutics

- **Endoscopic management is the default** — in adenomatous colorectal polyposis, perform [[polypectomy|endoscopic resection]] of colorectal polyps **≥10 mm** that have no features of submucosal invasion, and excise polyps **≥4 mm** where feasible (KC 10)
- **Colectomy is indicated for CRC or for endoscopically unmanageable polyposis** (KC 29). In the cohorts above, 10%–67% eventually required colorectal surgery
- **Choice and timing of operation are individualised** — CRC risk, rectal involvement, symptoms, desmoid risk, surgical morbidity including functional outcomes, and psychosocial impact; involve a multidisciplinary team with polyposis expertise early (KC 12)
- **Chemoprevention:** no CPUE-specific recommendation is made, and the sulindac and celecoxib trial evidence is in FAP ([[familial-adenomatous-polyposis]])

> [!pitfall] Record the polyp burden at every examination
> Document the **estimated polyp number and range of sizes, colon and rectum reported separately**, at baseline and at each follow-up colonoscopy ([[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]] KC 9). Serial counts are what make the timing of surgery decidable — and in CPUE the count is expected to rise.
