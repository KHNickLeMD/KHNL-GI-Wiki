## Assessment

### Establishing the Diagnosis

> [!pearl] CPUE is a positive label, not a failed work-up
> **≥10 cumulative lifetime colorectal adenomas with negative germline multigene panel testing (MGPT) meets criteria for colonic polyposis of unknown etiology (CPUE)** ([[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]] KC 6). It carries its own colonoscopy, [[upper-endoscopy|esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD)]] and thyroid surveillance — the patient is not returned to average-risk screening.

- The panel must have covered the **minimum gene set** ([[colorectal-polyposis]]); a negative *APC*/*MUTYH*-only test does not qualify someone as CPUE — that is an incomplete work-up ([[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]] KC 3)
- **≥20 adenomas with uninformative MGPT** → consider referral for ***APC* mosaicism** testing, case-by-case. **Where access and expertise are limited, manage as CPUE** (KC 4)
- Other terms in use: **multiple colorectal adenomas**, and **idiopathic adenomatous polyposis (IAP)**, which growing expert consensus supports as the preferred acronym. ACG 2026 uses CPUE for **>10–99 cumulative lifetime adenomas** with negative germline testing including colorectal cancer (CRC) and polyposis genes
- Common: in three European population-screening studies (27,689 colonoscopies, ages 61–71), **2%–3.2% of patients had ≥10 adenomas** on the examination. In one, **>90% of those with ≥20 adenomas had MGPT and no germline pathogenic variant (GPV)** — 96.7% of those tested

### Severity Assessment

The polyposis **progresses**, and surgery is commonly needed ([[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]]):

- Two hereditary registries, 83 patients with 10–99 cumulative adenomas at age ≥25 yr: **CRC on the baseline [[colonoscopy]] in 19.3%** (median age 58.3 yr). Over a median 3.9 yr of surveillance, adenoma count rose in **23.9%**; colorectal surgery was performed in **17 of 71 (24%)**; metachronous CRC occurred in **3 of the 16** who had CRC at baseline
- 27 patients with a mean of 35 ± 35.9 adenomas and negative *APC*/*MUTYH*: **3-fold increase in adenomas at 1 year and 4-fold at 2 years despite resection of all polyps**; 7.4% had CRC; **colorectal surgery was required in 67%**
- 70 patients with ≥10–99 adenomas: **2.3 adenomas/year** over 12.3 yr of surveillance; CRC in 5.7% at mean age 66 yr; **10% required colorectal surgery** for advanced neoplasia or endoscopically unmanageable burden
- **Extracolonic risk is not fully established.** In 83 patients having EGD, Spigelman stage I and II duodenal polyposis was found in **9.6%** (median age 58 yr) and one 71-year-old (1.2%) had an antral gastric cancer. In 128 patients with ≥10 adenomas and negative MGPT, **7% had gastroduodenal neoplasia** (mean age 58 yr); duodenal adenomas were found in **33% of those with ≥100 colorectal adenomas**, 2.7% at 20–99 and 4.4% at 10–19. **Ampullary adenomas were identified only in patients with ≥100 colorectal adenomas**

## Differential Diagnosis

*Workup: see [[colorectal-polyposis]].*

- **[[familial-adenomatous-polyposis]] / AFAP** — ***APC* mosaicism** is the commonest explanation for a CPUE-like phenotype; it accounts for up to **63%** of unexplained familial adenomatous polyposis (FAP) with >20 adenomas, and 65% of mosaic patients have an attenuated (20–100 adenoma) phenotype
- **[[mutyh-associated-polyposis|MUTYH-associated polyposis]]** and the rarer recessive syndromes — excluded only if the panel included *MUTYH*, *NTHL1*, *MSH3*, *MLH3*, *MBD4*
- **[[polymerase-proofreading-associated-polyposis|PPAP (*POLE* / *POLD1*)]]**, ***AXIN2*-associated polyposis** — likewise panel-dependent
- **Therapy-associated polyposis (TAP)** — survivors of childhood and young-adult cancer exposed to chemotherapy (particularly alkylating agents) and/or radiotherapy. In one multi-institutional study **53% of 34 patients with TAP and negative MGPT had ≥10 cumulative adenomas**. Adenomas, including advanced adenomas, are detected in up to **80%** of patients with serrated polyposis syndrome
- **[[serrated-polyposis-syndrome]]** — in one observational study **10% of SPS patients had >10 cumulative lifetime adenomas**. Germline MGPT for an adenomatous polyposis syndrome is warranted in TAP and SPS patients who meet the usual testing criteria (≥10–20 cumulative adenomas)
- **[[lynch-syndrome]]** — few polyps; excluded by mismatch repair genes on the panel

## Diagnostics

| Phenotype | Colonoscopy | EGD | Thyroid ultrasound |
|---|---|---|---|
| **≥20 cumulative adenomas** | **Every 1–3 yr**, interval by polyp size, number and histology (KC 29). Lengthen as findings allow | **At diagnosis**, then by **Spigelman stage** or by the features of gastric polyposis / ampullary adenomas, if present (KC 30). Lengthen if numerous EGDs are normal | **Offer at diagnosis**; surveillance ultrasound could be considered per the FAP/AFAP recommendations (KC 31). Cease if multiple examinations are normal |
| **10–19 cumulative adenomas** | By polyp size, number and histology | **At diagnosis**, then follow per Spigelman stage; cease if multiple examinations are normal | Not specified |

*Source: [[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]] KC 29–31 and Figure 6. The Spigelman staging table lives on [[familial-adenomatous-polyposis]].*

**First-degree relatives** ([[acg-2026-adenomatous-polyposis]]):

- FDRs of a patient with CPUE and **20–99 adenomas** → start colonoscopy and EGD per the **AFAP** recommendations; **≥100 adenomas** → per the **FAP** recommendations
- FDRs of a patient with CPUE and **10–19 adenomas** → start colonoscopy at **10 years younger than the youngest age of CPUE diagnosis, or age 40, whichever is earlier**. Polyps present → interval by size, number and histology per [[usmstf-2020-followup-colonoscopy|USMSTF]] recommendations. **Colonoscopy normal → consider surveillance in 5 years**, then at an interval based on the negative findings and family history

- **There is no prospective evidence informing any of these intervals.** They are based on the limited observational data above, and the spectrum of cancer risk in CPUE is **not as high as FAP** and varies with lifestyle and environmental exposures as well as genes
- The utility of EGD in CPUE patients with **fewer than 50 adenomas** has not been established — in one single-centre study of 150 patients without *APC*/*MUTYH* GPVs, duodenal adenomas were found in 23% and ampullary adenomas in 7%, but data are limited by selective EGD use

## Therapeutics

- **Endoscopic management is the default** — resect colorectal polyps ≥10 mm without features of submucosal invasion, and excise polyps ≥4 mm where feasible, as in [[familial-adenomatous-polyposis]]
- **Colectomy is indicated for CRC, or for endoscopically unmanageable polyposis** (KC 29). In the cohorts above, 10%–67% eventually required colorectal surgery
- Progression of polyposis **should be expected**; serial documentation of estimated polyp number and size range at each examination is what makes the surgical decision possible
- No chemoprevention recommendation is made for CPUE
