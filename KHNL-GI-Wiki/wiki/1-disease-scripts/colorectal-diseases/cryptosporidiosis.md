Immunocompromised patients (HIV/AIDS, transplant) are at highest risk for severe and chronic disease. Oocysts resist standard chlorination. [[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]]## Key Points (ACG 2016)- **Transmission:** Contaminated water (recreational — pools, lakes), food, person-to-person.- **Clinical presentation:** Watery diarrhea; cramps; nausea; self-limited in immunocompetent hosts (1–2 weeks); chronic/severe in immunocompromised.- **Diagnosis:** Stool antigen EIA (sensitive); modified acid-fast stain for oocysts; multiplex PCR (22-fold more sensitive than microscopy per one study).- **Treatment:**  - Nitazoxanide 500 mg BID × 3 days (immunocompetent)  - Immunocompromised: antiretroviral therapy/immune reconstitution key; nitazoxanide has limited efficacy in severe immunosuppression.- Chlorine resistance: water treatment requires filtration, UV, or ozone.See [[acute-diarrhea]] for diagnostic algorithm and management framework.
