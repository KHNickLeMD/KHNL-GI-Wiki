Rotavirus — with [[norovirus]] and adenovirus — is one of the viral agents that account for **most community-acquired [[acute-diarrhea|acute diarrhea]]**, which antibiotics do not shorten. [[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]] Classically the cause of severe dehydrating diarrhea in infants and young children; a routine infant vaccine is recommended. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]]

## Assessment

### Establishing the Diagnosis

- Acute **watery, non-bloody** diarrhea ± vomiting, low-grade fever; self-limited in the immunocompetent host.
- **Most cases need no etiologic testing.** Stool studies are reserved for dysentery, moderate–severe disease, symptoms >7 days, patients at high risk of spreading disease, or outbreaks. [[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]] (Recs 1–2)
- When testing is done: **culture-independent / multiplex molecular panels** detect rotavirus and are recommended at least as an adjunct to traditional methods (culture, microscopy, antigen testing), which miss most etiologies. [[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]] (Rec 3)
- **Fresh stool is the preferred specimen for viral agents** *(weak, low)*. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]] (Rec 19)
- In a **possible outbreak**, consider a broader bacterial/viral/parasitic panel regardless of fever or bloody stools, coordinated with public health *(strong, moderate)*. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]] (Rec 11)

### Severity Assessment

- Severity is driven by **volume depletion**, not by the organism — assess dehydration, oral intake, and host (infant, elderly, immunocompromised).
- **Immunocompromised host** — broaden the differential with culture, viral studies, and parasite examination *(strong, moderate)*. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]] (Rec 12)

## Differential Diagnosis

*Workup: see [[acute-diarrhea]].*

- Other viral gastroenteritis — [[norovirus]], adenovirus
- Non-inflammatory bacterial — [[enterotoxigenic-e-coli|ETEC]]
- Protozoal (watery, more often persistent) — [[giardiasis]], [[cryptosporidiosis]]
- [[clostridioides-difficile|*C. difficile*]] colitis (antibiotic / healthcare exposure)
- Non-infectious at ≥14 days — [[postinfectious-ibs|post-infectious IBS]], [[irritable-bowel-syndrome]], [[inflammatory-bowel-disease|IBD]]

## Diagnostics

| Test | Role |
|---|---|
| Multiplex GI PCR / culture-independent panel | Detects rotavirus; recommended at least as adjunct to traditional methods [[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]] |
| Fresh (not swab) stool | Preferred specimen for viral agents [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]] |
| Serology | **Do not use** to establish etiology *(strong, low)* [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]] |
| Peripheral WBC + differential | **Do not use** to establish etiology *(strong, low)* [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]] |
| Fecal leukocytes / stool lactoferrin | **Do not use** to establish etiology [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]] |
| Antibiotic sensitivity testing | Not recommended in acute diarrheal infection *(strong, very low)* [[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]] (Rec 4) |

- **Molecular caveat:** NAAT/multiplex panels detect nucleic acid, not necessarily a viable organism — interpret positives in clinical context. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]]

## Therapeutics

### Antibiotics — no role

- Antibiotics for community-acquired diarrhea **should be discouraged**: most is viral (norovirus, rotavirus, adenovirus) and is not shortened by antibiotics *(strong, very low)*. [[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]] (Rec 10)
- Empiric antimicrobial therapy is **not recommended** for acute watery diarrhea without recent international travel *(strong, low)*. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]] (Rec 36)

### Supportive care — the treatment

- **Rehydration is the foundation:** reduced-osmolarity ORS for mild–moderate dehydration; isotonic IV fluid (LR, NS) for severe dehydration, shock, or ileus. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]]
- Balanced electrolyte rehydration preferred in the **elderly with severe diarrhea** and in any traveler with cholera-like watery diarrhea; most others keep up with water, juices, sports drinks, soups, and saltine crackers *(strong, moderate)*. [[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]] (Rec 5)
- [[loperamide]] — **contraindicated <18 years**, and at any age with fever/inflammatory diarrhea or [[toxic-megacolon]] risk. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]]
- **Children:** ondansetron may aid ORS tolerance (>4 years); oral zinc shortens diarrhea at 6 months–5 years in zinc-deficient/malnourished settings. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]]
- [[probiotics]] — not recommended for acute diarrhea in adults, except post-antibiotic-associated illness *(strong, moderate)*. [[acg-2016-acute-diarrhea]] (Rec 6)

### Prevention

- **Rotavirus vaccine should be administered to all infants without a known contraindication** *(strong, high)*. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]] (Rec 57)
- Symptomatic people should avoid swimming, water-related activities, and sexual contact, with meticulous hand hygiene *(strong, low)*. [[idsa-2017-infectious-diarrhea]] (Rec 56)
