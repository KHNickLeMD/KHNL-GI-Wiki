## Assessment

- Aneurysm or pseudoaneurysm of the mesenteric (splanchnic) arterial circulation — grouped with the miscellaneous vascular disorders of the hepatic and mesenteric circulation. [[acg-2020-hepatic-mesenteric-circulation]]
- **SMA aneurysms are the most common site — ~60%.** [[acg-2020-hepatic-mesenteric-circulation]]

### Establishing the Diagnosis

- Frequently **asymptomatic and found incidentally** on cross-sectional imaging; the management decision turns on **size**, not symptoms (see Therapeutics).

### Severity Assessment

- **Size is the operative risk variable: rupture risk is higher with an aneurysm >2 cm.** [[acg-2020-hepatic-mesenteric-circulation]]

| Size | Risk | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **<2 cm, asymptomatic** | Lower rupture risk | Observe with imaging surveillance *(Conditional, low)* |
| **>2 cm** | Elevated rupture risk | **Treat — even if asymptomatic** *(Strong, low)* |

## Differential Diagnosis

- Mesenteric vein thrombosis (MVT) and [[portal-vein-thrombosis|portal vein thrombosis]] — the other mesenteric vascular disorders covered by the same guideline; venous rather than arterial. [[acg-2020-hepatic-mesenteric-circulation]]
- [[acute-mesenteric-ischemia|Acute mesenteric ischemia]] — overlapping presentation when an aneurysm thromboses or ruptures.
- [[hereditary-hemorrhagic-telangiectasia|Hereditary hemorrhagic telangiectasia]] — hepatic/visceral vascular malformations.

## Diagnostics

- Cross-sectional imaging defines the presence and, critically, the **diameter** that drives management.

> **Gap:** [[acg-2020-hepatic-mesenteric-circulation]] does not specify the preferred imaging modality, the surveillance interval for aneurysms <2 cm, or the treatment modality (open vs. endovascular) for those >2 cm. A dedicated splanchnic-aneurysm source would be needed to close these.

## Therapeutics

- **Asymptomatic, <2 cm:** observe with imaging surveillance. *(Conditional, low)* [[acg-2020-hepatic-mesenteric-circulation]]
- **>2 cm:** treat all, **even if asymptomatic**, because of rupture risk. *(Strong, low)* [[acg-2020-hepatic-mesenteric-circulation]]
