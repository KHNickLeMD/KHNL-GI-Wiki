Post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder (PTLD) is a spectrum of lymphoid proliferations occurring in the setting of immunosuppression after solid organ transplantation, most commonly driven by Epstein-Barr virus (EBV). It represents a major cause of de novo malignancy post-liver transplant. See [[liver-transplantation]] for surveillance and management recommendations.
