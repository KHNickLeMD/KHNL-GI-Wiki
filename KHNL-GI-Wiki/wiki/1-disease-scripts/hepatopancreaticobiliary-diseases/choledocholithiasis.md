Choledocholithiasis is the presence of gallstones in the common bile duct, the most common cause of **obstructive [[jaundice]]** in adults and a precipitant of acute cholangitis and gallstone [[acute-pancreatitis|pancreatitis]]. Suspicion is risk-stratified (ASGE high/intermediate/low probability) using total bilirubin, CBD diameter on ultrasound, and clinical cholangitis. **High-probability** patients proceed directly to [[ercp|ERCP]] for stone extraction; **intermediate-probability** patients undergo further imaging with MRCP or [[endoscopic-ultrasound|EUS]] before ERCP. Charcot's triad (fever, jaundice, RUQ pain) signals cholangitis requiring urgent biliary drainage and antibiotics. See [[biliary-stricture]] for the malignant-obstruction differential.
