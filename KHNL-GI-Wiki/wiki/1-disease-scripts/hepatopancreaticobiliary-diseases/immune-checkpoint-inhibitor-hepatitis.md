## Assessment

### Establishing the Diagnosis

ICI hepatotoxicity occurs as an immune-related adverse event (irAE) from PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors (pembrolizumab, nivolumab, atezolizumab) or CTLA-4 inhibitors (ipilimumab) or combinations.

**Key features distinguishing ICI hepatitis from other DILI:**

- **Onset:** typically 4–12 weeks after ICI initiation (range 2 weeks to months post-treatment)
- **Pattern:** Hepatocellular or mixed; cholestatic rare
- **Histology:** Zone 3 hepatocellular necrosis, lobular inflammation; can resemble AIH (plasma cells) — see [[autoimmune-hepatitis]]
- Autoantibodies **negative** in most ICI hepatitis (vs. AIH)

**Grading (CTCAE):**

- Grade 1: ALT/AST >ULN–3× ULN
- Grade 2: 3–5× ULN
- Grade 3: 5–20× ULN
- Grade 4: >20× ULN

---

## Differential Diagnosis

- [[autoimmune-hepatitis]] — clinical overlap; may need biopsy to distinguish
- Other DILI from co-medications
- Disease progression to liver (metastases)
- Viral hepatitis reactivation (HBV — check HBsAg/anti-HBc before ICI initiation)

---

## Diagnostics

*Stub — LFTs at baseline and each cycle; autoantibody panel (ANA/SMA to screen for baseline AIH); biopsy for Grade 3–4 or diagnostic uncertainty.*

---

## Therapeutics

Per ACG 2021 DILI guideline:

- **Grade 1:** Continue ICI; monitor LFTs weekly
- **Grade 2:** Withhold ICI; oral prednisone 0.5–1 mg/kg/day; restart ICI when Grade ≤1
- **Grade 3–4:** **Withhold ICI permanently**; IV methylprednisolone 1–2 mg/kg/day; if no response in 3 days → add mycophenolate mofetil (MMF) 500–1000 mg BID; tacrolimus alternative
- **Ipilimumab** combinations: higher grade 3–4 hepatitis rate (~8–10%) vs. PD-1 monotherapy (~1–2%)
