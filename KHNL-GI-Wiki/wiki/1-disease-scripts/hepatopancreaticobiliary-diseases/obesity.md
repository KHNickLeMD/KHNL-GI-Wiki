# Obesity

Obesity (commonly BMI ≥30 kg/m², or ≥27 with weight-related comorbidity) affects roughly 40% of US adults and drives multiple GI and metabolic comorbidities. Management spans lifestyle modification, pharmacotherapy (see [[aga-2022-obesity-pharm]], [[semaglutide]]), endoscopic bariatric therapy ([[intragastric-balloon]]), and [[bariatric-surgery]].
