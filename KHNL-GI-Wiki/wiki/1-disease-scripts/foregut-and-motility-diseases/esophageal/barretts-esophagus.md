Barrett's esophagus (BE): distal esophageal squamous epithelium replaced by columnar epithelium with goblet cells (intestinal metaplasia [IM]) — the only known precursor of [[esophageal-adenocarcinoma|esophageal adenocarcinoma]] (EAC). Arises from chronic [[gerd|gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD)]]; present in 5–12% with chronic GERD symptoms.

## Assessment

### Establishing the Diagnosis

**Diagnostic criteria — both required** ([[acg-2022-barretts|American College of Gastroenterology (ACG) 2022]]):

1. **IM (goblet cells) on biopsy** from the tubular esophagus — columnar mucosa with IM carries higher EAC risk than without (hazard ratio 3.54); UK/Asia-Pacific guidelines don't require IM
2. **Columnar segment ≥1 cm** — Prague reliability poor <1 cm (0.22 vs ~0.95 at ≥1 cm); irregular Z line <1 cm did not progress to high-grade dysplasia (HGD)/EAC in two large cohorts

- **Do not biopsy** a normal Z line or one displaced <1 cm above the gastric folds without visible lesions
- **Biopsy yield:** goblet cells are patchy — 1 biopsy finds IM ~34%; **≥8 biopsies → ~94%**. Segments 1–2 cm: ≥4 biopsies/cm circumferential + 1/cm of tongues
- Prior columnar mucosa but IM-negative → **one** repeat exam (IM in ≥25%); no further repeats
- Every exam: partial desufflation (inflated folds mimic [[hiatal-hernia|hiatal hernia]]/columnar segment), high-definition white light incl. retroflexed cardia, chromoendoscopy (acetic acid or narrow-band imaging [NBI])

### Severity Assessment

Dysplasia grade drives risk and management. **Quote the right endpoint** — EAC-only rates run ~3–4× lower than HGD-or-EAC.

| Grade | Annual EAC | Annual HGD or EAC | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nondysplastic BE (NDBE) **<3 cm** | 0.07% ([[acg-2022-barretts\|ACG 2022]]); 0.06% ([[aga-2025-barretts-surveillance\|American Gastroenterological Association (AGA) 2025]]) | 0.29% | Very low risk |
| NDBE **≥3 cm** | 0.25% (ACG); 0.31% (AGA) | 0.91% | HGD/EAC risk ~**doubles per 4 cm** (AGA 2025) |
| Indefinite for dysplasia (IND) | 0.6% (95% confidence interval [CI] 0.1–1.1) | 1.5% (1.0–2.0) — ≈ low-grade dysplasia (LGD) per ACG | Often inflammation; in 4.3–8.4% of BE biopsies |
| LGD (confirmed) | — | 1.73%–11.8% | Meta-analysis 1.73%/patient-yr; SURF randomized controlled trial (RCT) surveillance arm 11.8% |
| HGD | — | Substantially higher | Surveillance alone inappropriate |
| Intramucosal carcinoma (IMC) | — | — | Lymph node (LN) metastasis ~0–2%; endoscopic eradication therapy (EET) preferred |

- **Segment length** independently predicts progression (odds ratio [OR] 1.25 per cm, 95% CI 1.16–1.36)
- Other progression risks: male sex, **smoking (~1.5×, AGA 2025)**, older age, no [[proton-pump-inhibitors|proton pump inhibitor (PPI)]] use

### Classification / Typing

**Prague C&M:** **C** = circumferential extent, **M** = maximal extent incl. tongues, both in cm above the top of the gastric folds (C3M5 = 3 cm circumferential, 5 cm maximal).

| Grade | Definition | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| NDBE | IM, no dysplasia | — |
| IND | Can't separate dysplasia from inflammation | Expert gastrointestinal (GI) pathologist; intensify PPI |
| LGD | Low-grade cytologic dysplasia | **Second expert GI pathologist** before any management change |
| HGD | High-grade cytologic dysplasia | Second expert GI pathologist |
| IMC | EAC confined to mucosa | LN risk ~0–2% |
| T1b EAC | Submucosal invasion | sm1 vs sm2/3 decides EET vs surgery → [[esophageal-adenocarcinoma]] |

**LGD is overcalled** — agreement 42% (IND 23%); **community LGD downstaged in 73%**. Expert-confirmed LGD progresses 9.1%/patient-yr vs 0.6–0.9% if downstaged ([[acg-2022-barretts]]).

| Pathologists agreeing on LGD (of 3) | Annual progression to HGD/EAC |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2.4% |
| 2 | 6.3% |
| 3 | 20% |

> [!pearl] Concordant LGD means look harder now
> Pathologist agreement on LGD also tracks **prevalent** HGD/cancer — a confirmed read should trigger a search for missed neoplasia, not just a shorter interval ([[acg-2022-barretts]]).

**Visible lesions:** [[polypectomy|Paris classification]] (criteria + schematics there) — 0-I and 0-IIc carry more submucosal invasion; 0-III suggests deep invasion, less suited to endoscopic resection.

## Differential Diagnosis

*Workup: see [[dyspepsia]].*

- **[[gerd|GERD]] without BE** — irregular Z line <1 cm, no goblet cells
- **Cardiac-type columnar mucosa without IM** — not BE per ACG; no surveillance
- **[[eosinophilic-esophagitis|Eosinophilic esophagitis]]** — proximal/mid involvement, atopy; no EAC risk
- **[[achalasia|Achalasia]]** — [[dysphagia]] to solids + liquids; [[high-resolution-manometry|manometry]] diagnostic; squamous ([[esophageal-cancer|esophageal squamous cell carcinoma]]) not EAC risk
- **Inlet patch** — heterotopic gastric mucosa, proximal esophagus
- **[[infectious-esophagitis|Candida/infectious esophagitis]]** — exudative plaques, no metaplasia

## Diagnostics

### Initial Endoscopic Evaluation

**High-quality BE exam (10 steps):**

1. Identify and document landmarks — diaphragmatic hiatus, top of gastric folds (gastroesophageal junction [GEJ]), squamocolumnar junction (SCJ, Z line)
2. Consider a distal attachment cap (esp. prior dysplasia)
3. Wash with water jet; suction fluid
4. Vary insufflation to reveal subtle lesions
5. Adequate time on the segment and retroflexed cardia
6. High-definition white-light endoscopy (HD-WLE)
7. Chromoendoscopy (acetic acid or virtual)
8. Prague C&M for length
9. Paris for visible lesions
10. Seattle protocol with esophagus partially deflated

### Biopsy Protocols

- **Diagnostic exam:** ≥8 biopsies; segments >4 cm — Seattle 4-quadrant every 2 cm + targeted
- **Surveillance** ([[aga-2025-barretts-surveillance|AGA 2025]] Rec 3) — targeted biopsies of visible lesions **first**, then random 4-quadrant:

| Dysplasia history | 4-quadrant biopsies every |
|---|---|
| None | **2 cm** |
| Any (incl. current LGD) | **1 cm** |

- Chromoendoscopy-targeted biopsies are an **adjunct**, never a substitute for the structured protocol
- **After complete eradication of IM (CEIM):** 4-quadrant from high cardia just below the Z line + distal 2–3 cm of neosquamous epithelium (separate bottles); higher random biopsies add little

### Pathology and IHC

- **Expert GI pathologist review for all dysplasia (strong)** — ≥5 y experience ↓ major errors (OR 0.48); non-teaching hospital ↑ (OR 1.76)
- Goblet cells on hematoxylin and eosin; immunohistochemistry (IHC) for trefoil factor 3 can confirm IM

**Adjunct biomarkers and imaging:**

| Tool | Position | Key data |
|---|---|---|
| **Chromoendoscopy** (dye or virtual) | **Strong** — with HD-WLE (AGA 2025 Rec 3; [[asge-2019-barretts-screening-surveillance\|American Society for Gastrointestinal Endoscopy (ASGE) 2019]]) | +9% absolute dysplasia detection; virtual ≈ dye |
| **p53 IHC** | AGA 2025 Rec 5: no recommendation | Aberrant p53 OR 4–17 for progression |
| **TissueCypher** | AGA 2025 Rec 6: no recommendation | Sensitivity 30%, specificity 95% in NDBE |
| **Wide-area transepithelial sampling (WATS-3D)** | ASGE 2019 adjunct (conditional, low); AGA 2025 Rec 4, ACG: no recommendation | ~2% absolute ↑ detection, mostly LGD; false positives |
| [[confocal-laser-endomicroscopy\|Confocal laser endomicroscopy]] | ASGE 2019 against routine | — |
| [[artificial-intelligence-endoscopy\|Computer-aided detection]] | No recommendation yet (ACG 2022) | Promising |

- **Dysplasia only on WATS-3D** → repeat endoscopy in **3–6 months** with structured biopsies + resection of visible lesions; crypt dysplasia/IND/LGD → expert endoscopist on high-dose acid suppression **within 6 months** (AGA 2025)

### Adjunct Staging Tests

- **Endoscopic ultrasound (EUS) not for T1a vs T1b** — ASGE 2019 strong against (sensitivity 41%); resection histology stages. EUS and computed tomography/positron emission tomography for T1b and beyond → [[esophageal-adenocarcinoma]]

## Therapeutics

### Screening (Who Should Be Screened)

![[barretts-2022-screening-algorithm-06.png|700x571]]
*Figure 2 — Care algorithm for patients noted to have columnar mucosa in the tubular esophagus (diagnosis and surveillance). ([[acg-2022-barretts]])*

**Single screening [[upper-endoscopy]]** — criteria differ by society:

| Source | Who |
|---|---|
| **ACG 2022** | Chronic GERD (weekly ≥5 y) **plus ≥3**: male, age >50, White race, smoking (current/past), central [[obesity]], first-degree family history of BE/EAC |
| [[aga-2022-barretts-new-technology\|AGA 2022 clinical practice update (CPU)]] best practice advice (BPA) 1 | **≥3 risk factors from the same list, with GERD as just one of them** (GERD not required) — "3" is expert opinion |
| [[asge-2019-barretts-screening-surveillance\|ASGE 2019]] | **High:** first-degree family history of BE/EAC → screen · **Moderate:** GERD + ≥1 (age >50, central obesity, smoking, male) → may benefit · **Low:** don't screen |

- Risk tools (AGA 2022 BPA 10): HUNT (Nord-Trøndelag Health Study), M-BERET (Michigan BE pREdiction Tool), Kunzmann beat GERD symptoms alone; no published cut-points
- **No repeat screening** after a negative exam
- **Erosive esophagitis [[reflux-testing|Los Angeles (LA)]] grade B or worse** at index → repeat esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) after 8–12 wk PPI to unmask BE

> [!pitfall] Screening only GERD patients misses most cancers
> Most EAC patients never had frequent GERD symptoms or a BE diagnosis — a GERD-negative patient with ≥3 other risk factors can still be offered screening ([[aga-2022-barretts-new-technology]], [[asge-2019-barretts-screening-surveillance]]). The size of the miss, quantified: **fewer than 10% of EAC patients carry a prior BE diagnosis**, **~40% describe no antecedent GERD at all**, **~50% of short-segment BE patients have no GERD symptoms**, and only about **10% of patients with recurrent GERD symptoms are scoped in a given year**. Capturing the GERD-negative 40% of cancers "would involve screening approximately 80% of the adult US population" ([[aga-2018-barretts-screening-techniques]]).

#### Nonendoscopic and Alternative-Endoscopic Screening Tools

**ACG 2022 governs the recommendation** — a nonendoscopic capsule sponge device plus a biomarker is an **acceptable alternative** screening modality (conditional, very low quality). The performance, mechanics and economics behind that come from [[aga-2018-barretts-screening-techniques]], whose own 2018 position was that **no alternative test was yet recommended** — a stance its own sub-recommendation anticipated would be overtaken.

| Tool | How it works | Performance | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Capsule sponge (Cytosponge) + trefoil factor-3 (TFF3)** | Swallowed **gelatin capsule dissolves in ~5 min**, releasing a **3-cm mesh sponge on a string**; withdrawn by traction, scraping esophageal cells. TFF3 marks intestinalization | **BEST2** (1110 subjects): intention-to-treat sensitivity **79.9%**; **87.2%** with **≥3 cm circumferential BE**; **89.7%** on a **repeat** procedure. **99%** of >500 primary-care patients swallowed it safely; **82%** reported low pre-test anxiety | ⚠ **The widely quoted 91.5% came from an enriched 73-patient population with inadequate samples excluded** — the source says so explicitly. Rated only `+` for **surveillance** accuracy |
| **Transnasal endoscopy (TNE)** | Thin sheathed endoscope via the nose; **no sedation, ambulatory, no sterilization between uses** | Recovery **18.5 vs 67.3 min** (*P*<.001); complete esophageal evaluation **96% vs 100%**; **~80%** would repeat it. In primary care, **422/426 (99%)** completed it and **38%** had a management-changing finding (34% erosive esophagitis, 4% BE) | **Biopsy success 83% vs 100%** (*P*=.001) — a **technical** shortfall from using a 4.7 mm sheath with no biopsy channel when the 5.8 mm biopsy-capable one would not pass. ⚠ **Not adequate for dysplasia surveillance** — optical quality below high-definition scopes, no virtual chromoendoscopy |
| **Video [[capsule-endoscopy\|capsule endoscopy]]** | Swallowed imaging capsule | Pooled sensitivity **77%**, specificity **86%** (9 studies, 618 patients) | **No biopsy capability**; **esophageal capsule retention 0.7%–2.2%**, usually from an unsuspected stricture; the sedation-free cost saving is **negated by device cost** |
| **Tethered capsule endomicroscopy · liquid biopsy (circulating microRNA) · serum adipokines · electronic nose breath testing** | — | Promising but **unvalidated**; a 4-microRNA panel gave 78%/86% in 41 BE vs 15 controls | None has a surveillance role; liquid biopsy and breath testing are scored as **unestablished** for both screening and surveillance accuracy |

- **Cost:** capsule sponge screening versus no screening, **$26,358–$33,307 per quality-adjusted life-year** — below the **<$50,000** threshold cited. **Endoscopy versus capsule sponge costs $107,583–$330,361** per quality-adjusted life-year, above it. Unsedated TNE costs **3–4× less** than sedated endoscopy in the community ([[aga-2018-barretts-screening-techniques]]).
- ⚠ **A cheap test does not automatically save money.** Applied to an expanded population, the positives it generates all funnel back into [[upper-endoscopy|endoscopy]], so the societal saving "could be reduced, eliminated, or even reversed." The tools are for **enriching** an already-defined high-risk population, not for widening it.
- **Named harms of overdiagnosis:** psychological stress, higher health and life insurance premiums, and identification of innocuous lesions leading to potentially hazardous invasive treatment.

### Medical Therapy / Chemoprevention

- **Daily PPI for all BE** ([[aga-2025-barretts-surveillance|AGA 2025]] Rec 7, conditional/low) — observational ~71% ↓ HGD/EAC (adjusted OR 0.29); twice daily if ongoing symptoms or acid exposure. Long-term safety reassuring (enteric infection OR 1.33, number needed to harm >900/yr)
- Counsel **tobacco cessation** and **weight loss** (AGA 2025)
- PPI (OR 0.55) and statin (OR 0.48) use associated with less progression to dysplasia ([[asge-2019-barretts-screening-surveillance|ASGE 2019]])
- **Aspirin:** no chemoprevention recommendation (AspECT: no significant cancer-specific benefit); cardiac aspirin is fine
- **[[antireflux-surgery|Antireflux surgery]] is not antineoplastic** — AGA 2025 Rec 8: PPI over surgery to prevent progression (conditional/low)

### Surveillance Intervals

- **Refer any BE neoplasia — incl. IND and LGD** — to a high-volume EET endoscopist, BE-expert pathologist, multidisciplinary care ([[aga-2025-barretts-surveillance|AGA 2025]])
- **NDBE: surveillance suggested** (AGA 2025 Rec 1, conditional/low) — BOSS RCT: no survival difference but more early HGD/EAC found; discuss the uncertainty
- **Columnar mucosa <1 cm with IM, no neoplasia:** **against** surveillance (AGA 2025 Rec 2, conditional/very low)

| Histology | Interval / action |
|---|---|
| **NDBE** | **AGA 2025 (governs): every 3 y**; may extend to **5 y** if lower risk (e.g. <3 cm). ACG 2022: M <3 cm → 5 y; M ≥3 cm → 3 y |
| **IND** | Expert pathology → PPI twice daily (30–45 min before meals, ≥2 mo) → expert EGD **within 6 mo** on high-dose PPI (structured biopsies + resect visible lesions). Persistent IND → **annual EGD**; **no EET** (AGA 2025) |
| **LGD** | Four steps below |
| **HGD** | **EET** — surveillance alone inappropriate. Flat HGD → repeat HD-WLE at **6–8 wk** to find a visible lesion for [[endoscopic-mucosal-resection\|endoscopic mucosal resection (EMR)]] (ulcerated lesion → suspect invasive cancer); flat HGD → EAC 5–8%/yr ([[aga-2020-endoscopic-treatment-barretts-dysplasia\|AGA 2020]]) |

**Confirmed LGD — four decisions:**

1. **Is it really LGD?** ≥1 expert GI pathologist; downgraded to NDBE → NDBE intervals ([[aga-2016-low-grade-dysplasia-barretts|AGA 2016]])
2. **Confirmatory EGD** on high-dose acid suppression to exclude visible lesion/prevalent HGD-EAC — **AGA 2025: expert endoscopist within 6 mo** (AGA 2020: 3–6 mo; AGA 2016: 8–12 wk on twice-daily PPI)
   - Resect (don't biopsy) **any** visible abnormality — resection changes the grade in ~30%
   - No surveillance biopsies during **LA C/D esophagitis**; intensify therapy and resample
   - Proximal half of the segment ~2× as likely to harbor dysplasia
3. **EET or surveillance — documented shared decision** (AGA 2020 BPA 2; AGA 2016). Persistent LGD (2 consecutive EGDs) progresses 7.65%/yr vs 2.32%/yr (AGA 2020)
4. **If surveillance:** twice-daily PPI; EGD at **6 and 12 mo, then annually** until grade changes (AGA 2025; same in AGA 2016); 4-quadrant biopsies **every 1 cm**; reverts to NDBE → 3-y intervals. Expert-confirmed LGD progresses 5.7%/yr (AGA 2025)

- **Stop surveillance** when no longer fit for EET, life expectancy <5 y, or can't tolerate endoscopy — decide on **age and comorbidity** (AGA 2025); discuss around age 75

### Endoscopic Eradication Therapy (EET)

![[barretts-2022-eet-algorithm-18.png|700x431]]
*Figure 4 — Algorithm for patients referred for consideration of EET by dysplasia grade and depth of invasion. ([[acg-2022-barretts]])*

[[endoscopic-eradication-therapy|EET]] = resect visible lesions ([[endoscopic-mucosal-resection|EMR]]; [[endoscopic-submucosal-dissection|endoscopic submucosal dissection (ESD)]] for selected lesions), then ablate the residual flat segment ([[radiofrequency-ablation|radiofrequency ablation (RFA)]] preferred). Technique, ablation rules, outcomes, adverse events, center volume → [[endoscopic-eradication-therapy]].

| Indication | [[asge-2018-barretts-eet\|ASGE 2018]] | [[aga-2024-barretts-eet\|AGA 2024]] |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed HGD | EET over surveillance and over esophagectomy (**strong**) | EET over surveillance (**strong/moderate**, Rec 1) |
| IMC (T1a EAC) | EET over esophagectomy (**strong**) | Refer suspected T1 EAC for EET (Rec 5) |
| Confirmed LGD | EET over surveillance (**conditional**) | EET over surveillance (**conditional/low**, Rec 2) — surveillance reasonable if harms weigh more |
| NDBE | Not routine | **Against** routine EET (conditional/very low, Rec 3) |

- Confirm LGD/HGD with ≥1 expert GI pathologist before EET — changes the diagnosis in ~55% (ASGE 2018)
- **T1b:** EET is an alternative to esophagectomy only for **sm1 (<500 µm) with negative deep margin, well/moderate differentiation, and no lymphovascular invasion** — all four; full criteria and nodal risk → [[esophageal-adenocarcinoma]] ([[acg-2022-barretts]])
- LGD evidence: SURF RCT (n=136) — RFA cut HGD/EAC progression 26.5% → 1.5% (number needed to treat 4); persistence not required before offering EET
- Goal: complete eradication of dysplasia and CEIM within **18 months** (benchmark ≥80%); most need ~3 sessions

> [!pitfall] Ablate only flat, healed mucosa
> Inspect with HD-WLE + chromoendoscopy at every session and resect anything visible first — ablating over an unrecognized invasive cancer is the worst treatment-phase outcome ([[aga-2020-endoscopic-treatment-barretts-dysplasia]]).

### Post-CEIM Surveillance

- **CEIM** = 1–2 endoscopies with no visible BE and no IM on GEJ + tubular esophagus biopsies
- Recurrence/yr: IM 8.6–10.5%, dysplasia 2.0%, HGD/EAC 1.2%; **75% at the GEJ** (60% of these invisible) — biopsy per [[#Biopsy Protocols]]
- **Never stop surveillance** (ASGE 2018 Rec 7) — most recurrences within 3 y; >90% re-treated endoscopically

| Worst pre-treatment histology | Schedule after CEIM |
|---|---|
| LGD | 1 y → 3 y → **NDBE intervals** (AGA 2024 Rec 2; ACG 2022: every 2 y after year 3) |
| HGD / IMC | 3 mo → 6 mo → 12 mo → annually (AGA 2024 Rec 1; ACG concordant) |

- **Cardia IM alone** (no residual columnar mucosa in the tubular esophagus) **does not need more ablation** — found in up to 25% of adults without BE, usually not seen again ([[aga-2020-endoscopic-treatment-barretts-dysplasia|AGA 2020]])

### Post-Endoscopy Esophageal Adenocarcinoma (PEEC) and Examination Quality

Early post-endoscopy neoplasia is mostly **missed prevalent disease** ([[aga-2020-post-endoscopy-esophageal-adenocarcinoma|AGA 2020]]).

- **PEEC** (proposed): EAC and/or BE-related HGD within ~**1 year** of an index endoscopy. **Interval EAC:** after a negative exam, before the next scheduled one
- Scale: PEEC 3–25% across studies; **25.3%** of HGD/EAC in surveillance deemed missed. Failure to reach CEIM → recurrent HGD/EAC relative risk 2.2 — residual IM marks prevalent dysplasia
- Seattle sampling, even done well, covers **<5%** of the segment

| Proposed quality indicator | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **Neoplasia detection rate** (HGD/EAC at index screening exam) | ≥**4%** (pooled prevalence 7%) |
| Barrett's inspection time | **1 min per cm** of circumferential BE (needs validation) |
| NDBE surveillance interval | No sooner than **3–5 y** |
| LGD/BE ratio (practice level) | Ratio <0.15 → more progression to EAC (0.76%/yr vs 0.32%/yr) — undercalling LGD lets cancers through ([[aga-2016-low-grade-dysplasia-barretts\|AGA 2016]]) |

> [!howto] Four habits that reduce PEEC
> 1. Photo-document hiatus, GEJ, SCJ; Prague for length, Paris for lesions; resect or refer
> 2. HD-WLE **plus** virtual chromoendoscopy with a validated classification (Barrett's International NBI Group)
> 3. Inspect adequately and sample by Seattle protocol — "look more and biopsy appropriately"
> 4. Monitor upper-endoscopy quality locally
> ([[aga-2020-post-endoscopy-esophageal-adenocarcinoma]])
