## Assessment

### Establishing the Diagnosis

Reflux hypersensitivity is heartburn or chest pain with **physiologic acid exposure** but a **positive reflux-symptom association** — the patient's symptoms are genuinely triggered by reflux events that are themselves normal in number and acidity. It is an esophageal disorder of gut–brain interaction (E-DGBI) ([[disorders-of-gut-brain-interaction]]).

**Rome V criteria (A3)** ([[rome-v-2026-functional-esophageal-disorders]]):

> Must include **all** of the following:
> 1. **Retrosternal burning and/or chest pain**
> 2. **Regurgitation and belching are not considered** in this definition if they occur by themselves or together
> 3. **Evidence of triggering of symptoms by reflux events**
> 4. Absence of evidence that **[[gerd|gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD)]]** (abnormal acid exposure by technology and approach, and/or Los Angeles [LA] grade B or higher esophagitis) **or nonreflux esophagitis** is the cause of the symptoms
> 5. **Absence of disorders of esophagogastric junction (EGJ) obstruction and/or disorders of esophageal peristalsis** — except [[ineffective-esophageal-motility|ineffective esophageal motility (IEM)]], per [[chicago-classification-v4|Chicago Classification version 4.0]]
>
> Criteria fulfilled for the past 3 months, symptom onset ≥6 months before diagnosis, frequency **at least once a week**, despite **optimized antisecretory therapy for at least 8 weeks**.

- **"Optimized antisecretory therapy" = 8 weeks of optimized [[proton-pump-inhibitors|proton pump inhibitor (PPI)]] or [[potassium-competitive-acid-blockers|potassium-competitive acid blocker (PCAB)]] therapy.** Rome V specifies no dose beyond "optimized".

> [!pearl] Belching or regurgitation alone is a different diagnosis
> If belching and/or regurgitation are the **only** symptoms associated with reflux events, the answer is **[[rumination-syndrome|rumination]] or supragastric belching** — not reflux hypersensitivity ([[abdominal-bloating-and-distention]]).

### Separating It from Functional Heartburn and GERD

All three present as heartburn that fails a PPI. The reflux study separates them:

| Diagnosis | Acid exposure time (AET) | Reflux-symptom association |
|---|---|---|
| GERD | **>6%**, or LA grade B–D / Barrett's / peptic stricture | either |
| **Reflux hypersensitivity** | **<4%** (physiologic) | **positive** |
| [[functional-heartburn\|Functional heartburn]] | **<4%** (physiologic) | **negative** |

- **Positive reflux-symptom association = symptom index ≥50% and/or symptom association probability ≥95%.** This single pair of numbers is the whole boundary against functional heartburn.
- Physiologic reflux is confirmed by **AET <4% on all days of a prolonged wireless pH study**, or **AET <4% with <40 reflux episodes and mean nocturnal baseline impedance (MNBI) >2500 Ω** on pH-impedance monitoring. Testing may be done **on or off** PPI therapy.
- **AET 4%–6% is inconclusive** — MNBI then adjudicates: **<1500 Ω supports GERD**, **>2500 Ω reasonably excludes it**. The full adjunctive metric set is on [[ambulatory-reflux-monitoring]].
- **IEM does not exclude the diagnosis.** The exclusionary motor disorders are [[achalasia]], conclusive [[esophagogastric-junction-outflow-obstruction|EGJ outflow obstruction]], absent contractility, hypercontractile esophagus, and distal esophageal spasm.

> [!pitfall] Proven GERD off PPI does not exclude this diagnosis
> Physiologic reflux can still trigger symptoms **on** PPI therapy, so a patient with GERD documented off PPI may have reflux hypersensitivity on it. Prevalence in that setting is low, but the diagnosis is not ruled out ([[rome-v-2026-functional-esophageal-disorders]]).

### Severity Assessment

- **Rome V sets no severity grades for reflux hypersensitivity.** Severity is judged by symptom burden and quality-of-life impact.
- **Hypervigilance** (excessive attention to bodily sensations) and **visceral/symptom-specific anxiety** amplify perceived severity. Both are patient-reported outcomes *and* moderators of symptom severity and quality of life, so measuring them alongside disease-specific quality-of-life tools improves outcome assessment.

## Differential Diagnosis

*Workup: see [[reflux-testing]]; for a chest-pain presentation see [[noncardiac-chest-pain]].*

- [[gerd|GERD]] — pathologic acid burden; the exclusion that must be made first
- [[functional-heartburn|Functional heartburn]] — same physiologic acid exposure, **negative** symptom association
- [[functional-chest-pain|Functional chest pain]] — when chest pain predominates without heartburn
- [[eosinophilic-esophagitis|Eosinophilic esophagitis]] — excluded histologically (<15 eosinophils/high power field)
- [[rumination-syndrome|Rumination syndrome]] / supragastric belching — when regurgitation or belching is the reflux-associated symptom
- [[achalasia]], [[esophagogastric-junction-outflow-obstruction|EGJOO]], distal esophageal spasm, hypercontractile esophagus — exclusionary major motor disorders

## Diagnostics

- **[[upper-endoscopy|Upper endoscopy]]** — exclude LA grade B or higher esophagitis, [[barretts-esophagus|Barrett's esophagus]] and peptic stricture. Yield is limited when done on PPI (mucosal healing rates are high), so scope ideally after **2 to 4 weeks off PPI** to detect baseline esophagitis. Routine biopsies for eosinophilic esophagitis are **not** required without dysphagia, chest pain with eating, or suggestive endoscopic features.
- **[[ambulatory-reflux-monitoring|Ambulatory reflux monitoring]]** — the diagnostic test. Prolonged wireless pH or 24-hour pH-impedance; the symptom-association analysis is what makes the diagnosis, so the patient must mark symptoms accurately. **It remains the metric that separates reflux hypersensitivity from functional heartburn, and is limited by patient reporting accuracy.** Modality choice and the index formulas live on that page.
- **[[high-resolution-manometry|High-resolution manometry]]** — exclude the major motor disorders per Chicago Classification version 4.0.
- **MNBI is lower in reflux hypersensitivity than in functional heartburn**, consistent with impaired mucosal integrity, **but MNBI thresholds are not yet diagnostic** for the distinction.

### Pretest Probability

| Setting | Reflux hypersensitivity |
|---|---|
| General population (Rome Foundation global survey) | **0.8%** internet-based · **0.6%** household |
| PPI-refractory reflux symptoms | **10%–42%** (36% of 329 patients, 42% of 573 patients, 10% in the Spechler trial) |
| Proven GERD with persistent symptoms (GERD/E-DGBI overlap) | **25%–37%** — less common than functional heartburn |

- Among patients without esophagitis, **37%–60% have normal pH monitoring off therapy**.

## Therapeutics

Management parallels [[functional-heartburn]], starting with **reassurance and education that no serious underlying disease is present**.

- **Antisecretory therapy — response may be better here than in the other E-DGBIs.** Patients with an acid-sensitive esophagus may improve on standard or high-dose PPIs or PCABs; those whose triggers are weakly acid or nonacid reflux often remain refractory.
- **Pain modulators are the cornerstone** — tricyclic antidepressants (TCAs), selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs), serotonin norepinephrine reuptake inhibitors (SNRIs), gabapentinoids. Direct evidence is limited.
  - **Citalopram**, placebo-controlled: **38.5% remained symptomatic vs 66.7% on placebo** (*P* = .021).
  - **Imipramine 25 mg/day** reduced the proportion of symptomatic patients by 37.2% in a trial spanning functional heartburn and reflux hypersensitivity.
- **[[brain-gut-behavioral-therapy|Psychological therapies]]** — gut-directed cognitive behavioral therapy, hypnosis, biofeedback — should be integrated into management when appropriate.
- **[[antireflux-surgery|Antireflux surgery]]:** limited data suggest it **may help select patients, particularly those with a concurrent [[hiatal-hernia|hiatal hernia]]**. A randomized trial found it superior to medical therapy (PPI, [[baclofen]], desipramine) in refractory heartburn including reflux hypersensitivity, but response did not differ by acid burden. This is the one E-DGBI where surgery is not simply discouraged — it is to be **avoided** in functional heartburn and is **unsupported** in [[globus|globus]] alone.
- **Endoscopic radiofrequency energy delivery showed no benefit** in a sham-controlled trial.
