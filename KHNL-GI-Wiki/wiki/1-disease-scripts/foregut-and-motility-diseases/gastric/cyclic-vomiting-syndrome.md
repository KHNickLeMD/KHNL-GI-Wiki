# Cyclic Vomiting Syndrome (CVS)

Chronic [[disorders-of-gut-brain-interaction|disorder of gut–brain interaction (DGBI)]] of **stereotyped, recurrent discrete episodes of intense vomiting separated by symptom-free intervals**; strong migraine association. [[cannabinoid-hyperemesis-syndrome|Cannabinoid hyperemesis syndrome]] is a subtype ([[aga-2024-chs]]).

## Assessment

### Establishing the Diagnosis

**Rome IV criteria (adults):**
- Stereotypical episodes of vomiting regarding onset (acute) and duration (**<1 week**).
- **≥3 discrete episodes in the prior year AND 2 episodes in the past 6 months**, occurring **≥1 week apart**.
- **Absence of nausea and vomiting between episodes** (milder symptoms may persist between episodes).
- *Supportive:* personal or family history of migraine headaches.
- Criteria fulfilled for the last 3 months, with symptom onset **≥6 months** before diagnosis.

### Phases

Four phases (Fleisher) — recognizing the phase guides treatment:
1. **Interepisodic** (well interval) — prophylaxis.
2. **Prodromal** — abortive therapy window.
3. **Emetic** — supportive care, antiemetics, rehydration.
4. **Recovery.**

## Differential Diagnosis

*Workup: see [[nausea-and-vomiting]].*

- [[cannabinoid-hyperemesis-syndrome]] (CVS subtype tied to chronic cannabis use)
- [[gastroparesis]]
- [[rumination-syndrome]]
- pregnancy
- migraine
- functional (chronic) nausea and vomiting syndrome
- structural: bowel obstruction, mesenteric ischemia

## Diagnostics

- **Clinical diagnosis** (Rome IV); no confirmatory test.
- Limited, suspicion-driven evaluation to exclude mimics: anatomic (upper endoscopy or CT abdomen/pelvis to exclude obstruction); brain imaging if focal neurologic signs; pregnancy test.
- **ED priority:** exclude life-threatening mimics — acute abdomen, bowel obstruction, mesenteric ischemia, pancreatitis, myocardial infarction.

## Therapeutics

- **Prophylaxis (interepisodic):** tricyclic antidepressant (**amitriptyline**) — start 25 mg at bedtime, titrate weekly to minimal effective dose **75–100 mg at bedtime**.
- **Acute/emetic phase:** antiemetics; correct dehydration and electrolyte/metabolic derangements.
- Address migraine association; co-manage psychiatric comorbidity.
