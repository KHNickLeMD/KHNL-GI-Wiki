## Assessment
- Defined as mucosal break ≥5 mm with depth into submucosa, in stomach (gastric ulcer, GU) or duodenum (duodenal ulcer, DU)
- Etiology: H. pylori (~50% globally), NSAIDs/aspirin, idiopathic (~20%), Zollinger-Ellison, malignancy (GU), critical illness, viral (CMV, HSV in immunocompromised)

## Differential
- Gastric adenocarcinoma (esp non-healing GU), gastric lymphoma (MALT/DLBCL), erosive gastritis, dyspepsia, [[wiki/1-disease-scripts/foregut-and-motility-diseases/gastric/gastroparesis]], biliary pain

## Diagnostics
- EGD with biopsy of gastric ulcers (rule out malignancy; ≥4 biopsies of ulcer base + edge)
- All GU require biopsy + follow-up EGD at 8–12 weeks to confirm healing
- H. pylori testing in all PUD (Strong)
- Fasting gastrin if recurrent/refractory/multiple → Zollinger-Ellison

## Therapeutics
- PPI BID × 4–8 weeks (DU) or 8–12 weeks (GU); consider IV PPI for active bleeding ulcers
- H. pylori eradication if positive ([[helicobacter-pylori-infection]])
- Discontinue NSAIDs; if continued NSAID needed, co-prescribe PPI; consider COX-2 selective
- Acute bleeding: endoscopic hemostasis per Forrest class (see [[upper-gi-bleeding]]); high-dose PPI 80 mg bolus + 8 mg/h × 72h
- Refractory: maximize PPI, switch to [[vonoprazan]], surgical consult, evaluate Zollinger-Ellison
