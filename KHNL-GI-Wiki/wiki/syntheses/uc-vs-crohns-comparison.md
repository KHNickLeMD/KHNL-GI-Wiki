---title: "UC vs Crohn's Disease: Comparison"category: synthesistags: [uc, crohns, ibd, comparison]created: 2026-05-16updated: 2026-05-16sources: []---# UC vs Crohn's Disease: Comparison| Feature | Ulcerative Colitis | Crohn's Disease ||---|---|---|| **Location** | Colon and rectum only | Anywhere in GI tract (mouth to anus) || **Distribution** | Continuous, starting at rectum | Skip lesions (patchy, discontinuous) || **Depth of inflammation** | Mucosal only (superficial) | Transmural (full thickness) || **Rectal involvement** | Always (95%+) | Uncommon || **Terminal ileum** | Not involved | Commonly involved ("terminal ileitis") || **Granulomas** | Absent | Present (pathognomonic) || **Fistulas/abscesses** | Rare | Common || **Strictures** | Uncommon | Common || **Perianal disease** | Rare | Common (30–40%) || **Smoking** | Protective | Risk factor || **Bloody diarrhea** | Very common | Less common || **Abdominal mass** | Rare | Common (RLQ) || **Surgical cure** | Yes (colectomy) | No (recurs post-resection) || **Cancer risk** | Higher (pancolitis) | Elevated, but lower than UC || **ANCA** | pANCA positive (~60–70%) | Less common || **ASCA** | Less common | ASCA positive (~60–70%) || **Cobblestoning** | Absent | Present (endoscopy/imaging) || **Lead pipe colon** | Yes (chronic, loss of haustra) | No |## Key Mnemonics- **UC = Continuous, Colon, Curable** (surgically)- **Crohn's = Creeping fat, Cobblestones, Can-be-anywhere, Complications** (fistulas, strictures)## See Also- [[ulcerative-colitis]]- [[crohns-disease]]
