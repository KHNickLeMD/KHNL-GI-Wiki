## How This Guideline Rates Things — Two Separate Vocabularies

NCCN uses **two** schemes, both defined on document page CAT-1, and neither is GRADE. **The document numbers no recommendations**, so any numbering used on wiki pages is the wiki's own.

**1. Categories of evidence and consensus** — how strong the backing is:

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Category 1 | High-level evidence (≥1 randomized phase 3 trial, or high-quality robust meta-analysis) + uniform NCCN consensus (≥85% panel support) |
| Category 2A | Lower-level evidence + uniform NCCN consensus (≥85%) |
| Category 2B | Lower-level evidence + NCCN consensus ≥50% but <85% |
| Category 3 | Any level of evidence, with major NCCN disagreement |

**All recommendations are category 2A unless otherwise indicated** — printed on every algorithm page. Where a wiki page gives no category, the document gave none (i.e., 2A by default).

**2. Categories of preference** — how an intervention ranks among appropriate options:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Preferred** | Superior efficacy, safety and evidence; and, where relevant, affordability |
| **Other recommended** | Possibly somewhat less efficacious, more toxic, or on less mature data; or significantly less affordable for similar outcomes |
| **Useful in certain circumstances** | For selected patient populations, defined with the recommendation |

**All recommendations listed are considered appropriate** — the preference label ranks options, it does not make the lower ones inappropriate. ⚠ The two schemes are **independent**: a regimen can be *Preferred* and category 2A, or *Useful in certain circumstances* and category 1 (and in v3.2026 fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan is *Preferred* **and** moved from category 2A to **category 1**). Never collapse them into a single strength.

## Summary

The NCCN Gastric Cancer guideline covers workup, staging, and stage-directed treatment of gastric adenocarcinoma. Diagnosis is by [[upper-endoscopy|endoscopy]] with biopsy; staging combines endoscopic ultrasound, computed tomography (CT), positron emission tomography (PET)/CT, and diagnostic laparoscopy with peritoneal cytology for locally advanced disease. Biomarker testing — **HER2 (ERBB2), PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS), microsatellite instability–high (MSI-H)/mismatch repair–deficient (dMMR), and claudin 18.2** — is integral to treatment selection in advanced disease. Management is stage-based: endoscopic resection (endoscopic mucosal resection [EMR]/endoscopic submucosal dissection [ESD]) for early, low-risk T1a tumors meeting criteria; **perioperative chemotherapy (fluorouracil, leucovorin, oxaliplatin, docetaxel [FLOT])** with gastrectomy and D2 lymphadenectomy for resectable locally advanced disease; definitive chemoradiation/chemotherapy for unresectable disease; and biomarker-directed systemic therapy for metastatic disease.

Version 3.2026 reorganizes the systemic-therapy principles around HER2 status, makes **first-line trastuzumab** explicit for HER2-overexpressing (immunohistochemistry [IHC] 3+, or IHC 2+ with in situ hybridization [ISH]/fluorescence ISH [FISH] amplification) adenocarcinoma, elevates **fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan to a category 1** second-line/subsequent option for HER2-positive disease, adds **dostarlimab as a neoadjuvant option for MSI-H/dMMR** tumors, and clarifies that for MSI-H/dMMR tumors undergoing non-operative management, immunotherapy should be given for at least one year. Gastrectomy with appropriate lymphadenectomy remains the standard even after radiologic/endoscopic complete response to neoadjuvant immunotherapy, outside of prospective organ-preservation trials.

## Key Findings / Claims

- **Biomarker testing is integral.** HER2 (ERBB2), PD-L1 CPS, MSI-H/dMMR, and claudin 18.2 testing guide systemic therapy in advanced/metastatic gastric adenocarcinoma.
- **Early disease.** Selected early T1a tumors meeting size/histology/depth criteria are candidates for endoscopic resection (EMR/ESD); otherwise gastrectomy with D2 lymphadenectomy.
- **Resectable locally advanced disease.** Perioperative chemotherapy — **FLOT** preferred — with gastrectomy and D2 lymphadenectomy; perioperative/neoadjuvant immunotherapy is considered for MSI-H/dMMR tumors in multidisciplinary consultation (**dostarlimab** added as a neoadjuvant option).
- **MSI-H/dMMR organ preservation.** Radiologic/endoscopic complete responses to neoadjuvant immunotherapy do not reliably predict pathologic complete response; gastrectomy remains standard outside prospective trials. If non-operative management is pursued, immunotherapy should continue for **at least 1 year**.
- **First-line metastatic therapy.** HER2-overexpressing adenocarcinoma: add **trastuzumab** to first-line chemotherapy. HER2-negative: fluoropyrimidine + platinum chemotherapy, with **nivolumab or pembrolizumab** added based on PD-L1 CPS, and **zolbetuximab** for claudin 18.2-positive tumors.
- **Later-line therapy.** **Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan** is now a category 1 second-line/subsequent option for HER2-positive disease; other options include ramucirumab-based regimens, taxanes, irinotecan, and trifluridine/tipiracil.
- **Subcutaneous pembrolizumab** (pembrolizumab–berahyaluronidase alfa) may substitute for intravenous pembrolizumab.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- Algorithm-based guideline (category 2A by default), not a Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation (GRADE) systematic review; complements the American College of Gastroenterology (ACG) gastric-premalignant-conditions guideline (which governs precursor-lesion screening/surveillance).
- **Staging and expanded endoscopic-resection criteria overlap with other sources rather than conflicting.** The expanded ESD indication set NCCN attributes to Japanese and American guidelines is the same set [[asge-2023-esd]] gives, and that page owns it — [[gastric-adenocarcinoma]] cites ASGE for it, not NCCN. NCCN adds the surgical-adequacy framing: endoscopic resection of a cancer that is poorly differentiated, shows lymphovascular invasion (LVI), invades the deep submucosa, or has positive lateral/deep margins **counts as incomplete**, and gastrectomy with lymphadenectomy should be considered.
- **NCCN's own caution on EUS before endoscopic resection:** EUS is **not reliable for distinguishing superficial from deep submucosal invasion**, so endoscopic resection is itself the staging manoeuvre. EUS performed **after** chemotherapy or radiation also loses accuracy for post-treatment stage, and post-treatment biopsies may miss residual disease while still being informative.
- Reliability of imaging/endoscopy to confirm pathologic complete response in MSI-H/dMMR tumors is an open question; organ-preservation remains investigational.
