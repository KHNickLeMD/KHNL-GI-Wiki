## Summary

The NCCN Gastric Cancer guideline covers workup, staging, and stage-directed treatment of gastric adenocarcinoma. Diagnosis is by [[upper-endoscopy|endoscopy]] with biopsy; staging combines endoscopic ultrasound, CT, PET/CT, and diagnostic laparoscopy with peritoneal cytology for locally advanced disease. Biomarker testing — **HER2 (ERBB2), PD-L1 CPS, MSI-H/dMMR, and claudin 18.2** — is integral to treatment selection in advanced disease. Management is stage-based: endoscopic resection (EMR/ESD) for early, low-risk T1a tumors meeting criteria; **perioperative chemotherapy (FLOT)** with gastrectomy and D2 lymphadenectomy for resectable locally advanced disease; definitive chemoradiation/chemotherapy for unresectable disease; and biomarker-directed systemic therapy for metastatic disease.

Version 3.2026 reorganizes the systemic-therapy principles around HER2 status, makes **first-line trastuzumab** explicit for HER2-overexpressing (IHC 3+, or IHC 2+ with ISH/FISH amplification) adenocarcinoma, elevates **fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan to a category 1** second-line/subsequent option for HER2-positive disease, adds **dostarlimab as a neoadjuvant option for MSI-H/dMMR** tumors, and clarifies that for MSI-H/dMMR tumors undergoing non-operative management, immunotherapy should be given for at least one year. Gastrectomy with appropriate lymphadenectomy remains the standard even after radiologic/endoscopic complete response to neoadjuvant immunotherapy, outside of prospective organ-preservation trials.

## Key Findings / Claims

- **Biomarker testing is integral.** HER2 (ERBB2), PD-L1 CPS, MSI-H/dMMR, and claudin 18.2 testing guide systemic therapy in advanced/metastatic gastric adenocarcinoma.
- **Early disease.** Selected early T1a tumors meeting size/histology/depth criteria are candidates for endoscopic resection (EMR/ESD); otherwise gastrectomy with D2 lymphadenectomy.
- **Resectable locally advanced disease.** Perioperative chemotherapy — **FLOT** preferred — with gastrectomy and D2 lymphadenectomy; perioperative/neoadjuvant immunotherapy is considered for MSI-H/dMMR tumors in multidisciplinary consultation (**dostarlimab** added as a neoadjuvant option).
- **MSI-H/dMMR organ preservation.** Radiologic/endoscopic complete responses to neoadjuvant immunotherapy do not reliably predict pathologic complete response; gastrectomy remains standard outside prospective trials. If non-operative management is pursued, immunotherapy should continue for **at least 1 year**.
- **First-line metastatic therapy.** HER2-overexpressing adenocarcinoma: add **trastuzumab** to first-line chemotherapy. HER2-negative: fluoropyrimidine + platinum chemotherapy, with **nivolumab or pembrolizumab** added based on PD-L1 CPS, and **zolbetuximab** for claudin 18.2-positive tumors.
- **Later-line therapy.** **Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan** is now a category 1 second-line/subsequent option for HER2-positive disease; other options include ramucirumab-based regimens, taxanes, irinotecan, and trifluridine/tipiracil.
- **Subcutaneous pembrolizumab** (pembrolizumab–berahyaluronidase alfa) may substitute for IV pembrolizumab.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- Algorithm-based guideline (category 2A by default), not a GRADE systematic review; complements the ACG gastric-premalignant-conditions guideline already in the wiki (which governs precursor-lesion screening/surveillance).
- Reliability of imaging/endoscopy to confirm pathologic complete response in MSI-H/dMMR tumors is an open question; organ-preservation remains investigational.
