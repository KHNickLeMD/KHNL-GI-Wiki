## Summary

This guideline standardizes how to estimate and mitigate surgical risk in [[portal-hypertension|cirrhosis]]. It advances **cirrhosis-specific risk calculators (VOCAL-Penn Score)** over generic models, integrates assessment of **clinically significant portal hypertension (CSPH)** via [[noninvasive-liver-disease-assessment|liver stiffness]] + platelet count, and emphasizes preoperative optimization and selective transplant referral.

---

## Key Recommendations

- **Rec 1:** With unclear CSPH, use **liver stiffness measurement + platelet count** to rule in CSPH using validated thresholds.
- **Rec 2 (Strong):** With severe thrombocytopenia (**<50,000/mm³**) before invasive procedures, use **thrombopoietin receptor agonists**.
- **Rec 3:** In cirrhosis + CSPH with another TIPS indication (e.g., refractory [[ascites]]), suggest considering preoperative **TIPS**.
- **Rec 4:** For **major hepatic surgery**, refer to a high-volume liver/transplant center when feasible.

**Key concepts:**
- **VOCAL-Penn Score** (and MELD/MELD-Na, CTP, Mayo) estimate operative mortality; generic scores alone are inadequate.
- Very low **MELD (6–9)/CTP (5–6)** without CSPH or decompensation = lower risk.
- Consider **preoperative liver transplant evaluation** in selected elective patients, especially when predicted mortality **>15%**.
- Optimize before elective surgery: treat reversible insults (HBV/HCV/AIH), strict **alcohol/tobacco cessation**, **prehabilitation** for frailty/sarcopenia, and stay current on cirrhosis care (HCC screening, [[ascites]] and [[hepatic-encephalopathy]] optimization).

---

## Clinical Relevance

Shifts perioperative liver risk from CTP/MELD alone to VOCAL-Penn–anchored, CSPH-aware stratification with concrete pre-op optimization and TPO-agonist use for thrombocytopenia. Highly practical for surgical/anesthesia consults and pre-elective-surgery hepatology clinics. Connects to bleeding-risk physiology in [[cirrhosis-hemostasis]] and to [[liver-transplantation]] safety-net planning.
