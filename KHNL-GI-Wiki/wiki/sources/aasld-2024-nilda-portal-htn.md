## Summary

This guidance applies noninvasive tools — liver stiffness measurement (**LSM**), platelet count, and **spleen stiffness measurement (SSM)** — to detect **clinically significant portal hypertension (CSPH)** and identify patients who can safely avoid screening endoscopy, paralleling the Baveno framework. It positions HVPG and endoscopy as references rather than routine first tests.

---

## Key Points

- **Rule out CSPH / high-risk varices** (favorable Baveno-type criteria): **LSM <15 kPa with platelets >150 ×10⁹/L** identifies low risk — endoscopy can often be deferred.
- **Rule in CSPH:** higher LSM (≈≥20–25 kPa) with thrombocytopenia supports CSPH and elevated bleeding risk.
- **Spleen stiffness measurement (SSM)** adds discrimination in the indeterminate zone and is less affected by acute injury than LSM.
- Combine measures (LSM–spleen/platelet scores) to refine who needs screening [[upper-endoscopy]] for varices.

---

## Clinical Relevance

Lets clinicians noninvasively triage [[portal-hypertension]] severity and **safely omit endoscopy** in low-risk compensated patients, reserving HVPG/EGD for indeterminate or high-risk cases. Directly informs variceal screening within [[variceal-upper-gi-bleeding]] and complements the imaging-based NILDA guidance.
