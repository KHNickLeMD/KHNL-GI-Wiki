## Summary
- **Why:** resmetirom and semaglutide are approved for noncirrhotic metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) with F2–F3 fibrosis; fibrosis stage, not steatohepatitis alone, is the strongest predictor of liver-related and all-cause mortality in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD). The panel sets NIT thresholds for risk stratification, treatment initiation and response monitoring, limited to widely available tools — Fibrosis-4 Index (FIB-4), vibration-controlled transient elastography (VCTE) and Enhanced Liver Fibrosis (ELF). Magnetic resonance–based tests (MRE — expanded in the article as "magnetic resonance enterography"; magnetic resonance imaging–proton density fat fraction [MRI-PDFF]; MRE combined with FIB-4 [MEFIB]) were deliberately left out as costly and largely confined to academic or research settings.
- **Risk stratification:** FIB-4 first line; VCTE or ELF second line (ELF accepted as second line regardless of VCTE availability). Low risk = liver stiffness measurement (LSM) **<8 kPa** or ELF **<9.2** (ELF <9.0 also acceptable). Likely cirrhosis = LSM **≥20 kPa** or ELF **≥11.3** (Table 1 prints >20 / >11.3; the graphical abstract >20.0 / ≥11.3). The older ELF threshold of **7.7 "is no longer considered valid and should not be used"**.
- **Treatment window (noncirrhotic):** main window LSM **10–<15 kPa** or ELF **9.2–<10.5**; LSM **15–<20 kPa** or ELF **>10.5–<11.3** only with an additional NIT excluding cirrhosis; LSM **8–10 kPa** or ELF **9.0–9.2** reasonable in selected patients (multiple cardiometabolic risk factors [CMRFs]). Panel more comfortable starting at ELF **≥9.8** (the graphical abstract uses ELF 9.8–10.5 as its MASH F2–F3 treatment band). Drug choice by shared decision-making; semaglutide generally preferred in treatment-naïve patients with type 2 diabetes (T2D) or obesity; resmetirom preferred after prior glucagon-like peptide 1 receptor agonist (GLP-1RA) treatment; **no upfront combination**; sequential add-on acceptable.
- **Response monitoring:** after ~1 year of resmetirom (Figure 2: 48 weeks) or 72 weeks of semaglutide. Response = **≥30% fall in LSM** or **≥0.5-point fall in ELF**; progression = rise of >30% in LSM or >0.5 in ELF (text/Figure 2: ≥), confirmed by a second NIT. Progression on resmetirom → stop and switch; progression on semaglutide → add resmetirom and keep semaglutide for cardiometabolic benefit (5.10, Figure 2; 5.9, stop and switch, also passed). Neither response nor progression → continue, or individualize a switch/add-on.
- **Lifestyle (Figure 3, not voted):** Mediterranean-style diet, weight-loss targets by body weight, 150–300 min/week moderate or 75–150 min/week vigorous exercise, and protein/muscle-preservation measures for patients on GLP-1RA.

### Consensus points reported only in the text (no statement number or vote printed)
The treatment statements sit in Supplementary Table 5, which the article does not reproduce; these are the text's reports of the panel's position.
- **Second-line test (Noninvasive Risk Stratification, p. 2339):** FIB-4 the most frequently endorsed first-line test; VCTE the predominant second-line test. **All respondents** agreed ELF should be the second-line test **when VCTE is not available**; **87%** supported ELF as second line **regardless** of VCTE availability.
- **Complicated T2D (p. 2340):** reduced FIB-4 performance "may justify" VCTE or ELF as **first-line** testing (Figure 1 footnote says "patients with T2D", without "complicated").
- **ELF 7.7 (Discussion, p. 2343):** the previously used ELF threshold of 7.7 is no longer considered valid and should not be used.
- **Low-threshold treatment (pp. 2340, 2343):** in selected patients with multiple CMRFs, LSM **8–10 kPa** or ELF **9.0–9.2** may warrant treatment consideration "within an appropriate clinical context".
- **Agent choice (Pharmacologic Therapy, p. 2341):**
  - Pharmacologic therapy is given **in conjunction with lifestyle optimization**.
  - **Universal agreement:** in treatment-naïve patients with MASH who meet criteria, agent selection by **shared decision-making**.
  - Treatment-naïve MASH with coexisting **T2D or obesity** → **semaglutide generally preferred** (potential cardiometabolic benefit). Discussion (p. 2343) narrows this to patients "who have not been treated with a GLP1RA".
  - Already on **lower-dose semaglutide** for another indication → **dose escalation** to the dose approved for MASH can be considered, provided tolerability is maintained.
  - Previously treated with a **GLP-1RA for obesity or T2D** and still a MASH-treatment candidate → **resmetirom preferred**.
  - **Upfront combination** of semaglutide + resmetirom in treatment-naïve patients **not recommended** (no robust trial data); **sequential** strategy — adding the alternate agent after inadequate/suboptimal response to monotherapy — acceptable.
- **Timing of response assessment (Response Monitoring, p. 2341):** after approximately **1 year of optimized resmetirom** or **72 weeks of optimized semaglutide**. Response = **≥30% LSM reduction by VCTE** or **≥0.5-point ELF decrease**; on-treatment progression = **≥30% LSM increase** or **≥0.5-point ELF increase** (text uses ≥; Table 1 uses >). NIT results suggesting worsening should be **confirmed with a second, concordant NIT** before management decisions.
- **Worsening (p. 2341):** resmetirom — confirmed worsening supported discontinuation and an alternative regimen. Semaglutide — lower agreement on stopping (systemic cardiometabolic benefit); adding resmetirom while continuing semaglutide "considered reasonable, if such benefit is expected".
- **Neither response nor progression (p. 2341):** continuing the current regimen or switching to an alternative should be **individualized**.
- **Discordant NITs (Discussion, p. 2344, authors' view, not voted):** histology remains important to confirm stage or exclude a second liver disease; a pragmatic alternative is to **repeat a second-line NIT after ~6 months**. Prospective data lacking.
- **ELF response threshold** (≥0.5-point change) is acknowledged as **supported by limited evidence** (p. 2343).

### Graphical abstract — "The Roadmap for MASLD: From Risk Stratification to Management Action" (p. 2334, unnumbered)
- **Step 1 — who to screen for high-risk MASLD:** type 2 diabetes · medically complicated obesity · persistent liver enzyme elevation. First-line tool FIB-4:
  - **<1.3 (or <2.0 for age ≥65)** = low risk → maintain in primary care; retest in 1–3 years based on cardiometabolic risks (CMRs).
  - **≥1.3 (or ≥2.0 for age ≥65)** = high risk → step 2 testing (VCTE or ELF).
- **Step 2 — staging-to-action thresholds:**

| Stage | VCTE LSM (kPa) | ELF score | Action (graphical abstract) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Probable cirrhosis | >20.0 | ≥11.3 | Specialist referral for cirrhosis care; no resmetirom or semaglutide |
| Advanced fibrosis | 15.0–20.0 | 10.5–11.3 | Use a second NIT to exclude cirrhosis; if excluded, treat as MASH (F2–F3) |
| MASH F2–F3 | 10.0–15.0 | 9.8–10.5 | Refer to specialist; lifestyle intervention + eligible for resmetirom or semaglutide |
| At risk | 8.0–10.0 | 9.2\*–9.8 | Clinical judgement based on CMRs; treat as MASH (F2–F3) |
| Low risk | <8.0 | <9.2\* | Primary care management; lifestyle intervention + retest in 1–3 years based on CMRs |

\*Some regional protocols may use a lower threshold of 9.0.
- **Management goals:** weight loss **≥10%** to improve all pathologic features of MASH including fibrosis · Mediterranean pattern (more fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts, olive oil; strictly limit ultra-processed foods, saturated fats, added fructose) · 150–300 min/week moderate-intensity (or 75–150 min vigorous) exercise; resistance exercise 2–3 days a week · smoking cessation · total alcohol avoidance in advanced disease.

### Figure 1 — initial risk-stratification algorithm (p. 2339)
- **Entry:** T2D or obesity with one additional cardiometabolic risk (CMR) factor, **or** chronically elevated aminotransferases.
- **FIB-4 >1.3, or >2.0 if over 65 years old** (single cut-off; footnote *: for patients with T2D, VCTE or ELF can be considered as first-line test).
  - **No** → repeat testing within **1–3 years** (primary care setting).
  - **Yes** → move to second-line NIT (VCTE or ELF). Is a second-line test available outside hepatology or gastroenterology?
    - **No** → refer to gastroenterology or hepatology (Figure 2).
    - **Yes** → conduct second-line test (VCTE or ELF): **VCTE ≥8 kPa or ELF ≥9.2** (footnote **: in some clinical circumstances an ELF threshold of 9.0 is reasonable)?
      - **No** → repeat testing within 1–3 years (primary care).
      - **Yes** → refer to gastroenterology or hepatology.

### Figure 2 — algorithm for ELF, VCTE and medications (p. 2340)
Entry: clinical suspicion and assessment of at-risk MASH → obtain VCTE LSM **or** ELF.

| Band | VCTE LSM | ELF | Also required | Interpretation → action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | **<8 kPa** | **<9.2** | Absence of clinical or laboratory signs of portal hypertension or cirrhosis | Low likelihood of fibrosis stage ≥2 → retest in 1–3 years based on presence of CMRs; initiate lifestyle interventions; maximize comorbidity management based on CMRs |
| Treat | **≥10 and <15 kPa**\* | **≥9.2 and <10.5**\* | Absence of clinical or laboratory signs of portal hypertension or cirrhosis | High likelihood of fibrosis stage 2 or 3 → lifestyle optimization (all patients) **and** consider approved pharmacotherapy |
| Treat with confirmation | **≥15 and <20 kPa** | **>10.5 and <11.3** | **One additional NIT confirming absence of cirrhosis** **and** absence of clinical or laboratory signs of portal hypertension or cirrhosis | As above |
| Cirrhosis | **≥20 kPa** | **≥11.3** | — or any evidence of portal hypertension or cirrhosis | High likelihood of fibrosis stage 4 or cirrhosis → **no indication for MASH-directed pharmacotherapy** |

\*Footnote: VCTE **8–10 kPa** and ELF **9–9.2** are reasonable cut-points to initiate treatment **in selected patients**. In each band VCTE and ELF are joined by "OR". The figure assigns no band to VCTE 8–<10 kPa outside the footnote, and ELF exactly 10.5 falls in neither treatment band as printed.

- **Drug choice box:** regimen chosen by shared decision-making between patient and clinician; for treatment-naïve patients with **T2D and obesity**, semaglutide generally preferred (text p. 2341: "T2D or obesity").
- **Resmetirom (thyroid hormone receptor-β [THR-β] agonist) arm:**
  - **4 weeks:** assess liver enzymes.
  - **6 months:** consider VCTE to gauge response, **but not to determine efficacy or lack thereof**.
  - **3, 6 and 12 months:** assess liver enzymes.
  - **48 weeks:** assess for therapy efficacy or futility →
    - **Improving** (≥30% LSM reduction by VCTE **or** ≥0.5-point ELF reduction) → continue resmetirom.
    - **No progression** (stable or suboptimal response) → continue resmetirom and consider adding semaglutide.
    - **Worsening** (≥30% LSM increase **or** ≥0.5-point ELF increase; confirm progression with second NIT) → stop resmetirom; consider semaglutide or clinical trials.
- **Semaglutide (GLP-1 agonist) arm:** assess response; **72 weeks:** assess for therapy efficacy or futility →
  - **Improving** (same thresholds) → continue semaglutide.
  - **No progression** (stable or suboptimal response) → continue semaglutide and consider adding or switching to resmetirom.
  - **Worsening** (same thresholds; confirm with second NIT) → add resmetirom; continue semaglutide if cardiometabolic benefit is expected, or consider clinical trials.

### Figure 3 — lifestyle recommendations, patient-centered (p. 2342; not part of the Delphi vote)

| Domain | Recommendation (Figure 3) |
|---|---|
| Weight goal — MASLD with overweight/obesity | ≥5% total body weight loss to reduce liver fat · 7–10% to improve liver inflammation · ≥10% to improve fibrosis · consider bariatric procedures for obesity class II or III (classes not defined in the figure) |
| Weight goal — MASLD with normal weight | 3–5% total body weight loss to reduce liver fat |
| Diet | Mediterranean dietary pattern or similar plant-based diet; more fruits, vegetables, lentils, nuts, olive oil, unprocessed poultry and fish · limit ultra-processed food and saturated fats · avoid sugar-sweetened beverages |
| Activity | Increase daily physical activity; exercise **150–300 min/week moderate** or **75–150 min/week vigorous** intensity; individualized to needs, preferences, capacity, accessibility, affordability and health status |
| Smoking / alcohol | Avoid smoking · avoid alcohol (especially in advanced disease) |
| Patients on GLP-1RA (complementary) | Start each meal with **20–30 g protein** (eg, poultry, fish, low-fat dairy, legumes, tofu); total daily protein **1.0–1.5 g/kg body weight** · gradually increase moderate activity incl. **2–3 weekly resistance sessions (~30 min/session)** · sustainable long-term nutritional support with early dietitian involvement · proactively manage gastrointestinal adverse effects · monitor for macro- and micronutrient deficiencies |

- **Text (Lifestyle and Dietary Optimization, p. 2341):**
  - Lifestyle modification remains **first-line** management; eliminate excess alcohol and smoking; aerobic **plus** resistance training.
  - Sustained weight loss **≥10%** of baseline body weight is associated with meaningful improvement in steatosis, fibrosis and metabolic parameters; histologic benefit may occur with **>3%**.
  - Up to **40%** of semaglutide-induced weight loss may come from lean muscle, particularly without structured nutritional oversight; more than half of patients fail to meet minimal protein thresholds; vitamin D, potassium and calcium deficiencies common.
  - Protein: **>1.0 g/kg/d**, and **1.2–1.5 g/kg/d during active weight loss** (Figure 3 prints 1.0–1.5 g/kg).
  - Structured nutritional care (routine dietitian referral, defined protein targets, micronutrient monitoring, exercise prescription) as a core component of incretin therapy.

### Background figures (p. 2335, 2339)
- Global prevalence: MASLD ~34–38%, MASH ~5–7%; MASLD up to 60–70% in obesity or T2D, MASH with significant fibrosis up to 20–40% in these groups.
- Alcohol-associated liver disease (ALD) ~1% and metabolic dysfunction and alcohol-associated liver disease (MetALD) ~2% of the general population; estimates roughly double after adjusting for alcohol underreporting. US National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES): pure ALD (no CMRFs) 0.05–0.14%, pure MASLD (no alcohol history) 2.3–3.6%.
- Approval status (pp. 2341–2342): resmetirom approved in the United States and (conditional marketing authorization) the European Union; semaglutide in the United States and (semaglutide 2.4 mg, conditional) Canada — for noncirrhotic MASH with F2–F3 fibrosis, with diet and exercise.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

### Within the document
- **Competing thresholds all reached consensus.** ELF low risk <9.0 (1.3) **and** <9.2 (1.4); referral at ELF ≥9.2 (1.5) **and** ≥9.8 (1.6); treatment at ELF 9.2–10.5 "can be used to recommend" (2.1/2.2) **and** 9.8–11.3 "may be used to consider" (2.3/2.4); VCTE 8–15 kPa (3.1–3.3) **and** 10–15 kPa (3.4/3.5). Figure 2 settles on VCTE ≥10–<15 kPa and ELF ≥9.2–<10.5 as the main window, with 8–10 kPa / ELF 9–9.2 for selected patients; the Discussion (p. 2343) says the panel had "greater confidence" in an ELF threshold of 9.8 to reduce overtreatment, which Figure 2 does not use.
- **Upper treatment bound for ELF:** Figure 2 and statements 2.3/2.4 use <11.3 / 11.3; text p. 2339 says ELF "between 10.5 and 11.2" was supported. Abstract states the window as LSM **10 to 20 kPa** or ELF **9.2 to 11.3** "after exclusion of cirrhosis" for the whole range — it does not carry Figure 2's split (one additional NIT required only for 15–<20 kPa and >10.5–<11.3).
- **Cirrhosis cut-off sign:** Table 1 (2.5, 2.6, 3.8, 3.9) uses **>11.3** and **>20 kPa**; text (pp. 2340, 2342) and Figure 2 use **≥11.3** and **≥20 kPa**. VCTE band printed as 15–19.9 kPa (3.6/3.7), ">15 and <20 kPa" (text p. 2340) and "≥15 and <20 kPa" (Figure 2).
- **Graphical abstract vs Figure 2 (ELF treatment band):** the graphical abstract (p. 2334) puts the "MASH F2–F3 … eligible for resmetirom or semaglutide" band at ELF **9.8–10.5** and calls ELF **9.2–9.8** / VCTE **8–10 kPa** "at risk — clinical judgement based on CMRs; treat as MASH (F2–F3)"; Figure 2 puts the main treatment band at ELF **≥9.2–<10.5** and limits VCTE 8–10 kPa / ELF 9–9.2 to "selected patients". The graphical abstract's cirrhosis row uses VCTE **>20.0** but ELF **≥11.3**; FIB-4 cut-offs are "≥1.3 (≥2.0 for age ≥65)" there vs ">1.3 or >2.0 if over 65 years old" in Figure 1. Screening population: "type 2 diabetes · medically complicated obesity · persistent liver enzyme elevation" (graphical abstract) vs "T2D or obesity with one additional CMR or chronically elevated aminotransferases" (Figure 1).
- **Gaps in Figure 2:** VCTE 8–<10 kPa and ELF 10.5 exactly are not assigned to any box (the footnote covers 8–10 kPa / ELF 9–9.2 only "in selected patients").
- **Progression sign:** Table 1 (5.7–5.10, 5.12, 5.13) defines progression as ELF rise **>0.5** or LSM rise **>30%**; text (p. 2341) and Figure 2 use **≥0.5** and **≥30%**. Response is ≥30% / ≥0.5 throughout.
- **Timing of the resmetirom response check:** Figure 2 says **48 weeks**; statements 5.1/5.5/5.7/5.8/5.11–5.13 say "at least 1 y"; text says "approximately 1 year". For semaglutide: 5.3/5.6 and Figure 2 say **72 weeks**, but 5.7, 5.9–5.13 say "at least 1 y".
- **Progression on semaglutide:** 5.9 (stop and start an alternative; 70%, the lowest agreement in Table 1) and 5.10 (add resmetirom, continue semaglutide; 87%) both passed. Figure 2 follows 5.10 (or clinical trials).
- **Neither response nor progression:** 5.12 (continue; 87%) and 5.13 (consider a switch; 72%) both passed; the text says individualize; Figure 2 says continue **and** consider adding (resmetirom arm: add semaglutide; semaglutide arm: add or switch to resmetirom) — i.e., combination therapy as a sequential step, though upfront combination is not recommended.
- **Semaglutide preference population:** "T2D **or** obesity" (text p. 2341), "T2D **and** obesity" (Figure 2), and "obesity or T2D who have not been treated with a GLP1RA" (Discussion p. 2343).
- **First-line VCTE/ELF:** "patients with T2D" (Figure 1 footnote) vs "complicated T2D" (text p. 2340).
- **Protein target:** text >1.0 g/kg/d and 1.2–1.5 g/kg/d during active weight loss; Figure 3 prints 1.0–1.5 g/kg.
- **Abbreviation list** expands MRE as "magnetic resonance enterography" (also in the Discussion, p. 2342); other wiki sources (eg, [[aasld-2025-semaglutide-mash]]) use MRE for elastography.
- **Missing statements:** 42 initial statements were developed (some merged as redundant before round 2); Table 1 prints 30 final NIT statements, and its numbering skips 1.1, 1.2, all of section 4, 5.2 and 5.4; treatment recommendations and their votes are only in Supplementary Table 5, not reproduced in the article.

### With other wiki sources (US guidance governs where both speak)
- **ELF 7.7:** this consensus says ELF 7.7 is no longer valid and should not be used; [[aasld-2023-nafld]] lists ELF <7.7 as the rule-out cut-off for advanced fibrosis and cirrhosis, and [[aasld-2024-nilda-blood]] lists 7.7 as the lower/screening threshold. [[noninvasive-liver-disease-assessment]] currently prints ELF <7.7 as rule-out.
- **Treatment window, VCTE:** [[aasld-2025-semaglutide-mash]] and [[aasld-2024-resmetirom]] recommend VCTE **8–15 kPa** as the candidacy range; this consensus's Figure 2 narrows the main window to **≥10–<15 kPa** and treats 8–10 kPa as "selected patients" (statements 3.1–3.3 for 8–15 kPa also passed). The AASLD range governs the page.
- **Treatment window, ELF:** [[aasld-2025-semaglutide-mash]] ELF 9.2–10.5 (individualized 10.5–11.3) — matches Figure 2; [[aasld-2024-resmetirom]] ELF 9.2–10.4 (MAESTRO interquartile range, where imaging unavailable) — this consensus uses the same 9.2–<10.5 band for both drugs and makes ELF a co-equal second-line test.
- **VCTE 15–20 kPa:** [[aasld-2025-semaglutide-mash]] allows an individualized decision after excluding cirrhosis with another NIT, imaging, or platelets; this consensus also requires one additional NIT — consistent; [[aasld-2024-resmetirom]] gives no 15–20 kPa treatment band for resmetirom, while this consensus extends it to resmetirom (3.6).
- **Resmetirom response threshold:** [[aasld-2024-resmetirom]] suggests a change of **≥25% in VCTE** (or ≥20% MRE) at **12 months** as meaningful; this consensus uses **≥30%** VCTE or ≥0.5 ELF (Figure 2 at 48 weeks). AASLD considers **stopping** for worsening at 12 months and a **holistic** decision when neither better nor worse; this consensus says **discontinue and switch** after confirmed progression (5.8).
- **Semaglutide on progression:** [[aasld-2025-semaglutide-mash]] — NITs suggesting worsening or progression to cirrhosis → **stop semaglutide**; this consensus (5.10, Figure 2) → **add resmetirom and continue semaglutide** if cardiometabolic benefit expected (5.9, stop, also passed). AASLD governs.
- **Combination:** [[aasld-2025-semaglutide-mash]] states resmetirom + semaglutide 2.4 mg/week has not been studied, and [[aasld-2024-resmetirom]] gives insufficient data on adding resmetirom to an existing GLP-1RA; this consensus recommends against **upfront** combination but endorses sequential add-on (5.10, Figure 2) — no AASLD statement on sequencing.
- **Blood tests for monitoring:** [[aasld-2024-nilda-blood]] Guidance Statement 8 suggests **against** blood-based noninvasive liver disease assessment (NILDA) to follow progression, stability or regression of histologic stage; this consensus uses ELF change (≥0.5) to define response and progression on therapy and itself notes the ELF threshold rests on limited evidence. [[aasld-2025-semaglutide-mash]] also lists ELF decrease ≥0.5 as a response marker at 72 weeks.
- **FIB-4 repeat interval:** [[aasld-2023-nafld]] Guidance Statement 12 — repeat FIB-4 every **1–2 y** in prediabetes/T2D or ≥2 metabolic risk factors; Figure 1 here says repeat within **1–3 years**. AASLD governs.
- **Entry population for FIB-4:** [[aasld-2023-nafld]] applies FIB-4 as primary risk assessment broadly; Figure 1 starts from T2D or obesity plus one additional CMR, or chronically elevated aminotransferases.
- **Referral threshold:** Figure 1 refers at VCTE ≥8 kPa or ELF ≥9.2; [[aasld-2023-nafld]] considers direct referral when FIB-4 >2.67 or aminotransferases are persistently (>6 mo) above normal, and refers suspected advanced NASH or discordant NITs to a specialist (Guidance Statement 14).
- **Protein on GLP-1RA:** [[aasld-2025-semaglutide-mash]] 1.2–1.5 g/kg/day — matches this text's active-weight-loss target; Figure 3's 1.0–1.5 g/kg is lower at the bottom end.
- **Weight-loss targets:** consistent with [[aga-2021-lifestyle-modification-nafld]] (≥5% steatosis, ≥7% nonalcoholic steatohepatitis [NASH] resolution, ≥10% fibrosis; 3–5% in normal-weight nonalcoholic fatty liver disease) and its 150–300 / 75–150 min/week exercise targets; Figure 3 frames the middle band as 7–10% "to improve liver inflammation".
- **Discordant NITs:** [[aasld-2023-nafld]] (Guidance Statement 14) — refer to a specialist; this consensus adds repeating a second-line NIT at ~6 months as a pragmatic option.
