## Summary

This is the paper that defines **polysomy cytogenetically** for pancreatobiliary brushings, and that replaced the bladder-cancer probe set with a purpose-built one.

Biliary fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) had been performed with **UroVysion** (Abbott Molecular), a probe set designed for **bladder cancer** — chromosome enumeration probes (CEP) for chromosomes 3, 7 and 17 plus a locus probe for **9p21 (CDKN2A)**. The authors reasoned that the chromosomal regions altered in pancreatobiliary tumours are not the same ones, and set out to build a tailored set.

**Three phases.** *Phase 1*: 14 candidate probes tested on 29 formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded resections (15 cholangiocarcinoma, 14 pancreatic adenocarcinoma); the combination **1q21, 7p12, 8q24 and 9p21** detected cancer cells with **93% sensitivity and 100% specificity** in tissue and became the **pancreatobiliary (PB) FISH** set. *Phase 2*: cut-off values determined on the earliest **89 brushings**. *Phase 3*: cut-offs applied to a separate **validation cohort of 183 brushings**.

In validation, **PB FISH polysomy detected malignancy with 64.7% sensitivity against UroVysion's 45.9% and routine cytology's 18.8%** (both P < .001), at similar specificity (**92.9% / 90.8% / 100.0%**) — a 19% absolute increase in cancer detection over UroVysion. The gain held in the population where it matters most: among **primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC)** patients, **62% vs 41%** (P = .008).

## Key Findings / Claims

### The probe set

| Locus | Fluorophore | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **1q21** | Spectrum Gold | |
| **7p12** | Spectrum Green | |
| **8q24** | Spectrum Aqua | |
| **9p21** (CDKN2A) | Spectrum Red | carried over from UroVysion; **loss** of 9p21 is the informative change |

- Replaces **UroVysion** (CEP 3, CEP 7, CEP 17, 9p21), which was designed for bladder cancer.
- Selected from 14 candidates on probe signal quality, mean copy number per cell, frequency of gain/loss in tumour cells, and sensitivity — individually, 1q21 gain had the best performance (sensitivity 90.0%, specificity 100%, AUC 0.95 at an 8% cut-off) and 9p21 homozygous loss the best loss signal (83.3%, 100%, AUC 0.94 at 10%).

### FISH cell types and cut-off values (Figure 3) — the definitions the guidelines omit

| FISH cell type | Result | Signal pattern definition | Cut-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Disomy** | Negative | 2 copies of each probe | n/a |
| **Polysomy** | **Positive** | **≥3 copies of ≥2 probes, excluding tetrasomy** | **≥4 cells on the entire slide** |
| Tetrasomy | Equivocal | exactly 4 copies of each probe | ≥10% of cells\* |
| Single locus gain with 9p21 loss | Equivocal | ≥3 copies of one locus, **0–1 copy of 9p21**, 2 copies of the other probes | ≥5% of cells\* |
| Homozygous 9p21 loss | Equivocal | **0 copies of 9p21**, 2 copies of the other 3 probes | ≥5% of cells\* |
| Single locus gain — 1q21, 8q24 or 9p21 | Equivocal | ≥3 copies of 1q21, 8q24 **or** 9p21, 2 copies of the others | ≥8% of cells\* |
| Single locus gain — 7p12 | Equivocal | ≥3 copies of 7p12, 2 copies of the others | ≥10% of cells\* |

\* Based on 100-cell enumeration. **Only polysomy is a positive result**; every other abnormality is **equivocal**.

**The polysomy cut-off is an absolute count, not a proportion** — ≥4 polysomic cells anywhere on the slide, **regardless of the total number of cells present**. In the 89-specimen cut-off cohort this gave sensitivity **57%**, specificity **90%**.

### How the slide is actually read — the rules that change the count

- **100 consecutive epithelial cells** are enumerated systematically. **A maximum of 5 cells from any group of disomic cells** counts toward the 100 — otherwise sheets of benign cells consume the count.
- Then a **post-enumeration scan of the remainder of the slide** under the DAPI filter, targeting nuclei most likely to be abnormal (enlarged, irregular border). **Polysomic cells found on this scan are added to the total**; **non-polysomic abnormalities found on it are disregarded** (their frequencies come only from the 100-cell count).
- **A cell showing both polysomy and homozygous 9p21 loss is classified as polysomic.**
- **9p21 loss is the overlap discriminator.** Loss of 9p21 is unexpected in benign cells, so it distinguishes a single genuinely abnormal nucleus from two overlapping benign ones. Presence of 9p21 signals in FISH-negative cells was verified in every case as a negative control.
- **Specimen adequacy: ≥100 epithelial cells with acceptable probe signal quality.**

### Validation performance (183 brushings; 85 cancers within 2 years)

| Test | Sensitivity | Specificity |
|---|---|---|
| **PB FISH polysomy** | **64.7%** | 92.9% |
| UroVysion polysomy | 45.9% | 90.8% |
| Routine cytology (positive) | 18.8% | 100.0% |

- Both comparisons P < .001. **19% absolute increase in cancer detection** over UroVysion.
- Sensitivity by cancer type (PB FISH polysomy): **cholangiocarcinoma 62%** (41/66), pancreatic carcinoma 83% (5/6), metastases to the pancreatobiliary tract 67% (4/6).
- **Positive routine cytology was 100% predictive** — all 16 patients with positive cytology had cancer within 2 years.
- Independent predictors of malignancy on multivariable analysis: **polysomy on PB FISH (OR 13.96)**, **mass on cross-sectional imaging (OR 13.91)**, positive routine cytology (OR not estimable, zero cell count), and **absence of PSC (OR 0.18 for PSC)**.
- The validation cohort was **62.3% PSC** — i.e. predominantly a surveillance population, which is why PSC lowers the odds of cancer here (lower prevalence), not the test's accuracy.

### In PSC specifically

- **PB FISH 62% vs UroVysion 41% sensitivity** (P = .008).
- In the **101 PSC patients presenting without a mass lesion**, PB FISH detected **19% more cancers** than UroVysion while maintaining high specificity.
- Context the authors cite: pooled UroVysion performance in PSC across 8 studies is **51% sensitivity, 93% specificity**, with those authors concluding more sensitive methods are needed.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- ⚠ **This qualifies, but does not resolve, the page's standing ACG-vs-ASGE contradiction about FISH in PSC.** [[acg-2023-biliary-strictures]] says FISH's performance is *attenuated* in PSC; [[asge-2023-indeterminate-biliary-strictures]] anticipates a *higher* role. This paper shows PB FISH **outperforming UroVysion within PSC** (62% vs 41%) — which supports ASGE's direction of travel — but it is **tier 2 and compares two probe sets, not PSC against non-PSC strictures**. Both tier-1 guidelines keep the page; nothing was overwritten.
- ⚠ **The PSC odds ratio is about prevalence, not accuracy.** The multivariable OR of **0.18** for PSC reflects the lower cancer prevalence in a surveillance population, and the paper says so. It must not be read as the test performing worse in PSC.
- **Development study, retrospective, single institution (Mayo Clinic), and the authors say so:** "additional data from clinical practice are needed to confirm these preliminary results." Cut-offs were set in one cohort and tested in another from the same institution; there is no external validation.
- **Funding:** the work was supported by **Abbott Molecular Inc.**, the manufacturer of both the UroVysion and the pancreatobiliary probes.
- **Cancer status was defined by a composite reference standard** that itself includes **UroVysion polysomy plus imaging** as one route to "clinicopathologic proof of malignancy" — the comparator test partly defines the outcome it is measured against.
