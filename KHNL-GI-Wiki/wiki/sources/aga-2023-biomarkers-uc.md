# AGA Clinical Practice Guideline: Biomarkers for Management of Ulcerative Colitis (2023)

## Bibliographic Info

- **Year:** 2023
- **Journal:** Gastroenterology (AGA Clinical Practice Guideline)
- **Type:** GRADE-based guideline

---

## Summary

The UC companion guideline defines the role of **CRP, fecal calprotectin, and fecal lactoferrin** in monitoring established [[ulcerative-colitis]], supporting a biomarker- and symptom-based strategy over symptom monitoring alone.

---

## Key Recommendations

- Use a **biomarker- and symptom-based monitoring** strategy rather than symptoms alone.
- In patients in **symptomatic remission:** **fecal calprotectin <150 µg/g**, **normal fecal lactoferrin**, and/or **normal CRP** rule out active inflammation and support **avoiding routine endoscopy**.
- In patients with **active symptoms:** **fecal calprotectin >150 µg/g** (or elevated lactoferrin/CRP) supports active inflammation and treatment adjustment; discordant results (symptoms without biomarker elevation) warrant endoscopic confirmation.

---

## Clinical Relevance

Integrates stool and serum biomarkers (calprotectin <150 to rule out, >150 to rule in) into [[ulcerative-colitis]] treat-to-target monitoring, limiting unnecessary colonoscopy. Parallels the Crohn's biomarker guideline within the [[inflammatory-bowel-disease]] framework.
