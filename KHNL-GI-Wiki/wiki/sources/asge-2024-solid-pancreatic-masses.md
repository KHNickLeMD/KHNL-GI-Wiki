## Summary

This guideline addresses tissue acquisition and palliation for solid pancreatic masses using [[endoscopic-ultrasound|EUS]]. The headline change: **EUS fine-needle biopsy (FNB) needles are preferred over fine-needle aspiration (FNA)** for diagnosis.

---

## Key Recommendations (conditional)

- **EUS-FNB over FNA** for tissue acquisition of solid pancreatic masses (higher diagnostic yield/adequacy, more tissue for ancillary testing; fewer passes).
- Reasonable to use **22-gauge** needles; **rapid on-site evaluation (ROSE)** is not mandatory when FNB is used.
- For malignant distal biliary obstruction from a pancreatic head mass, **self-expandable metal stents** over plastic stents for durable drainage (coordinate with [[ercp]]).

---

## Clinical Relevance

Cements **EUS-FNB as the first-choice sampling technique** for solid pancreatic masses, reducing reliance on ROSE, and supports metal stents for malignant biliary obstruction. Core to the pancreatic-mass diagnostic pathway and adjacent to [[pancreatic-cysts]] evaluation.
