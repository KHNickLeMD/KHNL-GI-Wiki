## Summary

This guideline updates the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of [[hepatic-encephalopathy]] (HE) in [[portal-hypertension|cirrhosis]], spanning covert/minimal HE (CHE/MHE) through overt HE (OHE). It reframes HE as a [[disorders-of-gut-brain-interaction|gut–brain axis]] disorder, emphasizes that serum ammonia should **not** drive diagnosis or titration, and strengthens the role of nutrition, [[rifaximin]], and shunt/TIPS-directed strategies. It also argues that HE burden is under-weighted in transplant prioritization, supporting earlier [[liver-transplantation]] evaluation.

---

## Key Recommendations

**Diagnosis (CHE/MHE):**
- Use a **single-test strategy** over a 2-test combination for MHE/CHE (psychometric HE score, Stroop/EncephalApp, animal naming, etc.).
- **Do not** use serum ammonia alone to diagnose HE; CHE is defined by *excluding* OHE in the right clinical context. Isolated asterixis alone is not OHE.
- In cirrhosis + confusion without new focal deficits, routine brain imaging is not required; a normal ammonia should prompt search for **alternative diagnoses**.

**Treatment of OHE:**
- **Lactulose** is first-line to improve outcomes and prevent recurrence (*strong*). High-volume PEG (~4 L) is an alternative.
- Add **[[rifaximin]]** to lactulose for acute OHE and to prevent recurrence; in patients on lactulose maintenance with recurrent episodes, adding rifaximin is *strongly* recommended.
- Titrate lactulose to **2–3 soft BMs/day** outpatient (Bristol Stool Scale + frequency). Consider **zinc** if low serum zinc; second-line BCAA/LOLA/l-carnitine in selected patients.
- A thorough evaluation for **precipitants** (bleeding, infection, sedatives, metabolic derangement) is necessary; if no recovery by 48–72 h, reassess for alternative causes.

**Nutrition:**
- Protein target **1.2–1.5 g/kg/day**; **do not protein-restrict** (worsens muscle breakdown). See [[nutrition-in-liver-disease]].
- **BCAA supplementation** if protein needs unmet by food; **late-night snack** and **exercise** to reduce frailty/falls.

**Shunt / TIPS / transplant:**
- Consider **shunt embolization** in refractory HE with adequate hepatic function (SPSS).
- Start **rifaximin 14 days before elective TIPS** and continue ≥6 months; consider embolizing extrahepatic collaterals at TIPS to reduce post-TIPS HE.
- For multiple HE episodes with **MELD <15**, evaluate for **living-donor [[liver-transplantation]]**.

**Outpatient:** Serum ammonia should **not** guide initiation/titration; multidisciplinary medication reconciliation at discharge; HE therapy may be de-escalated stepwise after clinical recompensation.

---

## Clinical Relevance

Reinforces lactulose → add rifaximin as the backbone, formally deprioritizes ammonia monitoring, and integrates nutrition/frailty and TIPS-period prophylaxis. Relevant to inpatient decompensation management alongside [[acute-on-chronic-liver-failure]] and [[ascites]] workups.
