## Summary

This guidance covers **imaging-based** [[noninvasive-liver-disease-assessment]] of hepatic fibrosis and steatosis — principally vibration-controlled transient elastography (**VCTE/FibroScan**) with **controlled attenuation parameter (CAP)**, point/2D shear-wave elastography, and **magnetic resonance elastography (MRE)** / MRI-PDFF. It standardizes how liver stiffness measurement (LSM) and steatosis quantification stage disease and triage who needs biopsy.

---

## Key Points

- **LSM** (kPa range ~2.5–75) estimates fibrosis: low LSM helps **rule out** advanced fibrosis/cACLD, high LSM helps **rule in**; intermediate values warrant a second test (MRE, blood-based scores) or biopsy.
- Use the **XL probe** in patients with higher BMI (improves reliability; tends to give lower values than M probe — interpret accordingly); ensure adequate skin-to-capsule distance and fasting.
- **CAP** grades hepatic **steatosis**; **MRI-PDFF** is the most accurate noninvasive steatosis quantifier; **MRE** is the most accurate stiffness method (useful when VCTE is unreliable).
- Combine imaging with **blood-based biomarkers** (FIB-4) in sequential algorithms (e.g., SAFE) to reduce indeterminate results.

---

## Clinical Relevance

Operationalizes elastography-first fibrosis/steatosis staging that minimizes biopsy, central to MASLD/[[nafld-masld]] and chronic viral hepatitis workups. Pairs with the portal-hypertension NILDA guidance and feeds risk stratification for [[hcc-surveillance]].
