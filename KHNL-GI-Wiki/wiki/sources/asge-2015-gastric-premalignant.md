## Summary

ASGE Standards of Practice statement (GRADE) on the endoscopic management of premalignant and malignant gastric conditions — a revision/expansion of the 2006 ASGE upper-GI premalignant-surveillance document. Covers gastric polyps (fundic gland, hyperplastic, adenomatous; sporadic vs FAP/Lynch), [[gastric-intestinal-metaplasia|gastric intestinal metaplasia (GIM)]] and metaplastic atrophic gastritis, pernicious anemia, gastric carcinoids (types 1–4), [[gastric-adenocarcinoma|gastric adenocarcinoma]] (biopsy, EUS staging, palliation of malignant gastric outlet obstruction), gastric MALT lymphoma, and gastric [[gastrointestinal-stromal-tumor|GISTs]]/subepithelial lesions.

Core themes: gastric polyp histology cannot be predicted endoscopically → biopsy/resect; hyperplastic and adenomatous polyps carry malignant potential (FGPs do not, except in FAP); GIM and pernicious anemia confer increased gastric-cancer risk and warrant individualized surveillance; gastric cancer requires adequate biopsy sampling (≥7) and EUS for locoregional staging; EUS ± FNA is the cornerstone for evaluating gastric subepithelial lesions. Note this is an **older** document — for current gastric-premalignant surveillance the page's primary recommendations follow [[acg-2025-gastric-premalignant|ACG 2025]], [[aga-2020-gastric-intestinal-metaplasia|AGA 2020 GIM]], [[aga-2021-atrophic-gastritis|AGA 2021 atrophic gastritis]], and [[aga-2026-gastric-polyps|AGA 2026 gastric polyps]]; this source contributes the endoscopic-technique and staging detail.

## Key Findings / Claims

**Recommendations (verbatim, with GRADE quality of evidence; ⊕⊕⊕⊕ high, ⊕⊕⊕◯ moderate, ⊕⊕◯◯ low, ⊕◯◯◯ very low; "recommend" = strong, "suggest" = conditional/weak):**

1. We recommend solitary gastric polyps undergo biopsy or be resected when possible. (strong, ⊕⊕⊕⊕)
2. We suggest polypectomy of fundic gland polyps 1 cm or larger, hyperplastic polyps 0.5 cm or larger, and adenomatous polyps of any size when possible. (conditional, ⊕⊕◯◯)
3. We suggest surveillance endoscopy 1 year after removing adenomatous gastric polyps. (conditional, ⊕⊕◯◯)
4. In the setting of multiple polyps, we recommend biopsy or resection of the largest polyps and representative biopsy specimens be taken from others. (strong, ⊕⊕⊕⊕)
5. In the setting of multiple hyperplastic or adenomatous polyps, we suggest systematic sampling of the surrounding nonpolypoid gastric mucosa to assess for H pylori and metaplastic atrophic gastritis. (conditional, ⊕⊕◯◯)
6. We suggest sampling and, when feasible, resection of large gastric polyps in patients with FAP to confirm histology and to assess for dysplasia. (conditional, ⊕⊕◯◯)
7. We suggest surveillance endoscopy for patients with GIM who are at increased risk of gastric cancer due to ethnic background or family history. Optimal surveillance intervals have not been extensively studied and should be individualized. (conditional, ⊕⊕◯◯)
8. We recommend endoscopic resection and surveillance endoscopy for patients with confirmed GIM with HGD when feasible. (strong, ⊕⊕⊕◯)
9. We suggest endoscopy within 6 months of the diagnosis of pernicious anemia or the development of upper GI symptoms in patients with pernicious anemia. (conditional, ⊕⊕◯◯)
10. We recommend EUS for local staging of gastric carcinoids. (strong, ⊕⊕⊕◯)
11. We suggest endoscopic resection of small (<1 cm) type 1 and type 2 gastric carcinoids that do not demonstrate aggressive features such as angioinvasion, muscular wall invasion, high proliferative index, and/or metastatic disease and endoscopic surveillance thereafter every 1 to 2 years. We suggest endoscopic removal for type 3 and 4 gastric carcinoids (isolated and <1 cm in diameter). (conditional, ⊕⊕◯◯)
12. We recommend at least 7 biopsy samples be obtained of gastric masses or the heaped-up edges of ulcers suspicious for gastric adenocarcinoma. (strong, ⊕⊕⊕⊕)
13. We recommend EUS and when applicable, EUS-FNA to locally stage gastric cancer. (strong, ⊕⊕⊕◯)
14. We recommend endoscopically placed self-expanding metal stents for the palliation of malignant gastric outlet obstruction due to gastric cancer in patients with poor performance status or nonoperable anatomy. (strong, ⊕⊕⊕◯)
15. We recommend EUS with or without FNA in the evaluation of gastric submucosal lesions. (strong, ⊕⊕⊕⊕)
16. We suggest annual EUS surveillance of GISTs smaller than 2 cm if surgical resection is not performed to determine progression of size or change in echo features. (conditional, ⊕⊕◯◯)

**Other points:**
- Gastric polyp histology cannot be reliably distinguished by endoscopic appearance → biopsy/polypectomy when detected. 70%–90% of gastric epithelial polyps are FGPs or hyperplastic.
- **FGPs:** associated with long-term PPI use; **not** an increased cancer risk in the absence of FAP. **Hyperplastic polyps:** dysplasia/focal cancer in 5%–19%; risk factors for dysplasia = size >1 cm, pedunculated morphology. **Adenomatous polyps:** malignant potential; recurrence up to 2.6% after complete endoscopic excision; cancer in 1.3% on follow-up. Endoscopic submucosal resection reduces recurrence vs EMR but increases adverse events.
- Gastric polyps occur with H pylori and environmental metaplastic atrophic gastritis → polypectomy + treat H pylori.
- Gastric subepithelial/submucosal lesions arising from the muscularis propria can be removed endoscopically only by practitioners with dedicated training and skill.

## Relevance to Wiki
- Updates [[gastric-polyps]] — additive endoscopic thresholds: resect FGP ≥1 cm, hyperplastic ≥0.5 cm, adenoma any size; surveillance 1 y after adenoma removal; sample surrounding nonpolypoid mucosa (H pylori + atrophic gastritis); largest-polyp + representative sampling when multiple.
- Updates [[gastric-intestinal-metaplasia]] / [[gastric-premalignant-conditions]] — individualized surveillance for high-risk GIM (ethnicity/family history); endoscopic resection + surveillance for GIM with HGD.
- Updates [[atrophic-gastritis]] (pernicious-anemia context) — endoscopy within 6 months of pernicious-anemia diagnosis or new upper-GI symptoms (gastric carcinoid/adenocarcinoma risk).
- Updates [[gastric-adenocarcinoma]] — ≥7 biopsies of masses/ulcer edges; EUS ± FNA for locoregional staging; SEMS for palliation of malignant gastric outlet obstruction.
- Updates [[gastroenteropancreatic-neuroendocrine-tumors|gastric carcinoid]] management — EUS staging; resection criteria by type (1/2 <1 cm without aggressive features; isolated type 3/4 <1 cm).
- Cross-links [[endoscopic-ultrasound]] and [[gastrointestinal-stromal-tumor|GIST/subepithelial lesions]] — EUS ± FNA for subepithelial lesions; annual EUS surveillance of GIST <2 cm if unresected.

## Contradictions / Open Questions
- **Older than** the current gastric guidelines on the wiki ([[acg-2025-gastric-premalignant|ACG 2025]], [[aga-2020-gastric-intestinal-metaplasia|AGA 2020 GIM]], [[aga-2021-atrophic-gastritis|AGA 2021]], [[aga-2026-gastric-polyps|AGA 2026 gastric polyps]]). Where they specify GIM risk-stratification, staging systems (OLGA/OLGIM), and surveillance intervals, those newer documents govern the page's primary recommendations; this 2015 source contributes endoscopic technique/sampling and staging/palliation detail only — it does not overwrite the newer surveillance schemes.
- Predates routine endoscopic submucosal dissection (ESD) adoption for early gastric cancer in Western practice — see [[asge-2023-esd|ASGE 2023 ESD]] for current early-gastric-cancer resection criteria.
