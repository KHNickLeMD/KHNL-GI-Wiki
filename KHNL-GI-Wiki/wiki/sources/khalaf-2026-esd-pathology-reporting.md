## Summary
International consensus from **42 experts** (28 gastrointestinal pathologists, 14 therapeutic endoscopists; 15 countries, 5 continents) on how to measure and report invasive carcinoma in endoscopic submucosal dissection (ESD) specimens of the gastrointestinal tract (Abstract; Methods, p2). **56 statements reached consensus across seven domains** (Results, p3). The article prints a **24-bullet summary of the final recommendations (Table 1, p3)**; the statement-level wording, agreement ratings, interquartile ranges (IQRs) and rankings for all 56 statements are in online supplementary tables 2–5 (Results, p3). No GRADE or other evidence grading is applied; agreement is reported per domain.

Core standards: submucosal invasion (SMI) depth as a linear measurement in micrometres (rounded to the nearest 100 µm in the text), with the categorical submucosal levels 1–3 (SM1–SM3) used **only when the specimen includes muscularis propria (MP)**; SMI breadth in millimetres to 0.1 mm as an adjunct; a **positive margin = direct tumor contact with the inked resection surface**; hematoxylin and eosin (H&E) as the baseline stain with selective ancillary stains; tumor budding (TB) graded per the International Tumour Budding Consensus Conference (ITBCC); and lymph node metastasis (LNM) risk judged from **composite** histology, not one factor. Clinical domain: margins interpreted in context of tumor biology; positive deep margins in T1b or otherwise high-risk lesions generally support surgical referral, positive lateral margins in dysplasia or non-invasive lesions may be managed endoscopically or with surveillance (Table 1).

Expert-opinion consensus (Delphi); the authors note dependence on expert opinion, panel composition and response bias (Validity, reliability and limitations, p7). Evidence on SMI breadth is limited and mostly colorectal (Discussion, p5).

## Key Findings / Claims

### Methods (Methods, p2; Results, p2–3; Figure 1, p4)
- Modified Delphi, DELPHISTAR reporting framework; **5 iterative survey rounds, January–September 2025** (Methods, p2).
  - Round 1: open-ended responses · Round 2: rating of drafted statements · Rounds 3–4: revision and re-rating of statements not reaching consensus · Round 5: final prioritization (Methods, p2).
- Prompt items from a systematic literature review plus round-1 responses, refined by a steering committee (Methods, p2).
- Rating: **5-point Likert scale** (1 = strongly disagree; 5 = strongly agree). **Consensus predefined as ≥80% of responding panellists scoring ≥4** (Methods, p2).
- Subgroup voting: pathology statements voted by pathologists; clinical statements by endoscopists (Methods, p2).
- Final prioritization reported as mean ± standard deviation (SD) on a 10-point scale (Methods, p2).
- Panel: 57 invited (Results text, p2), 44 responded to round 1, 2 withdrew after round 1 → **42 completed** (28 pathologists, 14 endoscopists) (Results, p2).
- Figure 1 (p4) flow: 58 identified/invited → 57 screened → 1 excluded → round 1 n = 44 → 2 withdrew.
  - Pathologists: round 2, 54 statements voted · round 3, 18 · round 4, 3 · final round 5, prioritization of **50** consensus statements.
  - Endoscopists: round 2, 6 statements voted · round 3, 2 · final round 4, prioritization of **6** consensus statements.

- Results-text highlights (p3): SM1–SM3 only when MP present, otherwise micrometres, "as depth remains essential for composite lymph-node risk models" · measurement protocols standardize depth in micrometres (rounded to 100 µm), complete embedding, regular parallel sectioning, orientation preservation, **"while advising against routine desmin/caldesmon use"** · **intermediate- to high-grade TB (ITBCC grade BD2 or BD3) and poor differentiation confirmed as key predictors of LNM** · staining: H&E standard, selective ancillary stains (D2-40, CD31/34, elastic) for lymphovascular invasion (LVI) and dual immunohistochemistry (IHC) when architecture is distorted · clinical evaluation: integrate lesion biology and procedural factors in margin interpretation; refer positive deep margins for multidisciplinary review.

| # | Domain | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| T1.1 | SMI depth | Report SMI depth as a **linear measurement in micrometres (µm)**. |
| T1.2 | SMI depth | **SM1–SM3 classification only when the specimen includes muscularis propria.** |
| T1.3 | SMI depth | Invasion discontinuous or spanning multiple slides → report the **deepest point as a single linear measurement**. |
| T1.4 | SMI breadth | Report breadth of SMI in **millimetres, rounded to the nearest 0.1 mm**. |
| T1.5 | SMI breadth | Routine measurement of the **area** of SMI is **not recommended**. |
| T1.6 | Margin evaluation | **Positive margin = direct tumor contact with the inked resection surface.** |
| T1.7 | Margin evaluation | Margin status equivocal → deeper sections and ancillary staining **may** be used to clarify tumor involvement. |
| T1.8 | Margin evaluation | Recognize cautery artefact and specimen fragmentation as potential sources of diagnostic difficulty in margin assessment. |
| T1.9 | Margin evaluation | Document tumor **proximity** to inked margins when specimen orientation allows. |
| T1.10 | Staining methods | **H&E is the standard for invasion depth.** |
| T1.11 | Staining methods | Muscularis mucosae (MM) fragmented/unclear → use smooth muscle markers (desmin, smooth muscle actin [SMA], caldesmon). |
| T1.12 | Staining methods | Invasion difficult to detect (poor differentiation / indistinct interface) → cytokeratin stains (pan-cytokeratin, AE1/AE3) **in selected cases**. |
| T1.13 | Staining methods | **Lymphatic** invasion: H&E with **D2-40** as a standardized adjunct. **Vascular** invasion: H&E with elastic stains (e.g., elastic Van Gieson [EVG] / elastic trichrome) or CD31/CD34 as adjuncts. |
| T1.14 | Staining methods | **Perineural** invasion on H&E; **S100** for equivocal cases. |
| T1.15 | Staining methods | TB evaluated on H&E per ITBCC; cytokeratin IHC reserved for difficult cases (**not routine**). |
| T1.16 | Measurement protocols | Report SMI depth as a **quantitative measurement in µm**. |
| T1.17 | Measurement protocols | **Systematic parallel sectioning** recommended to preserve orientation and allow reliable assessment of lesion extent and margin status. |
| T1.18 | Measurement protocols | MM deformed or absent → alternative approaches (e.g., reconstruction from adjacent intact areas, or measurement from the tumor surface) **may** be used. |
| T1.19 | Risk assessment for LNM and TB | **Poor differentiation and poorly differentiated clusters** systematically evaluated as LNM risk factors. |
| T1.20 | Risk assessment for LNM and TB | TB assessed on H&E per ITBCC guidelines **and reported** in pathology reports. |
| T1.21 | Risk assessment for LNM and TB | Risk assessment should **integrate multiple histologic parameters**. |
| T1.22 | Clinical evaluation | **Lateral margins** interpreted in context of tumor biology; acceptable margin width **varies by lesion type** and may be affected by electrocoagulation artefact, tissue shrinkage and patient factors. |
| T1.23 | Clinical evaluation | **Deep margin** adequacy assessed in relation to tumor biology and other histologic risk features; clinical decisions **should not rely on margin distance alone**. |
| T1.24 | Clinical evaluation | Close or positive margins → **risk-stratified management and multidisciplinary discussion**. **Positive deep margins in T1b or otherwise high-risk lesions generally support surgical referral**; **positive lateral margins in dysplasia or non-invasive lesions may be managed endoscopically or with surveillance.** |

### Supporting guidance in the Discussion (ungraded narrative, not consensus statements)

**SMI depth and breadth** (Criteria for SMI, p5; Measurement protocols, p6)
- Full submucosal thickness often not assessable in ESD specimens → SM1–SM3 subdivision difficult; linear µm measurement often the most practical (p5).
- **Measure from the base or deepest fibre of the MM to the deepest point of invasion** (p5).
- MM deformed, ruptured or absent → virtual baseline reconstruction from adjacent intact areas, or measurement from the tumor surface, **recognizing the risk of overestimating SMI depth** (p5).
- MM may be absent, distorted, fragmented or **duplicated — particularly frequent in Barrett's esophagus** → reconstruct a virtual baseline from adjacent intact MM (p6).
- Recognize artefacts such as cautery effect or ulceration (p5).
- **Follow organ-specific cutoffs (e.g., 500 µm in stomach); retain Haggitt's system for pedunculated colorectal lesions** (p5).
- Depth in µm **rounded to the nearest 100 µm** — "sufficient precision without implying false accuracy"; breadth in mm to 0.1 mm; both can be added as synoptic-template fields (p6).
- Breadth = **greatest lateral extent of invasive submucosal carcinoma** in the specimen, not restricted to a T1b area (p5). Area measurement unnecessary in routine practice (p5).
- Multifocal or discontinuous invasion: deepest focus for the primary depth measurement; other foci **documented descriptively** — no standardized single aggregate breadth or three-dimensional estimate (p5, p6).
- Depth should not be interpreted in isolation; it contributes within composite models with LVI, TB and differentiation (p5).
- **Desmin** staining to reduce interobserver variability in colorectal SMI depth **was not supported by the panel**; desmin or caldesmon should not be applied routinely for all ESD specimens (p6).

**Specimen handling and margins** (Margin evaluation techniques, p5–6)
- **Pin the specimen immediately after resection** (prevents edge curling); send with a detailed orientation diagram (p5).
- Circumferential ESDs removed as a tube: only proximal and distal are true margins → mark cut lines so they are not read as margins (p5).
- Ink deep and lateral margins in **separate colours** where possible, or identify them clearly in the gross description (p5).
- **Embed completely**, section systematically (p5) — at regular intervals, **commonly around 2–3 mm where feasible**; no single interval suits every organ, specimen or laboratory (p6).
- Document margin distances in µm or mm; **close negative margins (<1 mm) interpreted with caution** in the context of other histologic risk factors (p6).
- Margin positive **only** when tumor cells contact the inked surface; **>2 mm clearance, recommended by some authors, is not universally adopted** (p6).
- Cautery artefact, retraction or piecemeal fragmentation → deeper levels ± adjunct IHC (p6).
- Normal tissue at the margin supports adequacy but should not be the sole criterion, particularly when deeper invasion is suspected (p6).
- Lateral margins: **wider clearance essential in invasive carcinoma**; narrower margins may be acceptable for dysplasia or in situ lesions (p6).
- Deep margin: a **narrow but negative deep margin may be acceptable in the absence of adverse features**; **any positive deep margin in an invasive lesion warrants escalation of therapy** (p6).
- Positive margins in ESD specimens are linked to higher recurrence and are considered **non-curative across all organ sites** (p5).
- Synoptic checklist items: pinning, inking colours, sectioning plan, margin distances (p6); also document **tumor size** with depth, breadth and margin distance (p6).

**Staining** (Staining methods, p6)
- Ancillary stains when H&E is equivocal or confounded by artefact (thermal injury, retraction space, crush, fragmented MM) (p6).
- Report the **reason** for ancillary testing and the final category — **present, absent or indeterminate**; stains unavailable or non-diagnostic → state as a limitation (p6).
- LVI: single IHC (D2-40, CD31, CD34) and dual IHC improve detection; elastic stains improve detection of venous invasion; perineural invasion usually seen on H&E, S100 or SOX-10 may help (p6).
- Distorted architecture (prior biopsy, thermal injury, tumor damage): smooth muscle markers (desmin, caldesmon, SMA) delineate MM; epithelial markers (cytokeratin AE1/AE3, pancytokeratin) clarify tumor extent; dual IHC where feasible, single-marker approach an effective alternative (p6).
- Antibody validation, internal/external quality assurance and on-slide controls recommended (p6).

**Tumor budding, differentiation, subtype** (Risk assessment of LNM and TB evaluation, p6–7)
- TB and poorly differentiated clusters = independent LNM risk factors (p6).
- Report **bud count in a standardized 20× field (0.785 mm²)** and the grade (p7):

| ITBCC grade | Buds per 20× field (0.785 mm²) |
|---|---|
| BD1 | 0–4 |
| BD2 | 5–9 |
| BD3 | ≥10 |

*Source: Risk assessment of LNM and TB evaluation section, p7.*
- Assess on H&E; IHC reserved for confirmation in difficult or equivocal cases, only to find a hotspot (p7).
- ITBCC criteria validated primarily for colorectal adenocarcinoma; TB is also prognostic in esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (SCC) and intestinal-type gastric adenocarcinoma → report TB for carcinomas resected by ESD throughout the gastrointestinal tract (p7).
- Integrate LVI, perineural invasion, margin status and differentiation into **composite models**, not reliance on a single factor (p7).
- Grade tubular adenocarcinoma by **organ-specific conventional criteria**; report **mucinous and signet-ring cell carcinoma by subtype**, not forced into tubular grading (p7).
- **No preferred grading system** (American Joint Committee on Cancer [AJCC], World Health Organization [WHO]) was defined (p7).

**Clinical evaluation** (Clinical evaluation, p7)
- Close or positive margins → individualize (p7).
- Otherwise curative resection with a **positive lateral margin limited to non-invasive neoplasia → endoscopic retreatment or close surveillance** (p7).
- **Positive deep margin in T1b or any high-risk context → generally warrants staging and multidisciplinary discussion of additional therapy** (p7).
- ESD specimens are smaller, with narrower margins and common thermal artefact and contraction → integrate lesion biology, full histology and patient factors rather than a single distance metric (p7).

### Organ-specific content (Discussion, p5)
Stated in the Discussion as background from cited literature and national guidelines; not consensus statements.

| Organ / histology | What the article states |
|---|---|
| Esophageal SCC | m1–m2 lesions carry minimal LNM risk; risk rises substantially from m3–Sm1. Japan Esophageal Society guidelines classify **pT1a-MM and pT1b-SM as higher-risk**, warranting consideration of additional therapy, particularly with high-risk features such as LVI (p5). |
| Esophagus (example threshold) | "MM vs SM in oesophagus <200 µm" given as an organ-specific threshold example (p5). |
| Barrett's-associated | MM duplication frequent → virtual-baseline reconstruction (p6). No Barrett's-specific depth cut-off given. |
| Stomach | Mucosa-confined, en bloc, negative margins, no LVI, favorable histology → curative by ESD without additional therapy. **SMI beyond 500 µm**, or LVI, positive margins, or high-grade / predominantly undifferentiated histology → markedly increased LNM risk, **warrants additional surgical resection**. **500 µm = critical cut-off between low- and high-risk SMI** (p5). |
| Colorectum | Deep SMI, LVI, TB and poor differentiation are LNM risk factors; SMI depth insufficient alone — composite assessment more accurate (p5). A meta-analysis suggests deep SMI's prognostic weight may be attenuated once LVI, poor differentiation and high-grade TB are considered (p6). Haggitt retained for pedunculated lesions (p5). No colorectal µm cut-off is stated. |
| Duodenum | Not addressed separately. |

## Contradictions / Open Questions
- **Invited panel size:** Results text says 57 experts invited (p2); Figure 1 says 58 identified and invited, 57 screened, 1 excluded (p4).
- **Number of rounds:** Methods describe 5 rounds with round 5 the final prioritization (p2); Figure 1 shows the endoscopist subgroup's final prioritization at **round 4** (p4).
- **Scale labels:** Results call the per-domain mean ± SD values "mean Likert scores" (5-point scale), but the values (e.g., 9.0 ± 0.4) fit the 10-point prioritization scale (p3).
- **Deep-margin scope:** Table 1 restricts surgical referral to positive deep margins in **T1b or otherwise high-risk** lesions (T1.24); the Discussion says **any positive deep margin in an invasive lesion** warrants escalation of therapy (p6), and elsewhere that positive margins are non-curative across all organ sites (p5).
- **Desmin placement:** Results text attributes "advising against routine desmin/caldesmon use" to the measurement-protocols domain (p3); Table 1 lists desmin/caldesmon only as selective stains when the MM is fragmented/unclear (T1.11), with no "not routine" bullet.
- **Perineural stain:** Table 1 names S100 only (T1.14); the Discussion adds SOX-10 (p6).
- **SM1–SM3 terminology vs wiki:** [[esophageal-cancer]] and [[endoscopic-submucosal-dissection]] (from [[aga-2019-esd-t1b-esophageal-cancer]]) define SM1 by µm depth (up to 200 µm SCC, up to 500 µm adenocarcinoma); this consensus reserves SM1–SM3 for specimens containing MP and otherwise reports µm. Thresholds do not conflict; the label usage does.
- **Positive deep margin:** [[endoscopic-submucosal-dissection]] ([[aga-2021-post-esd-surveillance]]) — positive deep (vertical) margin after ESD of a malignant neoplasm → in most cases, prompt surgical referral. This consensus: T1b/high-risk positive deep margins generally support surgical referral, via multidisciplinary discussion (T1.24).
- **Positive lateral margin:** [[colorectal-esd]] records the National Comprehensive Cancer Network (NCCN) recommendation of adjuvant surgery for any positive margin, irrespective of location (via [[aga-2025-endoscopic-resection-crc]]); this consensus allows endoscopic management or surveillance for positive lateral margins in dysplasia or non-invasive lesions (T1.24).
- **Desmin:** [[polypectomy]] ([[weo-2024-large-colorectal-lst-consensus]], non-US) suggests considering desmin with SMI (Conditional/Low); this consensus says desmin/caldesmon should not be routine, selective only when MM is unclear.
- **TB reporting:** [[polypectomy]] ([[usmstf-2020-malignant-colorectal-polyps|US Multi-Society Task Force (USMSTF) 2020]] Recommendation 5) lists TB as a suggested addition to the report; this consensus says TB should be reported (T1.20). Sectioning interval: USMSTF 2-mm intervals vs "commonly around 2–3 mm where feasible" here (p6).
- Statement-level wording and per-statement agreement for the 56 statements are only in the online supplement; Table 1 is a summary.
