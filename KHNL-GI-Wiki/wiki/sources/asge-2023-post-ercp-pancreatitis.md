# ASGE Guideline: Post-ERCP Pancreatitis Prevention (2023)

## Bibliographic Info

- **Authors:** ASGE Standards of Practice Committee
- **Year:** 2023
- **Journal:** Gastrointestinal Endoscopy
- **Type:** GRADE-based Standards of Practice guideline

---

## Summary

This guideline consolidates evidence-based strategies to prevent **post-ERCP pancreatitis (PEP)** — the most common serious complication of [[ercp]]. It endorses universal rectal NSAIDs, selective prophylactic pancreatic stenting, wire-assisted cannulation, and aggressive periprocedural hydration.

---

## Key Recommendations (conditional)

- **Rectal NSAIDs** (indomethacin or diclofenac) for **all patients** undergoing [[ercp]] — both unselected and high-risk.
- **Prophylactic pancreatic duct stent** in **high-risk** patients (and when pancreatic duct access is readily achievable).
- **Wire-assisted cannulation** over conventional contrast-assisted cannulation to reduce PEP.
- **Aggressive periprocedural hydration** with isotonic crystalloid (lactated Ringer's) in patients without contraindication.

---

## Clinical Relevance

Rectal NSAIDs for everyone, pancreatic stents for high-risk cases, wire-guided cannulation, and LR hydration are the four pillars of PEP prophylaxis. Directly informs safe practice for [[ercp]]; the hydration principle parallels early fluid management in [[acute-pancreatitis]].
