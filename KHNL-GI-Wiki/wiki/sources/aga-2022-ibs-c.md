## Summary

This guideline ranks drug therapy for constipation-predominant [[irritable-bowel-syndrome]] (IBS-C), a [[disorders-of-gut-brain-interaction|disorder of gut–brain interaction]]. Only **linaclotide** earned a strong recommendation; the remainder are conditional.

---

## Key Recommendations

- **[[linaclotide]]** — *recommended* (strong).
- **Tenapanor**, **[[plecanatide]]**, **tegaserod**, and **[[lubiprostone]]** — *suggested* (conditional).
- **PEG laxatives** — suggested (low-cost option, though limited effect on global IBS symptoms/pain).
- **Tricyclic antidepressants (TCAs)** — suggested for IBS (central neuromodulation).
- **Antispasmodics** — suggested; **against routine SSRIs** for IBS.

---

## Clinical Relevance

Linaclotide is the only strongly recommended agent for IBS-C; secretagogues (tenapanor, plecanatide, lubiprostone) and the prokinetic tegaserod are conditional alternatives, with TCAs for pain-predominant disease. Distinguish IBS-C from [[chronic-idiopathic-constipation]] (overlapping drugs, different targets).
