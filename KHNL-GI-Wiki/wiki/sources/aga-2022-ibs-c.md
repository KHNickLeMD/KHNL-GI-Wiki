## Summary

This American Gastroenterological Association (AGA) guideline ranks drug therapy for [[irritable-bowel-syndrome|irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)]] with constipation (IBS-C), a [[disorders-of-gut-brain-interaction|disorder of gut–brain interaction]]. Only **linaclotide** earned a strong recommendation; the remainder are conditional.

---

## Recommendations

Table 1 — Executive Summary of Recommendations (verbatim). *For all recommendation statements, the comparator was no drug treatment.* The panel agreed on **9 recommendations**: one strong (linaclotide) and eight conditional.

| # | Recommendation | Strength | Certainty of evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In patients with IBS-C, the AGA suggests using tenapanor | Conditional | Moderate |
| 2 | In patients with IBS-C, the AGA suggests using [[plecanatide]] | Conditional | Moderate |
| 3 | In patients with IBS-C, the AGA **recommends** using [[linaclotide]] | **Strong** | High |
| 4 | In patients with IBS-C, the AGA suggests using tegaserod<br/>*Implementation remark:* Tegaserod was reapproved for women under the age of 65 years without a history of cardiovascular ischemic events (such as myocardial infarction, stroke, TIA [transient ischemic attack], or angina) | Conditional | Moderate |
| 5 | In patients with IBS-C, the AGA suggests using [[lubiprostone]] | Conditional | Moderate |
| 6 | In patients with IBS-C, the AGA suggests using PEG [polyethylene glycol] laxatives | Conditional | Low |
| 7 | In patients with IBS, the AGA suggests using TCAs [tricyclic antidepressants] | Conditional | Low |
| 8 | In patients with IBS, the AGA suggests **against** using SSRIs [selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors] | Conditional | Low |
| 9 | In patients with IBS, the AGA suggests using antispasmodics | Conditional | Low |

## Clinical Relevance

- Linaclotide = the only strong recommendation; every other agent is conditional — tenapanor, plecanatide, lubiprostone, tegaserod and PEG (IBS-C), TCAs and antispasmodics (IBS), and a conditional recommendation **against** SSRIs (IBS).
- TCAs (Rec 7) apply to IBS generally: global relief met the clinically meaningful threshold; abdominal pain response did not.
- FDA-approved IBS-C doses as stated in the text: tenapanor 50 mg twice daily; plecanatide 3 mg once daily; linaclotide 290 µg once daily (72 or 145 µg once daily for [[chronic-idiopathic-constipation|chronic idiopathic constipation (CIC)]]); tegaserod 6 mg twice daily.
