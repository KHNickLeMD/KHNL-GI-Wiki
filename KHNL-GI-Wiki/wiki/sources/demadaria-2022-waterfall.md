- **Authors:** de-Madaria E, Buxbaum JL, Maisonneuve P, et al., for the ERICA Consortium
- **Year:** 2022
- **Journal/Publisher:** New England Journal of Medicine
- **DOI:** [10.1056/NEJMoa2202884](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2202884)
- **Type:** RCT (multicenter, open-label, parallel-group, superiority; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04381169)

## Summary
Open-label RCT at 18 centers across four countries (India, Italy, Mexico, Spain) comparing **aggressive vs moderate goal-directed fluid resuscitation with lactated Ringer's** in patients presenting with acute pancreatitis of any severity. Aggressive arm: 20 mL/kg bolus then 3 mL/kg/hr. Moderate arm: 10 mL/kg bolus if hypovolemic (no bolus if normovolemic) then 1.5 mL/kg/hr; both adjusted at 12/24/48/72 hr per clinical status. Primary outcome: progression to moderately severe or severe pancreatitis during hospitalization. Main safety outcome: fluid overload.

The trial was **halted early** at the first planned interim analysis (249 of a planned 744 patients) for between-group safety differences without an efficacy signal. This provides the primary-evidence basis for the shift away from historically recommended early aggressive hydration toward moderate, goal-directed resuscitation now reflected in ACG 2024.

## Key Findings / Claims
- No difference in progression to moderately severe/severe pancreatitis: **22.1% (aggressive) vs 17.3% (moderate)**; adjusted RR 1.30 (95% CI 0.78–2.18), P = 0.32.
- **Fluid overload roughly tripled with aggressive resuscitation: 20.5% vs 6.3%**; adjusted RR 2.85 (95% CI 1.36–5.94), P = 0.004.
- Median hospitalization 6 days (aggressive) vs 5 days (moderate).
- Bottom line: early aggressive fluid resuscitation caused more harm (fluid overload) without improving clinical outcomes.

## Contradictions / Open Questions
- Corroborates (does not conflict with) the existing `[[acg-2024-acute-pancreatitis]]` moderately-aggressive/goal-directed recommendation. Older observational data and small trials favoring vigorous hydration are superseded by this RCT.
- Excluded severe organ failure at baseline in some strata; optimal resuscitation in patients presenting with established severe disease remains less defined.
