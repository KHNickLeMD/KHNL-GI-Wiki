# SAGES Guideline Update: POEM for Achalasia (2024)

## Bibliographic Info

- **Authors:** SAGES Guidelines Committee
- **Year:** 2024 (update to the 2021 SAGES POEM guideline)
- **Type:** GRADE-based guideline update

---

## Summary

This update revisits the role of [[poem]] in [[achalasia]] in light of longer-term data, issuing 3 recommendations for adults. It positions POEM favorably versus pneumatic dilation and as comparable to laparoscopic Heller myotomy (LHM).

---

## Key Recommendations (conditional)

- **POEM over pneumatic dilation (PD)** for adults with achalasia.
- **POEM or laparoscopic Heller myotomy (LHM)** — either approach is acceptable; selection individualized (POEM is especially advantageous for **type III achalasia / spastic disorders** given a tailorable, longer myotomy).
- Defer to the **2021 SAGES guideline** for specific populations not re-addressed in this update.
- Counsel patients that **post-POEM GERD/reflux** is common (LHM is typically combined with a partial fundoplication); plan reflux monitoring/PPI.

---

## Clinical Relevance

Confirms POEM as a first-line achalasia therapy — superior to pneumatic dilation and on par with surgical myotomy — with type III disease a particular strength, balanced against higher post-procedure reflux. Directly supports the [[poem]] procedure note and [[achalasia]] management; reflux sequelae connect to [[gerd]].
