## Summary
This SAGES guideline used the GRADE methodology to compare [[poem|peroral endoscopic myotomy (POEM)]] against the two established treatments for [[achalasia]] — pneumatic dilation (PD) and laparoscopic Heller myotomy (LHM) with fundoplication — across three key questions. The panel issued four recommendations for adults and children.

The headline conclusion is a **strong recommendation favoring POEM over pneumatic dilation**, qualified by the caveat that the higher rate of post-procedure GERD (and consequent long-term PPI use) after POEM may sway the decision for patients to whom that matters. POEM versus LHM was judged a balanced trade-off: for subtypes I and II the panel concluded neither clearly dominates and **either POEM or LHM with fundoplication is acceptable** via shared decision-making, whereas for **subtype III achalasia the panel favored POEM** (the longer, tailored myotomy achievable endoscopically is advantageous for the spastic segment). The panel noted the evidence base derived predominantly from subtype I/II patients with relatively short (≤2-year) follow-up and called for further study of long-term efficacy and of strategies to mitigate the principal downside common to POEM and Heller — post-myotomy reflux. The defining clinical teaching point preserved here: **POEM lacks an inherent anti-reflux component, so post-POEM GERD is its main trade-off versus Heller-plus-fundoplication.**

## Key Findings / Claims — Recommendations
The four panel recommendations (GRADE; "recommends" = strong, "suggests" = conditional), near-verbatim:

1. **POEM vs pneumatic dilation:** The panel issues a **strong recommendation for the use of POEM over PD** for achalasia — *unless* the concern of continued postoperative PPI use remains a key decision-making concern to the patient. (Strong recommendation)
2. **POEM vs Heller, subtypes I/II:** The panel **suggests** that adult patients with achalasia subtypes I and II may be treated with **either POEM or laparoscopic Heller myotomy with fundoplication**, based on surgeon and patient shared decision-making. (Conditional recommendation)
3. **POEM vs Heller, subtype III:** The panel issued a **conditional recommendation favoring POEM over Heller myotomy for achalasia subtype III.** (Conditional recommendation)
4. **Research:** The panel recommends further investigation of strategies to address undesirable effects (notably GERD) for both POEM and Heller myotomy and their relative efficacy. (Research recommendation)

Scope notes: recommendations apply best to subtypes I and II (16 of 18 studies in the systematic review); evidence for type III POEM vs LHM was limited; follow-up data were largely short-term (≤2 years), used as a proxy for long-term outcomes.
