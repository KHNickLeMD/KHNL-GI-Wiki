## Summary
Phase 3 double-blind placebo-controlled RCT of **SER-109**, an orally administered microbiome therapeutic composed of **purified Firmicutes spores**, in patients with **recurrent C. difficile infection** (≥3 episodes inclusive of the qualifying episode). After standard-of-care antibiotic treatment (vancomycin or fidaxomicin) had resolved symptoms, patients received SER-109 or placebo (four capsules daily for 3 days). Rationale: antibiotics kill toxin-producing vegetative bacteria but not spores and do not repair the disrupted microbiome; restoring spore-forming Firmicutes shifts bile-acid metabolism (raising secondary bile acids) to inhibit C. difficile spore germination.

SER-109 was later FDA-approved (2023) as the first orally administered fecal microbiota spore product for prevention of recurrent CDI (marketed as VOWST / fecal microbiota spores, live-brpk).

## Key Findings / Claims
- Recurrence up to 8 weeks: **12% (SER-109) vs 40% (placebo)**; relative risk 0.32 (95% CI 0.18–0.58), P < 0.001.
- Benefit consistent across strata — age <65 (RR 0.24) and ≥65 (RR 0.36); prior vancomycin (RR 0.41) and fidaxomicin (RR 0.09).
- Safety similar to placebo; most adverse events mild-to-moderate and gastrointestinal.
- SER-109 dose species detected as early as week 1, associated with bile-acid profiles that inhibit C. difficile spore germination.
