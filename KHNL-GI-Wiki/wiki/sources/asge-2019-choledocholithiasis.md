# ASGE Guideline: Endoscopic Management of Choledocholithiasis (2019)

## Bibliographic Info

- **Authors:** ASGE Standards of Practice Committee
- **Year:** 2019
- **Journal:** Gastrointestinal Endoscopy
- **Type:** GRADE-based Standards of Practice guideline

---

## Summary

This guideline risk-stratifies suspected common bile duct (CBD) stones and defines the diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopic pathway, including stone-clearance techniques and the role of early [[ercp]] in gallstone pancreatitis.

---

## Key Recommendations

- **Risk stratification:** **high-probability** CBD stones → proceed directly to **[[ercp]]**; **intermediate-probability** → confirm first with **EUS or MRCP** (avoid unnecessary ERCP); low-probability → cholecystectomy with intraoperative cholangiogram as needed.
- **EUS and MRCP** have comparable high accuracy for detecting CBD stones; choose by availability/expertise.
- **Large/difficult stones:** **endoscopic papillary large-balloon dilation after sphincterotomy** improves clearance over sphincterotomy alone; consider mechanical lithotripsy/cholangioscopy-guided lithotripsy.
- **Gallstone pancreatitis:** **early ERCP** for concurrent **cholangitis or persistent biliary obstruction** (not routinely for uncomplicated gallstone pancreatitis).

---

## Clinical Relevance

ERCP for high-probability stones, EUS/MRCP to triage intermediate-probability cases, large-balloon dilation for big stones, and early ERCP only when cholangitis/obstruction complicates [[acute-pancreatitis|gallstone pancreatitis]]. Core to [[ercp]] practice and biliary obstruction workup.
