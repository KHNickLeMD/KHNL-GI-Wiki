## Summary

This guidance addresses contraception, fertility, pregnancy, and postpartum management across liver diseases — both **liver diseases unique to pregnancy** and **pre-existing liver disease/cirrhosis** in pregnancy.

---

## Key Points

- **Contraception:** clinicians should routinely inquire about sexual activity and pregnancy intentions in all reproductive-aged adolescents and women with chronic liver disease or liver transplant; effective contraception (including long-acting reversible contraception [LARC]/intrauterine devices [IUDs]) for those wishing to avoid pregnancy. **Estrogen-containing agents should be avoided in decompensated cirrhosis, Budd-Chiari syndrome, hepatocellular adenomas, and transplant recipients with graft failure**; combined hormonal contraception is acceptable with other chronic liver diseases.
- **Cirrhosis in pregnancy:** preconception counseling; **prepregnancy endoscopic variceal screening is recommended; screen in the early second trimester if not performed within 1 year before conception, or if there is ongoing liver injury or decompensation**; co-manage with maternal-fetal medicine (MFM).
- **Pregnancy-specific liver diseases:** intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy (diagnosis: pruritus in the 2nd or 3rd trimester plus total bile acids >10 μmol/L without other causes; ursodeoxycholic acid first-line), preeclampsia/hemolysis, elevated liver enzymes, and low platelets (HELLP), and acute fatty liver of pregnancy (expeditious delivery after maternal stabilization) — see the [[liver-disease-in-pregnancy]] schema.
- **Medications:** avoid teratogens (e.g., **mycophenolate**, ribavirin); manage autoimmune hepatitis and transplant immunosuppression with pregnancy-compatible regimens.

## Clinical Relevance

Provides contraception guidance, variceal screening in cirrhotic pregnancy, MFM co-management, and safe-medication selection across liver disease in reproductive-age patients. Directly supports the [[liver-disease-in-pregnancy]] schema and overlaps with [[autoimmune-hepatitis]] and [[liver-transplantation]] management.
