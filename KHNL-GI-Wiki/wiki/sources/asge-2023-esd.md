# ASGE Guideline: Endoscopic Submucosal Dissection (ESD) for Early Esophageal & Gastric Cancer (2023)

## Bibliographic Info

- **Authors:** ASGE Standards of Practice Committee
- **Year:** 2023
- **Journal:** Gastrointestinal Endoscopy
- **Type:** GRADE-based Standards of Practice guideline

---

## Summary

This guideline positions **endoscopic submucosal dissection (ESD)** versus EMR for early esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC), early esophageal adenocarcinoma (EAC), and early [[gastric-adenocarcinoma]] (GAC) and their precursors — favoring ESD (en-bloc resection) for larger lesions and discouraging surgery when endoscopic cure is feasible.

---

## Key Recommendations (conditional)

- **ESCC:** **ESD over EMR** for early, well-differentiated, nonulcerated cancer **>15 mm**; for ≤15 mm lesions, **either ESD or EMR**; **against surgery** when endoscopic resection is feasible.
- **EAC:** **ESD over EMR** for early-stage lesions (especially larger/nodular); smaller flat lesions may use either.
- **GAC:** **ESD over EMR** for early, well-differentiated lesions; for similar lesions **<20 mm**, either ESD or EMR; **against surgery** when curative endoscopic resection is achievable.
- Lesions exceeding size/depth criteria → **surgical evaluation** over endoscopic approaches.

---

## Clinical Relevance

ESD enables en-bloc, curative resection of early luminal cancers and is preferred over EMR for larger lesions, sparing patients esophagectomy/gastrectomy. Core to [[endoscopic-oncology]] and management of early [[gastric-adenocarcinoma]]/[[barretts-esophagus]]-associated neoplasia.
