## Summary

The **CT/MRI Liver Imaging Reporting and Data System (LI-RADS) v2018 Core** is the primary document of the system. It applies to an **untreated observation without pathologic proof in a patient at high risk for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)**; that scope line is the first criterion — LI-RADS is not applied to the general population, nor to an observation already proven at pathology.

Categorization runs in **four steps**: (1) take the categories that are assigned *before* the diagnostic table — **LR-NC, LR-TIV, LR-1, LR-2, LR-M** — and only *otherwise* go to the table, which assigns **LR-3, LR-4, LR-5** from **arterial phase hyperenhancement (APHE)**, **observation size** and a count of the other major features; (2) optionally adjust by **ancillary features**; (3) apply **tiebreaking rules**; (4) sanity-check the category.

The two rules that catch readers out are both in steps 2 and 3. **Ancillary features can never upgrade to LR-5** — the ceiling on an ancillary upgrade is LR-4. And **every tiebreak goes to the lower-certainty category**: unsure between LR-1 and LR-2 → LR-2; unsure between LR-4 and LR-5 → LR-4; unsure about hepatocellular origin → **LR-M**; unsure about tumor in vein → **not** LR-TIV.

v2018's headline change is that **threshold growth was simplified to ≥50% size increase of a mass in ≤6 months**, which made **AASLD, OPTN and LI-RADS agree on one definition**.

## Key Findings / Claims

### Step 1 — categories assigned before the table
| If… | Category |
|---|---|
| Cannot be categorized because of **image degradation or omission** | **LR-NC** |
| **Definite tumor in vein (TIV)** | **LR-TIV** |
| **Definitely benign** | **LR-1** |
| **Probably benign** | **LR-2** |
| **Probably or definitely malignant but not HCC-specific** (e.g. targetoid) | **LR-M** |
| Otherwise | Use the **CT/MRI diagnostic table** |

Table categories: **LR-3** intermediate probability of malignancy · **LR-4** probably HCC · **LR-5** definitely HCC.

### The CT/MRI diagnostic table (v2018)
Additional major features counted: **enhancing "capsule"**, **nonperipheral "washout"**, **threshold growth**.

| | **No APHE**, <20 mm | **No APHE**, ≥20 mm | **Nonrim APHE**, <10 mm | **Nonrim APHE**, 10–19 mm | **Nonrim APHE**, ≥20 mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **None** | LR-3 | LR-3 | LR-3 | LR-3 | **LR-4** |
| **One** | LR-3 | **LR-4** | **LR-4** | **LR-4** (see note) | **LR-5** |
| **≥ Two** | **LR-4** | **LR-4** | **LR-4** | **LR-5** | **LR-5** |

- **The nonrim-APHE, 10–19 mm, one-additional-feature cell is split**: **LR-4 if the one feature is an enhancing "capsule"; LR-5 if it is nonperipheral "washout" OR threshold growth.**
- **If unsure about the presence of any major feature, characterize that feature as absent.**

### Threshold growth (v2018 definition)
- **≥50% size increase of a mass in ≤6 months.**
- **Simplified in v2018**, and now **identical across AASLD, OPTN and LI-RADS**.
- Growth that no longer qualifies is **subthreshold growth** — which is not a major feature but **is** an ancillary feature favoring malignancy.

### LR-M criteria
**Targetoid mass**, **OR** a **nontargetoid mass with one or more of:**
- **Infiltrative appearance**
- **Marked diffusion restriction**
- **Necrosis or severe ischemia**
- Another feature that in the radiologist's judgment suggests non-HCC malignancy (**specify it in the report**)

…**with no tumor in vein** and **not meeting LR-5 criteria**.

- A **targetoid appearance suggests non-HCC malignancy but does not exclude HCC.**

### LR-1 and LR-2 entities
- **LR-1 (definitely benign):** cyst · hemangioma · perfusion alteration (e.g. arterioportal shunt) · hepatic fat deposition or sparing · hypertrophic pseudomass · confluent fibrosis or focal scar — **when definite** — and **spontaneous disappearance**. List not exhaustive.
- **LR-2 (probably benign):** the same six entities **when probable**, plus a **distinctive nodule without malignant imaging features**. List not exhaustive.
- **Distinctive nodule, defined:** a **solid nodule <20 mm**, distinctive in imaging appearance compared with background nodules, **with no major feature of HCC, no LR-M feature and no ancillary feature of malignancy**. Common appearances: T1 hyperintense, T2 hypointense, siderotic, hepatobiliary-phase hyperintense, or any combination. **If ≥20 mm, categorize LR-3 or higher** depending on features.
- ⚠ **Nodules suggestive of focal nodular hyperplasia or hepatocellular adenoma usually should be LR-3**; with caution they may be LR-2; **they should not be LR-1.**

### Step 2 — ancillary features (optional, radiologist's discretion)
Usable for improved detection, increased confidence, or category adjustment.

- **≥1 ancillary feature favoring malignancy → upgrade by one category, up to LR-4.** Absence of these features must **not** be used to downgrade.
- **≥1 ancillary feature favoring benignity → downgrade by one category.** Absence must **not** be used to upgrade.
- **Both present → do not adjust.**
- **Ancillary features cannot be used to upgrade to LR-5.**
- **If unsure about the presence of any ancillary feature, characterize it as absent.**

| Favoring malignancy (not HCC-specific) | Favoring HCC in particular | Favoring benignity |
|---|---|---|
| Ultrasound visibility as a discrete nodule · **subthreshold growth** · restricted diffusion · mild–moderate T2 hyperintensity · corona enhancement · fat sparing in a solid mass · iron sparing in a solid mass · transitional-phase hypointensity · hepatobiliary-phase hypointensity | Nonenhancing "capsule" · nodule-in-nodule · mosaic architecture · blood products in mass · fat in mass, more than adjacent liver | **Size stability >2 years** · size reduction · parallels blood pool · undistorted vessels · iron in mass more than liver · marked T2 hyperintensity · hepatobiliary-phase isointensity |

### Step 3 — tiebreaking rules
- **Unsure about the presence of TIV → do not categorize as LR-TIV.**
- **Unsure between two categories → choose the one reflecting lower certainty**: lower certainty of benignity (LR-1 → LR-2), lower certainty of malignancy (LR-5 → LR-4), lower certainty of hepatocellular origin (**→ LR-M**).

### Step 4 — final check
After steps 1–3, ask whether the assigned category seems reasonable and appropriate before moving on.

### Management by category
| Category | Management |
|---|---|
| **LR-1** | Return to surveillance in **6 months** |
| **LR-2** | Return to surveillance in **6 months** |
| **LR-3** | Consider repeat diagnostic imaging in **≤6 months** |
| **LR-4** | Repeat or alternative diagnostic imaging in **3–6 months** |
| **LR-5** | **HCC confirmed** |
| **LR-M** | **Multidisciplinary discussion for tailored workup** |
| **LR-TIV** | **Multidisciplinary discussion for tailored workup** |
| **LR-NC** | Repeat or alternative diagnostic imaging in **≤3 months** |

- **LR-M and LR-TIV should be reported** — they may affect pretransplant workup.
- **Most LR-TIV observations are HCC**, but some are intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma or another malignancy; **report the likely etiology**.
- **OPTN users in the USA:** the Core carries a separate conversion of LI-RADS categories to OPTN classes.

## Contradictions / Open Questions
- **No contradiction with [[aasld-2023-hcc]]** — v2018 deliberately harmonized threshold growth with AASLD and OPTN, and the LR-3/4/5 table on the wiki came from AASLD 2023 Figure 6, which reproduces this one.
- **The ultrasound visualization score (A/B/C) is not in this document** — it belongs to the **US LI-RADS Surveillance Core**, a separate ACR publication, which remains uningested.
- **Several LR-M sub-features defer to a manual that was pending at publication** (marked diffusion restriction, necrosis or severe ischemia, "other feature in the radiologist's judgment"). The Core names them but does not define them, and the document says so.
- **The system is a radiology instrument.** It assigns a category and a management interval; it does not stage, and the treatment decisions on the HCC pages come from the hepatology and oncology sources.
