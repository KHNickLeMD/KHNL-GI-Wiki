## Summary

This guideline provides diagnosis, management, and **surveillance** recommendations for the hamartomatous polyposis syndromes — **Peutz-Jeghers syndrome (PJS)**, **juvenile polyposis syndrome (JPS)**, and **PTEN hamartoma tumor syndrome (Cowden)** — addressing GI polyp burden and extraintestinal cancer risk.

---

## Key Recommendations

- **[[peutz-jeghers-syndrome|Peutz-Jeghers (PJS)]]:** baseline **upper endoscopy + colonoscopy at age 8–10**, repeat every ~3 years (resume at 18 if negative at baseline); lifelong **small-bowel surveillance** (capsule/MRE) for intussusception risk; extraintestinal screening (breast, pancreas, gynecologic, testicular).
- **[[juvenile-polyposis-syndrome|Juvenile polyposis (JPS)]]:** begin **colonoscopy + upper endoscopy at age 12–15** (or earlier if symptomatic), with intervals by polyp burden; evaluate for *SMAD4* (HHT overlap) and *BMPR1A*.
- **PTEN/[[cowden-syndrome|Cowden]]:** **colonoscopy starting at age 35** (earlier if symptomatic); coordinate intensive extraintestinal cancer surveillance (breast, thyroid, endometrial, renal).
- Manage symptomatic/large polyps endoscopically; refer for genetic testing and family cascade screening.

---

## Clinical Relevance

Sets concrete surveillance start ages and intervals across PJS, JPS, and Cowden, integrating small-bowel and extraintestinal cancer screening. Complements the vault's hereditary syndrome notes and [[colorectal-cancer]] prevention.
