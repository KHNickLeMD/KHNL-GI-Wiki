## Summary

The **Simple Endoscopic Score for Crohn's Disease (SES-CD)** treats every endoscopic variable and every bowel segment as equally important. That is biologically wrong: lesions in some segments are much less likely to heal on therapy than the same lesions elsewhere. The **Modified Multiplier of the SES-CD (MM-SES-CD)** keeps SES-CD's parameters and anatomical locations but **weights each category by its measured impact on the probability of achieving endoscopic remission (ER)**.

Earlier work had fixed only the two extremes — **≥45 = low probability of ER at 1 year (8.2%)** and **<14 = high probability (55.0%)**. **This paper's purpose is to cut the middle**, producing a usable four-band severity scale.

**Data.** Post-hoc pooled analysis of three trial programmes — **UNITI 1/2 and IM-UNITI** (ustekinumab), **EXTEND** (adalimumab), and **CT-P13** (infliximab biosimilar) — giving **350 participants** with centrally read endoscopy, moderate-to-severe Crohn's disease (CDAI 220–450) and a **baseline SES-CD ≥3**. Mean baseline MM-SES-CD 37.9 (SD 18.5); mean SES-CD 14.1 (SD 8.1).

**Result.** Maximum Youden index for 1-year ER fell at **30.5**, and for clinical remission at **32**, so **31** was adopted as the mild/moderate boundary. The resulting bands separate 1-year ER cleanly and monotonically: **50.0% → 30.3% → 21.7% → 8.8%** (p < 0.001). The same gradient holds for **post-induction** scores at week 8 and for **isolated ileal disease**, which needed its own cut-offs (**14** and **33**).

**The clinical-remission signal is much weaker than the endoscopic one** — a distinction the paper makes and the wiki carries. Rates of 1-year CDAI remission were only **numerically** higher in lower bands and **not statistically significant**; only **time to** clinical remission differed (log-rank p = 0.0492).

## Key Findings / Claims

### Table 1 — the MM-SES-CD multipliers

Each baseline SES-CD parameter is scored **0–3 as usual**, then multiplied by a weight that depends on **which segment it is in**. Total = sum of all weighted items.

| Segment | Ulcerations (presence) | Extent of ulcerations | Affected surface | Strictures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ileum** | **× 1** | **× 3** | **× 5** | **× 4** |
| **Right colon** | **× 3** | **× 2** | **× 1** | **× 1** |
| **Transverse colon** | **× 1** | **× 1** | **× 1** | **× 1** |
| **Left colon** | **× 3** | **× 2** | **× 1** | **× 2** |
| **Rectum** | **× 3** | **× 1** | **× 0.5** | **× 2** |

Plus a **segment count term**: **number of segments with ulcerations × 4** — 0 → 0, 1 → 4, 2 → 8, 3 → 12, 4 → 16, **5 → 20**.

- **Category values** within each parameter: ulceration presence **none 0 · small 1 · medium 2 · large 3**; extent of ulcerations **0% 0 · <10% 1 · 10–30% 2 · >30% 3**; affected surface **0% 0 · <50% 1 · 50–75% 2 · >75% 3**; strictures **none 0 · single passable 1 · multiple passable 2 · non-passable 3**.
- **The ileum is where the weighting bites:** affected surface **×5** and strictures **×4** in the ileum, against **×1** in the transverse colon. The **rectal affected-surface multiplier is ×0.5** — the only fractional weight, and the only item worth less than its raw SES-CD score.
- An **online calculator** is provided by the authors (`mcmasteribd.com/mm-ses-cd`); the paper states computing it "requires identical effort" to the SES-CD.

### Severity categories (baseline score)

| MM-SES-CD | Category | 1-year endoscopic remission | 1-year ER-1 (no ulceration) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **<14** | **Very mild / remission** | **50.0%** (11/22) | 59.1% (13/22) |
| **14–30** | **Mild** | **30.3%** (37/122) | 35.2% (43/122) |
| **31–44** | **Moderate** | **21.7%** (20/92) | 25.0% (23/92) |
| **≥45** | **Severe** | **8.8%** (10/114) | 14.0% (16/114) |

*p < 0.001 for both columns. **ER defined as SES-CD <3** (per International Organization for the Study of IBD recommendations); **ER-1** = absence of mucosal ulceration.*

- **The 31 boundary is this paper's contribution** — maximum Youden index **0.139 at a cut-off of 30.5** for 1-year ER and **0.137 at 32** for clinical remission; **31** chosen between them.
- The **≥45** and **<14** anchors were established previously (ER at 1 year **8.2%** and **55.0%** respectively).

### Isolated ileal disease needs different cut-offs

- Separate thresholds: **mild <14**, **moderate 14–32**, **severe ≥33** (maximum Youden index 0.140 and 0.111).
- 1-year ER: **mild 66.7%** (6/9) · **moderate 33.3%** (17/51) · **severe 13.3%** (2/15), **p = 0.027**.
- 75 of the 350 participants had isolated ileal disease.

### Post-induction (week 8) scores carry the same gradient

- 1-year ER by post-induction band: **very mild/inactive 50.0%** (3/6) · **mild 19.2%** (5/26) · **moderate 10.3%** (3/29) · **severe 2.6%** (1/38), **p < 0.001**.
- Based on 99 UNITI and EXTEND participants with week-8 endoscopy.

### ⚠ Clinical remission does not separate the way endoscopic remission does

- 1-year clinical remission (**CDAI <150**) was only **numerically** higher at lower MM-SES-CD and was **not statistically significant**.
- Only **time to** clinical remission over 52 weeks differed (**log-rank p = 0.0492**).
- The same pattern held for SES-CD bands: 1-year CR **50.8% / 41.8% / 39.6%** across mild/moderate/severe, **p = 0.320** — not significant.

### The underlying SES-CD, for reference

- Four variables scored **0–3** in **five segments**: **ulcer size** (none 0 · <0.5 cm 1 · 0.5–2 cm 2 · >2 cm 3); **extent of ulcerated surface** (none 0 · <10% 1 · 10–30% 2 · >30% 3); **affected surface** (unaffected 0 · <50% 1 · 50–70% 2 · >70% 3); **narrowing** (none 0 · single passable 1 · multiple passable 2 · cannot be passed 3).
- Proposed SES-CD spectrum: **≥16 severely active · 7–15 moderately active · 3–6 mildly active · ≤3 inactive**.
- ⚠ The paper's **affected-surface bands differ between its own text and its Table 1** — the text gives **50–70% / >70%**, Table 1 gives **50–75% / >75%**. Table 1 is the scoring tool and is what the wiki reproduces.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- **⚠ The <22.5 threshold on [[ibd-endoscopic-scoring]] is from a different analysis and a different endpoint.** That figure predicts **disease progression**; the bands here predict **endoscopic remission on active therapy at 1 year**. They are not alternative cut-offs for the same question and **must not be merged** — the page now says so.
- **No contradiction with the guideline** that names the tool; this paper supplies what it omits.
- **Derived in moderate-to-severe trial populations on biologic therapy**, all with baseline SES-CD ≥3 and CDAI 220–450. **Whether the bands behave the same in milder, non-trial, or non-biologic-treated disease is untested.**
- **Post-hoc analysis of trials designed for other questions**, pooling three different drugs (ustekinumab, adalimumab, infliximab biosimilar). No prospective validation.
- **Small cells at the extremes:** the very-mild band rests on **22 patients** at baseline and **6** post-induction, and isolated ileal mild disease on **9**. The percentage differences between bands are better supported than the absolute rates.
- **The weights are empirical, not mechanistic.** Why rectal affected surface should count **half** a point per SES-CD unit while ileal affected surface counts **five** is a finding about healing probability, not an explanation.
