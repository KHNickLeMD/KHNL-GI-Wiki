## Summary

This American Gastroenterological Association (AGA) living guideline (14 recommendations; updates the AGA 2020 UC guideline) ranks advanced therapies for moderate-to-severe [[ulcerative-colitis]] and again advances **higher-efficacy-first** selection and **early advanced therapy over step-up**. It situates UC drug selection within the broader [[inflammatory-bowel-disease]] treatment framework.

---

## Key Recommendations

Numbering, wording and grades as in the guideline's Table 3 (executive summary). Population: adult outpatients with moderate-to-severe UC.

### Use and positioning of advanced therapies

| # | Recommendation | Strength · certainty |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Recommends** infliximab, golimumab, vedolizumab, tofacitinib\*, upadacitinib\*, ustekinumab, ozanimod, etrasimod, risankizumab and guselkumab over no treatment | Strong · moderate to high |
| 2 | Suggests adalimumab, filgotinib\* or mirikizumab over no treatment | Conditional · moderate |
| 3 | **Advanced-therapy–naïve:** suggests a HIGHER-efficacy drug (infliximab, vedolizumab, ozanimod, etrasimod, upadacitinib\*, risankizumab, guselkumab) OR an INTERMEDIATE-efficacy drug (golimumab, ustekinumab, tofacitinib\*, filgotinib\*, mirikizumab) rather than a LOWER-efficacy drug (adalimumab) | Conditional · low |
| 4 | **Previously exposed to ≥1 advanced therapy, particularly TNF [tumor necrosis factor] antagonists:** suggests a HIGHER-efficacy drug (tofacitinib, upadacitinib, ustekinumab) OR an INTERMEDIATE-efficacy drug (filgotinib, mirikizumab, risankizumab, guselkumab) rather than a LOWER-efficacy drug (adalimumab, vedolizumab, ozanimod, etrasimod) | Conditional · low |

\*In the United States, the FDA label recommends JAK [Janus kinase] inhibitors for prior failure or intolerance of TNF-antagonist therapy (Table 3 footnote). Filgotinib is not available in the United States (Figure 1 footnote).

Implementation considerations (Recs 1–4, condensed):
- JAK inhibitors (tofacitinib, filgotinib, upadacitinib): restricted use in advanced-therapy–naïve patients; possibly higher major adverse cardiovascular event and cancer risk than TNF antagonists in older adults with cardiovascular risk factors
- Biosimilars of infliximab, adalimumab, ustekinumab ≈ originator for therapy selection; subcutaneous infliximab and vedolizumab comparable to intravenous maintenance
- Particularly severe disease: extended induction (up to 16 wk) or dose escalation may help for certain agents
- Vedolizumab and anti-IL therapies: possibly fewer infectious complications than TNF antagonists — may be preferred at higher risk of immunosuppression-related infection or malignancy
- JAK inhibitors and S1P [sphingosine-1-phosphate] receptor modulators: limited pregnancy data — avoid in women of childbearing age contemplating pregnancy
- After prior advanced therapies: lower-efficacy drugs may need longer treatment for response; observational data support infliximab and golimumab

### Immunomodulators

| # | Recommendation | Strength · certainty |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Suggests **against** thiopurine monotherapy for **induction** of remission | Conditional · very low |
| 6 | In remission: suggests thiopurine monotherapy rather than no treatment for **maintenance** (remission typically induced by corticosteroids) | Conditional · low |
| 7 | Suggests **against** methotrexate monotherapy for induction or maintenance | Conditional · low |

### Combination of biologics and immunomodulators

| # | Recommendation | Strength · certainty |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | Suggests infliximab + immunomodulator over infliximab or immunomodulator alone | Conditional · moderate |
| 9 | Suggests adalimumab or golimumab + immunomodulator over adalimumab, golimumab or immunomodulator monotherapy | Conditional · low |
| 10 | No recommendation for or against non-TNF biologics + immunomodulator over non-TNF biologic alone | No recommendation (knowledge gap) |

### De-escalation

| # | Recommendation | Strength · certainty |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | Corticosteroid-free clinical remission for **at least 6 months** on TNF antagonist + immunomodulator: no recommendation on withdrawing the immunomodulator vs continuing combination | No recommendation (knowledge gap) |
| 12 | Same population: suggests **against** withdrawing the TNF antagonist | Conditional · very low |
| 13 | Failed 5-ASAs [5-aminosalicylates] and escalated to immunomodulators or advanced therapies: suggests **stopping 5-ASAs** | Conditional · low |

- Rec 13: significant but incomplete response may still benefit from ongoing 5-ASA — particularly residual proctitis (add rectal 5-ASA); independent colorectal-cancer-prevention benefit of long-term 5-ASA not robustly demonstrated

### Step therapy

| # | Recommendation | Strength · certainty |
|---|---|---|
| 14 | Suggests **early advanced therapy** with or without an immunomodulator rather than gradual step-up after 5-ASA failure | Conditional · very low |

- Rec 14 comment: patients (particularly less severe disease) who value 5-ASA safety over immunosuppressive efficacy may reasonably choose gradual step therapy

### Key overarching considerations (Table 4, ungraded)

- Confirm active inflammation (symptoms, biomarkers and/or endoscopy) before starting advanced therapy
- Pretreatment: hepatitis B and tuberculosis screening before any biologic or advanced small molecule; thiopurine methyltransferase testing before thiopurines; baseline electrocardiogram before S1P receptor modulators
- Consider influenza, pneumococcal and herpes zoster vaccination (zoster particularly before S1P modulator or JAK inhibitor)
- Monitor: symptomatic response within 3 mo; symptomatic and biochemical remission within 3–6 mo; endoscopic improvement/remission within 6–12 mo

## Clinical Relevance

Efficacy-tiered, early-advanced-therapy strategy for moderate-to-severe [[ulcerative-colitis]]; tiers differ by line of therapy (vedolizumab and the S1P modulators are higher-efficacy first-line but lower-efficacy after prior exposure; tofacitinib and ustekinumab rise to higher efficacy after prior exposure). Thiopurines are suggested against for induction but suggested for maintenance. Pairs with health maintenance per [[ibd-preventive-care]]. Living guideline — rankings evolve with comparative data.
