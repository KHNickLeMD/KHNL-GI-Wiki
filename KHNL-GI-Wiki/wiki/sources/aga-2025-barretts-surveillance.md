## Summary

This AGA guideline addresses surveillance of non-dysplastic [[barretts-esophagus]] (NDBE) and refinements in sampling technique and acid suppression. It endorses interval surveillance for true intestinal-metaplasia BE, a structured (Seattle-protocol) biopsy approach augmented by chromoendoscopy, and daily PPI therapy — while declining to endorse several adjunctive technologies given limited certainty. Surveillance via [[upper-endoscopy]] aims to detect dysplasia/early neoplasia within the [[endoscopic-oncology]] treatment window.

---

## Key Recommendations

- **Rec 1:** In **NDBE**, *suggests* endoscopic surveillance; panel consensus favors high-quality endoscopy roughly **every 3 years** (interval data limited; individualized by segment length).
- **Rec 2:** In **columnar-lined esophagus without intestinal metaplasia or neoplasia**, *suggests against* surveillance endoscopy.
- **Rec 3 (Recommends):** Use a **structured biopsy protocol** (Seattle protocol — 4-quadrant every 1–2 cm + targeted biopsies of visible lesions), with **chromoendoscopy/CE-directed** biopsies as an adjunct.
- **Rec 4:** **No recommendation** for/against **WATS-3D** as an adjunctive sampling technique.
- **Recs 5–6:** No recommendation for/against routine **p53 immunostaining** or **TissueCypher** tissue-based biomarkers.
- **Rec 7:** Suggests **daily PPI** therapy in BE.
- **Rec 8:** Suggests **PPIs over antireflux surgery** for cancer-risk reduction in BE.

---

## Clinical Relevance

Confirms ~3-year surveillance for NDBE with disciplined Seattle-protocol sampling and daily PPI as the chemopreventive backbone; advanced biomarkers/WATS-3D are not yet endorsed for routine use. Tightly linked to [[gerd]] (the upstream driver) and to dysplasia management via [[endoscopic-oncology]]; complements premalignant-mucosa surveillance themes shared with [[gastric-premalignant-conditions]].
