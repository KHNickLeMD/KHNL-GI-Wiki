## Summary

The NCCN Colon Cancer guideline covers workup, staging, and stage-directed treatment of colon adenocarcinoma (rectal cancer is addressed in a companion guideline). Diagnosis is by [[colonoscopy]] with biopsy; staging uses computed tomography (CT) chest/abdomen/pelvis, carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA), and TNM. Universal **mismatch-repair (MMR)/microsatellite instability (MSI)** testing is recommended for all patients (Lynch screening and immunotherapy selection), and **RAS (KRAS/NRAS), BRAF V600E, HER2 (ERBB2), and NTRK** testing is recommended for metastatic disease to direct systemic therapy; **DPYD** testing is addressed before fluoropyrimidine exposure.

Management is stage-based: endoscopic resection for selected early (T1) cancers in a polyp; colectomy with en-bloc regional lymphadenectomy for localized disease; **adjuvant oxaliplatin-based chemotherapy (FOLFOX [folinic acid, fluorouracil, oxaliplatin] or CAPOX [capecitabine, oxaliplatin])** for stage III and high-risk stage II disease, with duration (3 vs 6 months) risk-stratified per the IDEA analyses; and biomarker-directed systemic therapy for metastatic disease, including resection/local therapy for resectable oligometastatic (especially liver-limited) disease. Biologic and targeted therapy is biomarker- and sidedness-dependent: **anti-EGFR therapy (cetuximab/panitumumab) only for RAS-wild-type, BRAF-wild-type, left-sided tumors**; **bevacizumab** broadly; **encorafenib + cetuximab** for BRAF V600E; **pembrolizumab (or nivolumab-based immunotherapy)** for MSI-high (MSI-H)/deficient MMR (dMMR); and HER2-directed therapy for HER2-amplified, RAS-wild-type tumors.

## Key Findings / Claims

- **Universal MMR/MSI testing** for all colon cancers (Lynch syndrome screening and immunotherapy candidacy). See [[lynch-syndrome]].
- **Expanded molecular profiling for metastatic disease:** RAS (KRAS/NRAS), BRAF V600E, HER2 (ERBB2) amplification, NTRK fusions; **DPYD** testing informs fluoropyrimidine dosing/toxicity risk.
- **Localized disease:** endoscopic resection for selected favorable T1 cancers; otherwise colectomy with regional lymphadenectomy (≥12 nodes for adequate staging).
- **Adjuvant therapy:** **FOLFOX or CAPOX** for stage III and high-risk stage II; treatment duration (3 vs 6 months) is risk-stratified (IDEA). MSI-H stage II tumors generally do not benefit from single-agent fluoropyrimidine adjuvant therapy.
- **Metastatic, biomarker- and sidedness-directed:**
  - **Anti-EGFR (cetuximab/panitumumab)** only for **RAS-wild-type, BRAF-wild-type, left-sided** primaries.
  - **Bevacizumab** added to chemotherapy backbones broadly.
  - **BRAF V600E** → **encorafenib + cetuximab** (± a MEK inhibitor in some settings).
  - **MSI-H/dMMR** → **pembrolizumab** first-line (or nivolumab ± ipilimumab).
  - **HER2-amplified, RAS-wild-type** → HER2-directed therapy (e.g., trastuzumab + tucatinib/pertuzumab/lapatinib, or trastuzumab deruxtecan).
  - **KRAS G12C** → a G12C inhibitor (adagrasib/sotorasib) combined with an anti-EGFR antibody in later lines.
- **Resectable oligometastatic disease** (especially liver-limited) is treated with curative-intent resection/ablation integrated with systemic therapy.

## Contradictions / Open Questions

- Algorithm-based guideline (category 2A by default), not a Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation (GRADE) systematic review; complements the ACG/USMSTF screening and surveillance guidelines already cited on the CRC page (which govern prevention/early detection rather than oncologic treatment).
- Optimal adjuvant duration (3 vs 6 months) remains risk-stratified rather than uniform; immunotherapy's role in localized dMMR disease is evolving.
