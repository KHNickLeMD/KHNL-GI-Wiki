## Summary

Comprehensive American College of Gastroenterology reference on adult [[crohns-disease|Crohn's disease (CD)]] of its era, spanning clinical features, natural history, intestinal malignancy risk, diagnosis, disease-activity definitions, medical therapy, fistulizing disease, maintenance, and postoperative prevention. It endorsed fecal calprotectin to help separate [[inflammatory-bowel-disease|inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)]] from [[irritable-bowel-syndrome|irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)]], avoidance of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) and tobacco, and assessment of stress, depression, and anxiety as part of comprehensive care.

Therapeutics: sulfasalazine for mild-to-moderate colonic disease, controlled ileal release budesonide 9 mg daily for mild-to-moderate ileocecal disease, oral mesalamine rejected for active CD. Thiopurines and methotrexate positioned as steroid-sparing and maintenance agents with thiopurine methyltransferase (TPMT) testing before thiopurines. Anti-tumor necrosis factor (anti-TNF) agents (infliximab, adalimumab, certolizumab pegol), the anti-integrins vedolizumab and natalizumab, and the anti-interleukin (IL)-12/23 antibody ustekinumab were positioned for moderate-to-severe and refractory disease, with combination infliximab + immunomodulator favoured over either alone in agent-naive patients, and natalizumab restricted by John Cunningham (JC) virus serology. Fistulizing disease (infliximab ± antibiotics, setons, abscess drainage before anti-TNF) and postoperative recurrence prevention (thiopurines, imidazole antibiotics, early anti-TNF in high-risk patients) are covered in detail.

**How this document grades itself** (important — the numbering and grades below are the document's own, not the wiki's). Evidence quality is graded **high → moderate → low → very low**. Strength is **strong** when desirable effects of an intervention clearly outweigh undesirable effects, and **conditional** when uncertainty remains about the trade-offs. Table 1 numbers the 60 GRADE recommendations 1–60 and prints strength + evidence level for each; in the body text these appear as numbered shaded boxes. Table 2 numbers the 53 summary statements 1–53; summary statements are descriptive, carry **no** evidence-based rating, and appear unnumbered in italics in the body text. One recommendation (#18) is printed as **"weak recommendation"** rather than "conditional" — reproduced here as published.
