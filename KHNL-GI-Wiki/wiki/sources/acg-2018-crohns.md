## Summary
This 2018 ACG guideline was the comprehensive adult [[crohns-disease|Crohn's disease]] reference of its era, spanning diagnosis, disease classification, and the full medical and surgical armamentarium. It endorsed fecal calprotectin to distinguish [[inflammatory-bowel-disease|IBD]] from IBS, emphasized smoking cessation and avoidance of NSAIDs, and addressed psychological comorbidity as part of comprehensive care.

On therapeutics it positioned budesonide and (for colonic disease) sulfasalazine for mild-moderate disease, while finding oral mesalamine largely ineffective for Crohn's. Thiopurines and methotrexate were endorsed as steroid-sparing/maintenance agents, with TPMT testing before thiopurines. Anti-TNF agents (infliximab, adalimumab, certolizumab), the anti-integrin vedolizumab, and ustekinumab were positioned for moderate-severe and refractory disease, with combination anti-TNF + immunomodulator favored over monotherapy and JC-virus-guided use of natalizumab.

Fistulizing disease (infliximab ± antibiotics, setons, abscess drainage before anti-TNF) and post-operative recurrence prevention (thiopurines, imidazole antibiotics, early anti-TNF in high-risk patients) are covered in detail. Drug positioning here predates newer agents (e.g. risankizumab, upadacitinib) and is superseded by the [[acg-2025-crohns|ACG Clinical Guideline: Management of Crohn's Disease (2025)]] and [[aga-2025-crohns-pharm|AGA Pharmacologic Management of Moderate–Severe Crohn's Disease (2025)]].
