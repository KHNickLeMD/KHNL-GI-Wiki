# AGA Clinical Practice Guideline: GI Evaluation of Iron-Deficiency Anemia (2020)

## Bibliographic Info

- **Year:** 2020
- **Journal:** Gastroenterology (AGA Clinical Practice Guideline)
- **Type:** GRADE-based guideline

---

## Summary

This guideline standardizes the GI workup of **iron-deficiency anemia (IDA)** — defining iron deficiency, when to scope, and adjunctive testing.

---

## Key Recommendations

- **Diagnose iron deficiency** using a **ferritin cutoff of 45 ng/mL** (over the older 15 ng/mL) — improves sensitivity for true deficiency.
- In asymptomatic patients with IDA, the AGA **recommends bidirectional endoscopy** (EGD **and** colonoscopy) to evaluate for a GI source.
- **Bidirectional endoscopy over iron studies/capsule** as the initial structural evaluation; pursue **capsule endoscopy** for the small bowel if bidirectional endoscopy is negative and suspicion persists ([[small-bowel-bleeding]]).
- During EGD, obtain **duodenal biopsies for celiac disease**; perform **noninvasive [[helicobacter-pylori-infection|H. pylori]] testing and treatment** when bidirectional endoscopy is unrevealing.

---

## Clinical Relevance

Sets ferritin <45 ng/mL as the practical iron-deficiency threshold and mandates bidirectional endoscopy (with celiac biopsies and H. pylori test-and-treat) for IDA workup, escalating to capsule for suspected small-bowel sources. Feeds the [[small-bowel-bleeding]] and [[celiac-disease]] pathways.
