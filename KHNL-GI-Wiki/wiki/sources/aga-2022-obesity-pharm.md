## Summary

This guideline recommends **adding pharmacotherapy to lifestyle intervention** for adults with obesity and ranks available agents by efficacy and certainty. It strongly endorses combining drugs with lifestyle change.

---

## Key Recommendations

- **Strong:** add **pharmacotherapy to lifestyle intervention** (vs lifestyle alone) for adults with obesity (BMI ≥30, or ≥27 with weight-related comorbidity).
- **Preferred agents (moderate certainty, by efficacy):** **[[semaglutide]] 2.4 mg** (highest efficacy), **liraglutide 3.0 mg**, **phentermine-topiramate ER**, **naltrexone-bupropion ER**.
- **Phentermine** and **diethylpropion** suggested (short-term/selected).
- **Against orlistat** as a preferred option (GI side effects, modest efficacy).
- Match agent to comorbidities, contraindications, cost/access, and patient values.
