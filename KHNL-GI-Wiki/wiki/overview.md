### *Helicobacter pylori* Infection
*H. pylori* is one of the most prevalent bacterial infections worldwide, affecting ~50% of the global population. It is causally linked to peptic ulcer disease, gastric adenocarcinoma, and MALT lymphoma. Eradication is recommended in all infected patients. First-line regimen choice is guided by local clarithromycin resistance rates: bismuth quadruple therapy (preferred when resistance >15%) vs. concomitant or clarithromycin-based triple therapy (where resistance is low). Resistance-guided or susceptibility-testing approaches are emerging.
