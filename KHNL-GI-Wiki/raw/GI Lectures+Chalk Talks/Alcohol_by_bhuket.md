## Summary

### Teaching: Alcohol Consumption Quantification
- **Standard drink equivalents**: 12 oz beer (approximately 6% ABV) = 5 oz wine = 1.5 oz shot
- **Volume conversions**: 1.5 oz shot = approximately 50 cc
- **Practical calculations for patient assessment**:
  - 750 ml bottle = 15 shots (750 / 50)
  - Handle (1.75 liters) = 35 shots
  - Tall boy (24 oz beer) = equivalent to 2 standard beers
- **Clinical importance**: Quantifying alcohol intake helps predict sedation requirements and assess what clinicians are up against when treating patients
- **Assessment tip**: Consider the "double it" rule when patients self-report consumption
- **Watch for underreporting**: Patients may say "one glass" but use very large containers
