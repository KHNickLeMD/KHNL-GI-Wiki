## Right-Sided Colon Cancer Detection
- Approximately 40% of late-onset colon cancer (over age 50) occurs on the right side
- Right-sided colon lesions are challenging: area often not well prepped, lesions tend to be flat (serrated polyps)
- Techniques: double-look method and retroflexion in the cecum

## Screening Modalities
- FIT: Good sensitivity for CRC, very good specificity
- Cologuard: Slightly better than FIT, performed every three years
- Colonoscopy: 100% detection rate, only preventative test that decreases cancer incidence by removing polyps
- Perforation risk: approximately 1 in 2,500 to 4,000
- CT Colonography: Cannot detect flat lesions or lesions <5mm well
- SHIELD Test: Blood-based cell-free DNA test, only 13% sensitive for pre-cancerous lesions
- NextGen Cologuard: 40% detection rate for advanced pre-cancerous lesions

## Screening and Surveillance Intervals
- Average Risk: Start at age 45, repeat every 10 years if normal
- Piecemeal Resection: 6-month follow-up required
- 10-year interval based on approximately 10 years for new polyps to develop into cancer

## Special Populations
- Personal History of Colon Cancer: return at 1 year, then 3 years later, then every 5 years for life
- Lynch Syndrome: Colonoscopy every 1-2 years
- First-degree relative: start at age 40 or 10 years younger than youngest case, every 5 years
- IBD Patients: Annual colonoscopy if PSC, family history CRC, pseudopolyps, dysplasia, or poorly controlled disease

## Polyp Classification and Detection
- Paris Classification: pedunculated, semi-pedunculated, flat, or excavated
- Lateral Spreading Tumors: >10mm diameter, cross multiple folds, use NBI to demarcate margins
- NICE Classification: distinguishes hyperplastic from adenomas and cancerous lesions

## Polypectomy Techniques
- <9mm: Cold snare
- 10-19mm: Cold or hot snare with or without lifting
- >=20mm: Refer to advanced endoscopist
- Pedunculated Polyps >10mm: use hot snare, consider endo-loop or clips if stalk >5mm
- Tattoo distal to lesion, do not inject too close

## Quality Metrics
- Cecal intubation rate: >=95%
- ADR: >35% (updated from >=25%)
- ADR for FIT-positive: >50%
- Sessile serrated lesion detection rate: >6%
- Withdrawal time: >=6 minutes

## Early-Onset Colorectal Cancer
- Colon cancer deaths in under-50 age group increasing while other cancer deaths decrease
