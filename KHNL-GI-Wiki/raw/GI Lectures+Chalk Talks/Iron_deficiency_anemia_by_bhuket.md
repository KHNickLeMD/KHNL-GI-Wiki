## Summary

### Structured Approach to IDA
- Three physiologic causes:
  - **Insufficient substrate (dietary iron)**: Patients at risk include those with poor oral intake (alcoholics, elderly with "tea and toast" diet, vegans)
  - **Malabsorption**: Iron absorbed in duodenum and jejunum; celiac disease is the prototype duodenal/jejunal disease
  - **Blood loss**: If present, would be occult loss (slower process)

### Clinical Assessment
- Malabsorption considered less likely based on body habitus
- About 25% of IDA cases do not have identifiable cause despite thorough workup
- Patient cooperation poses challenge for procedures like EGD

### Management Plan
- Send celiac serologies
- Start iron supplementation - IV iron preferred given low hemoglobin
- Workup including EGD, colonoscopy, or capsule endoscopy could be pursued as outpatient

### PPI Discussion
- Primary team started PPI but patient has no hard indication for it
- Classic PPI indications include peptic ulcer disease and GERD
- Recommendation is to not use PPI without clear indication
