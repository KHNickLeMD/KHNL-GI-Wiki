Calcineurin inhibitors (CNIs) — primarily [[tacrolimus]] and cyclosporine — are the backbone of maintenance immunosuppression in solid organ transplantation. CNI nephrotoxicity is a major cause of chronic kidney disease after liver transplantation; see [[liver-transplantation]] for trough targets and minimization strategies.
