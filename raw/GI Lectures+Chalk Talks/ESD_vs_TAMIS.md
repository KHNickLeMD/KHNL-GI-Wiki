# ESD vs TAMIS
**Type:** Lecture/Workshop
**Author:** Kevin Tang
**Date:** 2026-02-11

## Study Overview
- RCT comparing ESD vs TAMIS for early rectal neoplasms (Gastroenterology)
- Multi-center trial in Spain, 73 patients

## Key Results
- 12-month local recurrence: ESD 0, TAMIS 2
- ESD non-inferior to TAMIS
- ESD: higher complete/curative resection rate
- Hospital stay: ESD 1 day vs TAMIS 2-3 days
- Cost: TAMIS approximately double ESD
- Patient preference strongly favored ESD

## Complications
- ESD: 2 perforations (intraprocedural)
- TAMIS: higher delayed complications requiring readmission

## Conclusions
- ESD non-inferior to TAMIS for 12-month recurrence
- ESD: fewer technical constraints, higher success, less hospitalization, lower cost, greater patient acceptance
