## Key Diagnostic Points
- FibroScan cutoffs: <8 kPa (low risk), >12.5 kPa (high risk F3-F4), gray zone 8-12.5 kPa
- MRE cutoffs: 3.14 for F2/F3, 5.0 for cirrhosis; slightly better than FibroScan
- ELF cutoff of 11.3 has reasonable specificity for cirrhosis

## Weight Loss and Fibrosis Improvement
- 5-7% weight loss improves steatosis, but 10% required for fibrosis improvement
- Lifestyle alone: 2-5%; Semaglutide: ~15%; Tirzepatide: ~20%; Bariatric surgery: 20-30%
- BRAVES trial: bariatric surgery achieved 50% MASH resolution vs 15% lifestyle alone
- Optimal threshold 10-20%; above 20% no additional histological benefit (1-year study)

## FDA-Approved MASH Treatments
- **Semaglutide**: ESSENCE trial 36% fibrosis regression vs 22% placebo; approved F2/F3
- **Tirzepatide**: SYNERGY phase 2 slightly higher delta; not yet FDA-approved for MASH
- **Resmetirom**: First FDA-approved MASH-specific treatment; 9-10% delta; 80mg if <100kg, 100mg if >100kg; CANNOT use in cirrhosis or active alcohol use

## Treatment Strategy
- Start with one treatment, wait 6+ months before adding second agent
- Consider patient comorbidities when choosing first-line
- Pioglitazone has efficacy but causes weight gain

## New Nomenclature
- MASLD, MetALD, and ALD represent a continuum
- Multiple societies now recommend screening in high-risk populations
