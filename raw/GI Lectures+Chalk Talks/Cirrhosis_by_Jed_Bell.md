## Summary

### Cirrhosis Classification
- Always good to specify if it is COMPENSATED or DECOMPENSATED

### Compensated Cirrhosis
- No history of:
  1. Ascites
  2. Hepatic encephalopathy (HE)
  3. Variceal bleeding
- Good to further clarify if they have:
  - Varices or no varices
  - Portal hypertension or not

### Decompensated Cirrhosis
- Simply if they have a history of ascites, HE, or variceal bleeds
- Sometimes, with treatment (e.g. treating HCV), patients can move from decompensated to compensated if their symptoms significantly improve AND they are without the above complications for many years
