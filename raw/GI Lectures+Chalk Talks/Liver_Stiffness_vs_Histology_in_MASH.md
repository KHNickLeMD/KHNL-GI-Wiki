# Liver Stiffness vs Histology in MASH
**Type:** Lecture/Workshop
**Date:** 2026-03-04
**Source:** https://www.notion.so/319b476aba7980aaa0caccabf53cc1a0

---

## Study Overview
- Compared prognostic performance of liver stiffness measurement (LSM) by VCTE vs histologic fibrosis staging for predicting liver-related events in MASH
- Post-hoc analysis of VCT prognosis study, 16 centers across US, Europe, and Asia
- 3,532 patients with both VCTE and liver biopsy (2004-2023)

## Methods
- LSM risk stratification: <10 kPa (low), 10-19.9 kPa (intermediate), >=20 kPa (high) based on Baveno criteria
- Histologic staging: NASH-CRN system F0-F2, F3, F4
- Primary outcome: composite of decompensation, transplant, liver-related death

## Results
- Median follow-up 56 months; 126 patients (3.6%) developed liver-related events
- 5-year cumulative incidence: ~14% for LSM >=20 kPa vs much lower for <10 kPa
- 5-year by histology: 16% for F4 cirrhosis
- LSM stratification predictive even within same histologic stage

## Key Finding: Discordant Cases
- F0-F2 with low LSM: only 0.1% incidence of liver-related events
- F4 with LSM >=20 kPa: nearly 20% incidence
- F3 with intermediate LSM (10-19.9): 2.9% five-year incidence
- F3 with high LSM (>=20): 7.8% five-year incidence
- LSM added prognostic value beyond histology alone

## Model Performance
- 5-year Brier scores nearly identical: 1.389% for LSM vs 1.391% for histology
- Performance curves nearly overlapping

## Conclusions
- LSM by VCTE comparable to histologic fibrosis staging for predicting liver-related events
- Supports LSM as non-invasive surrogate endpoint in clinical trials
- Baveno criteria cutoffs (10 and 20 kPa) validated
- When LSM and histology disagree, LSM should not be disregarded
- F3 and F4 may exist on a continuum; some F3 patients may have significant portal hypertension
