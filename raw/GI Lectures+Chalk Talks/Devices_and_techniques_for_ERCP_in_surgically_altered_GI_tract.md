## Summary

This page contains the full text of an ASGE Technology Committee review on devices and techniques for ERCP in the surgically altered GI tract. The content is extensive and includes abbreviations, procedural descriptions, and evidence-based methodology for performing ERCP in patients with surgically altered anatomy including Billroth-II, Roux-en-Y gastric bypass, and other reconstructions. The review covers double-balloon enteroscopy (DBE), single-balloon enteroscopy (SBE), percutaneous transhepatic cholangiography (PTC), and self-expandable metal stents (SEMS) among other techniques.
