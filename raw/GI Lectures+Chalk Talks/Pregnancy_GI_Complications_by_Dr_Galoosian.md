## Summary

### Hyperemesis Gravidarum
- Typically occurs in the first trimester and usually improves as pregnancy progresses
- Generally does not require specific intervention

### Intrahepatic Cholestasis of Pregnancy (ICP) Management
- UDCA is the standard treatment for ICP
- Bile acid level of 100 is the threshold for early delivery at 37 weeks
- For bile acids at 50, titrate UDCA to goal of 10; early delivery is not necessarily indicated
- Bile acids at 50 are associated with higher risk of preterm labor
- Well-controlled bile acids typically do not require early delivery
- ICP does not cause acute fatty liver of pregnancy
