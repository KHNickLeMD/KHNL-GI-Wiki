## EQ-5D Quality of Life Assessment
- Measures health-related quality of life across five domains: mobility, self-care, visual activity, pain and discomfort, and anxiety/depression
- Patient responses mapped to utility value ranging from 1 to 100

## Quality-Adjusted Life Years (QALYs) in Decision Making
- QALYs calculated by multiplying probability of survival by quality of life metric
- Subjective ratings of quality of life in different health states impact comparison between treatment options

## Decision Tree Example: Immediate Surgery vs. Medical Management
- Immediate surgery pathway: 99.5% survival probability, resulting in 31.84 QALYs
- Continued medical management pathway: higher surgical mortality risk but overall higher quality of life, resulting in 34.06 QALYs
- Patients accept a small gamble of higher surgical mortality in exchange for relatively high chance of better quality of life

## Incorporating Patient Preferences
- To maximize survival: Immediate colectomy is the best choice
- To maximize quality of life: Wait-and-see pathway is optimal
- Individual patient preferences can significantly affect expected utility calculations
- Example: If patient strongly anticipates hating life with an ileostomy/ostomy bag, this changes the utility estimate
