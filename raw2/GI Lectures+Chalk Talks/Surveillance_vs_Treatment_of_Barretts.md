# Surveillance vs Treatment of Barrett's
**Type:** Lecture/Workshop
**Date:** 2026-02-11
**Source:** https://www.notion.so/304b476aba79800f9085d1d1826e9883

---

## Study Overview (BEST3)
- Multi-center RCT from UK: surveillance vs symptom-triggered endoscopy in Barrett's
- 109 centers, enrolled 2009-2011, median 12.8-year follow-up
- Mean age 63, 71% male, 56% long-segment Barrett's, 94% on PPIs

## Key Findings
- No significant difference in overall survival (HR 0.95)
- No difference in cancer-specific survival or stage at diagnosis
- EAC incidence 2.1%
- Post-hoc: increased mortality in surveillance arm for <65, reduced for >65

## Study Limitations
- Open-label design led to contamination: 59% of control had endoscopy
- Lower than expected cancer progression (potentially underpowered)
- Did not require intestinal metaplasia for Barrett's diagnosis
- No standardization of surveillance technique

## Current Guidelines
- ACG and 2025 AGA still recommend surveillance every 3-5 years
- Tissue Cipher: nine-panel biomarker tool for risk stratification in non-dysplastic Barrett's
- AspECT trial showed lower all-cause mortality with high-dose PPI
