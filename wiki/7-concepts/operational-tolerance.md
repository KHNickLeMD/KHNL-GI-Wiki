Operational tolerance refers to the state in which a transplant recipient maintains stable allograft function with normal histology while off all immunosuppression. In liver transplantation, spontaneous tolerance occurs in a minority of patients (estimated 10–20% in select series), more commonly in those who are long-term post-transplant, older at transplant, and male. Immunosuppression withdrawal outside of clinical trials is not currently recommended per [[aasld-2012-liver-transplant-long-term]]. See [[liver-transplantation]] for clinical context.
